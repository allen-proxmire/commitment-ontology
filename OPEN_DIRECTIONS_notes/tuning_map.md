# The tuning, mapped (14 Nov 2026; reading)

**What is tuned.** On a lattice that picks a frame, loops shift each species' limiting speed at dimension four by about its coupling over π (α/π ≈ 2 × 10⁻³ for charged leptons in QED; Collins et al. 2004, by analogy). CO holds the species' speeds equal by counter-terms set by hand (chosen, 16 Nov: the frame kept, since it carries the photon and neutrino tests). One counter-term per species' speed relative to light; the precision each needs is set by that species' best bound:

| speed held equal to light's | loop shift (order) | bound | precision of the counter-term |
|---|---|---|---|
| electron | α/π ≈ 2 × 10⁻³ | ≲ 5 × 10⁻²⁰ (Crab) | about 1 part in 10¹⁷ |
| proton (through its quarks and gluons) | α_s/π at the hop scale, and α/π | ≲ 4 × 10⁻²³ if the highest-energy cosmic rays are protons (Coleman and Glashow 1999); much weaker for heavy nuclei | up to about 1 part in 10²⁰ |
| gravity | loops of all species | ≲ 10⁻¹⁵ (GW170817) | about 1 part in 10¹² |
| ~~light's terms by direction (tuning 4)~~ | on the earlier palindromic walk only; removed by the flow step's cube symmetry | birefringence ≲ 10⁻³² | — |

**What the tuning does not touch** (earlier walk; superseded by 'The flow step' below). The counter-terms are dimension-four speeds. The walk's tree-level speed excess for a massive pattern, s = ½ sin w·mcℓ/ħ, grows with mass and is not a dimension-four speed; nor is the massless walk's quadratic dispersion (at w = 5π/16: −0.086 (kℓ)² along an axis, −0.068 and −0.062 along face and body diagonals). The first is swallowed if each species' physical speed is tuned to light's; the second is not a speed and stays (prediction_map.md).

**Ways the counter-terms might be fixed by CO instead of by hand.**
- *Running to equal speeds at long wavelength* (Chadha and Nielsen 1983): in gauge theories, unequal speeds flow together toward low energies, but only logarithmically. From the hop scale to laboratory energies that leaves splits of order α/π divided by a logarithm, far above the bounds. Excluded as the whole fix (as found in the literature on emergent Lorentz invariance).
- *Self-consistency of the closed loop.* Under P7f each species' local light speed is set by the field's lapse and stretch through N/ψ² (P7f restated; on the earlier walk, its Wilson flip). If the rule fixed each species' dressed speed (not its bare one) to the field's light cone, the counter-terms would follow from P7f rather than being chosen. As stated this restates the tuning as a rule (one condition per species), unless the closed loop, matter sourcing the field and the field acting back, drives unequal speeds together on its own. In GR, species with different light cones (a bimetric theory) do not relax to one; the expectation is no. Under P7f the field rescales every species' local speed by the same factor, so the ratio of two species' speeds does not depend on the lapse or stretch and no closed-loop dynamics can pull them together. Excluded as a mechanism under P7f as stated; the remaining form restates the tuning as a rule.
- *The beginning sets them once.* The counter-terms as initial conditions of the first commitments: a story, not fewer inputs.
- *A network with non-commuting coordinates* (κ-Poincaré type): the one door the frame note leaves open, with the soccer-ball cost.

**Also tuned, and missing from the first count.** Wilson-type walks break chiral symmetry, so each fermion mass gets an additive shift of order (α/π)ħ/(ℓc) needing its own counter-term (as the critical hopping parameter in lattice QCD): a second tuning per fermion.

**Reading.** The tuning is one number per species' speed (plus one per fermion mass), at precisions from 10¹² (gravity) to 10¹⁷–10²⁰ (electrons, protons). No CO mechanism found fixes them. The closed loop's self-consistency cannot fix them under P7f.

**Tuning 2, candidate removal (14 Nov 2026).** A domain-wall walk (MATTER_runs/B2_domain_wall; MATTER_record): the mass's additive shift falls as e^(−αL) in the inner length, α ≈ 0.39 per place, under static single-particle disturbances uniform and periodic along x. Leftover mass below mₑ (at ℓ = ℓ_P) for L ≳ 130. Not removed, and enlarged: the speed counter-terms are needed per handedness (the walls' speeds are held equal only by a CP-type antiunitary reflection that potential-type corrections break). Open: interactions and gauge fields (lattice QCD: the leftover mass falls more slowly in rough backgrounds), the 3+1D form with interactions (free fermions one-handed at each wall, run B4), chiral gauge couplings on domain walls.

**Tuning 3: light as a collective mode (reading, desk).** ED-I-06's architecture, fields as stable participation structures, read in CO: the link field's lattice form is not a separate rule but is induced by matter's transit on the links. Integrating out the walk generates the which-way phase's cost (induced gauge theory: Zeldovich 1967, Sakharov 1967), and the induced cost carries the walk's lattice structure, so light's short-wavelength bending c₂ becomes an output: one number per direction, computable from the walk's vacuum polarisation at order k⁴. The check: is the induced c₂ ≳ −4×10⁻⁴ in every direction and polarisation? Pass removes tuning 3 for light; fail excludes the reading. Costs and pokes:
- Induced gauge theory also fixes the coupling: 1/α ≈ (2/3π) Σ N_c q² ln(Λ/m), summed over charged species. With Λ at the Planck scale and the known charges this gives 1/α of order 10² (a rough sum gives about 80, against 137 at low energy). Order-of-magnitude only, and a standard observation, not a CO result; but if the link field is induced, 1/137 stops being a free input and becomes a check.
- The induced cost has no reason to be the improved (straight) form; the reading predicts c₂, it does not choose it.
- Gluons follow if colour links are induced the same way; gravity is a separate question (the closed loop's field already acts back through P7f).
- First step: compute the induced photon dispersion from CO's walk in 1+1 or 2+1 dimensions at one loop.

**Tuning 4: light's terms by direction (15 Nov 2026; earlier walk, excluded by the flow step below).** Needed across the Wilson window (I4 at w = 5π/16; I5 at w = 0.95 and 1.10, offsets −0.067/−0.064 and −0.031/+0.047/−0.037): on CO's palindromic walk the vacuum's lattice-scale response to light depends on the axis by constant offsets, per unit-charge species ε_z − ε_x = −0.0757 − 0.071θ and Z_B for B along y −0.0629 − 0.057θ, while the leading logs are isotropic. Against light's own coefficients that is 0.8% (ε) and 0.5% (Z_B) per species, e² = 4πα, and about eight times more summed over the known charges. The same fraction holds whether light is posited (loops add to its bare terms) or induced (the logs make up 1/e²); for induced light a counter-term means light is no longer purely induced. Light's ε and 1/μ, diagonal as measured, need four counter-terms beyond the coupling and the common speed, set to about 1 part in 10³⁰ by birefringence bounds. Cause: if the walk had the cube's symmetry, every two-index response would be isotropic; permuting the axes is the same as permuting the nesting order, so the palindrome lacks that symmetry, while its light species' dispersion stays cubic to the precision checked. Routes tried: cycling the order per step (no gap: extra massless species, excluded); the body-centred-cubic automaton (doubled, symmetric only under half-turns about the axes, first-order direction-dependent dispersion, excluded). Routes tried, continued: a sublattice-dependent order (not unitary on CO's step; C2). Open: a single-step walk that is cubic, gapped and has one light species (silence; candidates to check: a partitioned step built from commuting local blocks, the doubler-free walks of Gupta and Short, Phys. Rev. A 114, 012208 (2026), in 3D). Expected, not computed: tuning 1's speed counter-terms also by direction; the off-diagonal entries of 1/μ (those of ε are below 10⁻⁹, I5).

**The flow step (15 Nov 2026).** Matter's step is now one tick of the cube-symmetric Wilson–Dirac flow (candidate; r = ½ posited). Tuning 4 does not arise (two-index responses isotropic by symmetry, C3i): excluded by the step. Tuning 1 is one counter-term per species and handedness, the same in every direction; induced light on the step runs about 0.7% below light's speed at the physical mass (C3j). The tree-level forms change: s = ½ r·mcℓ/ħ and c₂(n̂) = 3(r²/8 − Σn_j⁴/6), −0.406, −0.156, −0.073 (axis, face, body) at r = ½, so electrons never outrun straight light. Tuning 2's candidate removal holds on the step (B3b).
