# The tuning, mapped (14 Nov 2026; reading)

**What is tuned.** On a lattice that picks a frame, loops shift each species' limiting speed at dimension four by about its coupling over π (α/π ≈ 2 × 10⁻³ for charged leptons in QED; Collins et al. 2004, by analogy). CO holds the species' speeds equal by counter-terms set by hand (the author's ruling, for now). One counter-term per species' speed relative to light; the precision each needs is set by that species' best bound:

| speed held equal to light's | loop shift (order) | bound | precision of the counter-term |
|---|---|---|---|
| electron | α/π ≈ 2 × 10⁻³ | ≲ 5 × 10⁻²⁰ (Crab) | about 1 part in 10¹⁷ |
| proton (through its quarks and gluons) | α_s/π at the hop scale, and α/π | ≲ 4 × 10⁻²³ if the highest-energy cosmic rays are protons (Coleman and Glashow 1999); much weaker for heavy nuclei | up to about 1 part in 10²⁰ |
| gravity | loops of all species | ≲ 10⁻¹⁵ (GW170817) | about 1 part in 10¹² |

**What the tuning does not touch.** The counter-terms are dimension-four speeds. The walk's tree-level speed excess for a massive pattern, s = ½ sin w·mcℓ/ħ, grows with mass and is not a dimension-four speed; nor is the massless walk's quadratic dispersion (at w = 5π/16: −0.086 (kℓ)² along an axis, −0.068 and −0.062 along face and body diagonals). The first is swallowed if each species' physical speed is tuned to light's; the second is not a speed and stays (prediction_map.md).

**Ways the counter-terms might be fixed by CO instead of by hand.**
- *Running to equal speeds at long wavelength* (Chadha and Nielsen 1983): in gauge theories, unequal speeds flow together toward low energies, but only logarithmically. From the hop scale to laboratory energies that leaves splits of order α/π divided by a logarithm, far above the bounds. Excluded as the whole fix (as found in the literature on emergent Lorentz invariance).
- *Self-consistency of the closed loop.* Under P7f each species' local light speed is set by the field's lapse and stretch through its Wilson flip. If the rule fixed each species' dressed speed (not its bare one) to the field's light cone, the counter-terms would follow from P7f rather than being chosen. As stated this restates the tuning as a rule (one condition per species), unless the closed loop, matter sourcing the field and the field acting back, drives unequal speeds together on its own. In GR, species with different light cones (a bimetric theory) do not relax to one; the expectation is no. Under P7f the field rescales every species' local speed by the same factor, so the ratio of two species' speeds does not depend on the lapse or stretch and no closed-loop dynamics can pull them together. Excluded as a mechanism under P7f as stated; the remaining form restates the tuning as a rule.
- *The beginning sets them once.* The counter-terms as initial conditions of the first commitments: a story, not fewer inputs.
- *A network with non-commuting coordinates* (κ-Poincaré type): the one door the frame note leaves open, with the soccer-ball cost.

**Also tuned, and missing from the first count.** Wilson-type walks break chiral symmetry, so each fermion mass gets an additive shift of order (α/π)ħ/(ℓc) needing its own counter-term (as the critical hopping parameter in lattice QCD): a second tuning per fermion.

**Reading.** The tuning is one number per species' speed (plus one per fermion mass), at precisions from 10¹² (gravity) to 10¹⁷–10²⁰ (electrons, protons). No CO mechanism found fixes them. The closed loop's self-consistency cannot fix them under P7f.
