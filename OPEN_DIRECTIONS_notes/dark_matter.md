# Dark matter: a scratchpad

Shapes and doodles, graded as such. Nothing here is a ruling.

**The shape: a pattern that renews but has no channel.** CO separates two things standard physics bundles together:
- *What has mass gravitates through renewal.* Each repeat keeps δħ (C3), and that draw is the sink; charge plays no part.
- *What can be engaged* is anything with a channel: a final state through which another commitment can take a quantum from it (C8, candidate). Charge is a channel for light; colour is a channel for gluons.

A pattern that renews but carries no channel would gravitate fully and be engaged by nothing. In the author's earlier words: *a pattern that commits but never measures.* Standard physics' dark matter (heavy, cold, felt only through gravity) is then permitted in CO with nothing added, though not predicted.

**What it has to do.**
- *Collisionless:* no channel, no scattering. Halos stay extended; the dark mass in the Bullet Cluster (Clowe et al. 2006) passes through with the galaxies, while the gas, which has channels, is slowed.
- *Cold:* a channel-less pattern never comes into heat balance with anything, so it keeps whatever speed the beginning gives it; a heavy pattern made slow stays slow. How slow the beginning makes it is not ruled.
- *Never detected directly:* every underground search looks for a channel, so CO expects them to keep finding nothing. (Gravity-only dark matter models expect the same, so this does not single CO out.)
- *Made only at the start:* with no channel, nothing later can make it. It would come out of the beginning, when every place was over capacity and committed at once. C7 says matter forms immediately but not which patterns, or how many; that the beginning makes channel-less ones is assumed. Made before the spreading, it faces the same question as ordinary matter made there: how it survives the spreading.

**Two that do not work.**
- *The record as dark matter:* no. The record is not extra mass; the retained share is how ordinary matter gravitates. Counting it again would double count, and it sits where matter has been, not in halos.
- *Neutrinos:* no. They are light and fast, and would wash out small structure; they are excluded as the bulk of dark matter. Whatever the dark pattern is, it differs from them.

**How much: the "about five".** Dark matter is about 5.4 times ordinary matter by mass (Planck 2018: Ω_c h² ≈ 0.120 against Ω_b h² ≈ 0.0224). CO cannot derive that ratio without masses, which are inputs. One CO-shaped relation: if the beginning made dark patterns and ordinary matter in equal numbers, by the same "no room for a second pattern" that the author proposes for the matter–antimatter asymmetry, then the dark pattern's mass would be about 5.4 proton masses, around 5 GeV. That matches, in its simplest form, the asymmetric-dark-matter relation of the standard literature (Kaplan, Luty and Zurek 2009, who give 5–15 GeV). The standard version uses a channel to destroy the dark antiparticles; a channel-less pair cannot annihilate, so this needs the beginning to make no dark antipatterns.

**No anti-dark, from the same hunch.** Read tightly, the hunch says a saturated place at the start makes one pattern, not a pair, because there is no room for the second. That rule does not ask which kind of pattern it makes, so it leaves ordinary matter without primordial antimatter and dark patterns without antipatterns at once: the condition above follows from the hunch rather than being added. It carries one bill. In the standard story, matter and antimatter were made almost equally and annihilated, leaving about one proton per 1.6 × 10⁹ photons of the cosmic background light (η ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰); a beginning that never made antimatter still owes a source for that light and that ratio. Equal numbers of dark and ordinary patterns is not given; it would need a rule for how the beginning picks a kind. Since a channel-less pattern cannot annihilate, whatever the beginning made of it is all there is. Grade: shape. It would be a number with a mass as input, not a continuous number from the primitives; and a channel-less 5 GeV pattern would show only through gravity, where 5 GeV is hard to tell from heavier.

**The photon ratio, read.** Three things follow when the hunch is held against the standard history.
- *What survives is a net excess, not "no antimatter".* Once the early universe is hot enough (in the quark era and down to a few tens of MeV for nucleons), light makes matter–antimatter pairs and pairs make light, in balance, whatever the beginning left. Only the net count of matter over antimatter is kept through that era. So the hunch's lasting content is the excess: one matter pattern per saturated place, with any antimatter later made in pairs from light. Channel-less dark patterns are not remade this way, since light cannot reach them.
- *The ratio becomes a question about the beginning.* With the excess fixed at the start, η = n_B/n_γ ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰ is the number of matter patterns per quantum of light today, fixed since electron–positron annihilation a few seconds in: about 1.6 × 10⁹ quanta of light per matter pattern. The hunch owes a reason the beginning's possibility went almost entirely to light. It gives none yet; η is a continuous number, like 1/137, and stays a silence.
- *One sign everywhere.* If each saturated place chose matter or antimatter on its own, the universe would be patches of each, and their borders would glow in gamma rays; such patches are excluded out to about a gigaparsec, near the size of the visible universe (Cohen, De Rújula and Glashow 1998). Standard physics answers with a slight bias between matter and antimatter (Sakharov's conditions: baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, departure from thermal equilibrium). The author's beginning offers another answer: if it was one event, a single timeless ball turning definite at many places at once, the sign could be chosen once for all places. That would give one sign everywhere without a bias, but says nothing about η.

**The story of threes.** Ordinary matter: patterns that renew with channels, in three families. Dark matter: patterns that renew without. Whether the dark kind also comes in three is open.

**Doodles, flagged as doodles.**
- *MOND's scale.* MOND's acceleration a₀ ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² is close to cH₀/2π. In CO the floor of possibility falls at about the expansion rate; if a galaxy's outskirts felt that falling floor, the coincidence would have a CO-shaped reason. Not worked.
- *The network's edge.* An older picture of the author's: at the network's edge (the visible horizon) something overcomes weak gravity, and in the Bullet Cluster the collision jammed the network into a knot that carries the extra pull. The horizon half has a well-known relative: Verlinde's emergent gravity (SciPost Phys. 2, 016 (2017)) derives an apparent dark matter from the de Sitter horizon, with a₀ tied to cH₀. It fits galaxy weak lensing in one test (Brouwer et al. 2017) and has trouble with clusters and with the timing of structure. The knot half would have to travel with the galaxies and not the gas, as the Bullet Cluster's lensing mass does, and would also have to supply the missing mass of clusters that have not merged in billions of years, which show it as well (lensing, X-ray gas held up by its own pressure, galaxy speeds). A knot made only in mergers cannot. CO's which-way field is a U(1) phase, which allows vortex lines but no point defects; CO has no field of directions on a sphere.
- *Superfluid dark matter.* Berezhiani and Khoury (2015) propose dark matter that condenses in galaxies and acts like MOND there, while acting as ordinary dark matter in clusters and in the early universe. Channel-less patterns could still bind among themselves, but the model's MOND-like force needs a phonon coupling to ordinary matter, which is a channel, and its condensate needs heat balance among dark patterns; how it fits the shape above is open.

**Grade.** Shape: dark matter as a renewing pattern with no channel, made only at the start. The equal-numbers relation (dark pattern ≈ 5 GeV) is a shape resting on the author's matter–antimatter hunch. No anti-dark from the matter–antimatter hunch: shape. The hunch read as a net excess: shape; one sign everywhere from a single beginning: shape; the baryon-to-photon ratio η: silence. The MOND, network-edge and superfluid pictures are ungraded sketches.
