# The beginning: what it does and what it still owes

Shapes and readings, graded as such. Numbers from RECORD_OF_METHOD/MATTER_runs/C12_first_ball/ (the visible universe's part of the ball; the standard hot history run back to light at the hop's energy; ℓ between 5.7 × 10⁻³⁵ m and the candidate 1.0 × 10⁻³² m).

## Inflation's four jobs, in the ball's units

| job | the standard need | in CO |
|---|---|---|
| one temperature everywhere | without inflation, the ball at the end of the epoch is 10⁸¹–10⁸⁸ regions never in contact (Hubble length 260 hops at the largest ℓ, 1.5 at the smallest) | every place commits at once, with no order among the first commitments (the beginning's shape): sameness is put in by the rule, not made by contact |
| flat space | expansion balanced against density to 1 part in 10⁵⁶–10⁶¹ at the epoch's end | see Flatness, below |
| the first ripples | 10⁻⁵ in amplitude, nearly the same on every scale | silence. Independent noise from place to place gives about 10⁻⁴² on the scales seen in the sky: the ripples must be correlated across the ball from the start |
| no monopoles | diluted by the stretch | no unified group is broken, so none of that kind are made; whether the which-way phase on the relations makes lattice monopoles of its own is owed |

The epoch itself is short: about 6 e-folds in length (dark_matter.md), not inflation's 60. At its end space grows by 0.4% per tick in length at the largest ℓ and 66% at the smallest.

## Flatness (21 Nov 2026, desk)

*The tuning in its usual form* treats the expansion rate and the density as two numbers that must agree to 1 part in 10⁵⁶–10⁶¹ at the epoch's end. For uniform space Einstein's constraint is Friedmann's equation, and the one free datum is the curvature k, whose scale stretches with space: today's bound |Ω_k| < 0.002 says the curvature radius is at least 7 times the visible universe's radius, at every epoch.

*On the network.* A uniformly curved space is a flat one with a stretch that bends slowly from place to place (conformally flat; ψ² ∝ 1/(1 + kr²/4)); curvature is the stretch's large-scale bending (its second derivative; a plain gradient is flat). On the cubic graph, k = 0 is the stretch being uniform, and today's bound asks that it vary by no more than about half a percent across the visible ball.

*The flat rate in CO's constants.* With G = δc³ℓ²/(4πħ) and a place holding energy ε·ħc/ℓ, the constraint gives

$$(H\tau)^2=\frac{2}{3}\,\delta\,\varepsilon,\qquad \tau=\ell/c,$$

the growth per tick in length; births per place per tick are 3Hτ. (ε ≈ 0.66 reproduces the standard history's 0.4% and 66% per tick.)

*What this does to the problem.* It moves the tuning; it does not remove it. Two things must hold:
1. *The births obey the constraint.* The earlier rule for the epoch (births at the commitment rate of over-capacity places) cannot: its rate is fixed per tick, while the constraint's falls as √ε through the epoch, by about 10⁴. Chosen (candidate): expansion is carried by the shift, an outward river b = H(x − x₀), and, in the comoving description, births follow it, τ∇·b = 3Hτ per place per tick (Λ's birth chance its constant part). With the stretch uniform the constraint is then 6H² = 16πGρ + 2Λc², so the birth rate is the constraint: an identity, not a match. On the step a ball sourcing its own outward river expands as flat dust (run F7: shells on the k = 0 path within 2.3%, one growth factor for all shells with a spread of 1.1%; the river and the uniform ball are entered). The river was entered at zero energy, the flat case; why the beginning makes that river is item 2.
2. *"At once" covers the stretch,* not only the temperature: the places' stretch uniform to half a percent across the ball.

Also open: the rule near a mass, where the river converges (∇·b < 0).

*If births cannot exceed one per place per tick:* 3Hτ ≤ 1 needs δε ≤ 1/6; at ε ≈ 0.66, δ ≤ 0.25 and ℓ ≥ 1.15 × 10⁻³⁴ m. Conditional on item 1 and on that cap; not a bound.

*The river and the tick.* The river grows with distance from the chosen centre, and the tick's limit on a drift (about π/τ_f − 3 times light's speed along an axis) is reached at that many Hubble lengths: 4.9 at τ_f = 0.4, 13 at 0.2, 28 at 0.1 (about 2.8 along a body diagonal at 0.4). Since τ_f is a convention and any place can be the centre, this is a limit on one description's reach, not an edge of the network; in the comoving description (births, no river) it does not arise.

Grade: reading. With births following the river (chosen, candidate), the birth rate is gravity's constraint and flatness is the stretch being uniform across the ball; that uniformity rests on the beginning and is not shown.
