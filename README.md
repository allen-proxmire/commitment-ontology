# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously. **A reconstruction, from two primitives, of gravity in empty space and for dust, and of quantum mechanics up to 1930 for a fixed number of particles, short of the exchange sign.**

GR has no global now; QM has no event. CO takes both as primitives: commitment and possibility. That places it among event theories, with Haag's programme and Blanchard–Jadczyk's event-enhanced quantum theory rather than with the spontaneous-collapse models: an event happens when possibility in transit is engaged by what has already committed, never on its own, so a pattern left alone is not resolved at any observable rate (candidate). Taking both on carries three costs: a now seems to pick out a preferred frame, which relativity forbids; events seem to wash out interference, as measurements do, though quantum mechanics needs it kept; and CO must say when a contact is an event at all. The first it answers as relativity does, for the primitives (ruled): a commitment's now is its past light cone, point-relative, nothing added. The lattice that carries matter does pick a frame; whether it can be made not to is open. The second has a candidate for a single pattern (a commitment resolves exactly the possibility it engages) and a candidate for an entangled pair whose price is named but not paid. The third it does not yet answer.

CO has no collapse term and predicts none: a pattern left alone is not resolved at any observable rate, so an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time (candidate; the free stock has no amplitude and is not branched, so the trace a renewal leaves in it carries no which-way information; the consistency of a Born-weighted dent with no back-action, no-signalling included, is open). This is quantum mechanics' own prediction, not a new one; it separates CO from the spontaneous-collapse models, not from quantum mechanics.

Every experiment to date agrees with quantum mechanics and with the collapse models inside their surviving parameter window; parameter-free Diósi–Penrose is already excluded. Large-mass interferometry and levitated-optomechanics proposals would narrow that window; they would not separate CO from quantum mechanics.

One outcome would falsify it: two masses becoming entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto–Vedral 2017). That is semiclassical gravity's prediction too, shared rather than new. Beyond it CO predicts nothing of its own, and says so.

It asks to be judged as an ontology without a measurement dynamics is judged, the standard applied to Haag's programme and to the interpretations: internal consistency, economy of assumptions, recovery of what is known, and, if it comes to differ, a difference that can be tested. 

The ontology has been in development for all of 2026. The intent is that it resonates as a logical story of how the universe operates, and for others to advance the ideas if possible.
