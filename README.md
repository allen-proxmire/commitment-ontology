**[`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md).** Two primitives, possibility and commitment, and two constants of their own, δ and P_max. From them: Newton's law, gravitational redshift, universal free fall, the black hole as starvation, the arrow of time, the beginning, inflation with its own exit, Friedmann's equation with the matter and radiation eras, Λ as the one constant the symmetry allows, and the four classical tests of gravity (standard weak-field physics, reached from the primitives). Gravitational waves: a scalar field radiates wrongly, so relations carry an occupancy and, with "sliding costs nothing", the cost of mismatch is forced to Einstein–Hilbert (Regge on the network), giving GR's quadrupole and tensor polarizations; the exterior is then GR's, and the exponential-metric prediction that once stood here was withdrawn after review. Three dimensions follow as a consistency condition of the rules (standard pieces); locality is supported by runs and flatness is shown to be dimension-blind. The radiation era is not derived. Complete as far as the two primitives force it, with no prediction that differs from GR.

**[`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md).** One addition to the inventory: commitments happen at a rate. Complete as far as the one addition forces.

**[`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md).** What remains: the particle masses, the field's strength (1/137), and colour. Each needs something new. Exploratory. CO covers physics up to 1930, gravity, electromagnetism and the electron, and stops at the nucleus.
