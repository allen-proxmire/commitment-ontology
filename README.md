# Commitment Ontology

CO is an ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment, and a short list of qualities they are given (P1–P8, C1–C9, each graded in [`TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md`](TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md)). From them it reaches gravity and, largely by identification with known constructions, quantum mechanics up to 1930 for a fixed number of particles, with pair creation and annihilation by identification (candidate). It is incomplete, and says where.

*Possibility* sits at places and moves between related places. It is never made or lost in passing; what a draw takes from it becomes record, and new places are born with their share. 

*Commitment* is an irreversible event that engages possibility. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow.

GR has no global now; QM has no event. CO takes both as primitives, which places it among event theories (Haag's programme, Blanchard–Jadczyk) rather than collapse models. A commitment's now is its past light cone, nothing added. When a contact counts as an event is not yet answered.

**What goes in.** Two primitives and the qualities P1–P8, C1–C9, each posited or candidate. For gravity beyond linear order: locality and two derivatives at every order, Lorentz-invariant interactions, and the metric identification. Four constants of the theory's own (δ, P_max, Λ, and the stopping number N) and a birth share whose value is open. One tuning, accepted for now: the lattice's loops would give species different top speeds, and the counter-terms that keep them equal (to the 10⁻²⁰ the data require) are set by hand. G, c, ħ, 1/137 and the particle masses as measured. From quantum mechanics: the Born rule as an identification, Lüders conditioning, the joint law of an entangled pair, and an interaction's coupling and pointer basis. Three exclusions from experiment: a fixed hop order, a scalar gravitational wave, a one-way hop.

**What comes out.**
- *Gravity* ([`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md)). The static weak field: Newton's 1/r, redshift and free fall from ratio hopping (a static form; the field that propagates comes from no backdrop, below); light bending and the Shapiro delay once Cassini fixes γ = 1; Mercury's precession once it fixes β = 1. G's form ties the hop length to δ, resting on a relation's stiffness equalling the hop's conductance (shape). A gyroscope's geodetic and frame-dragging precession match Gravity Probe B (candidate), given the single coupling, γ = 1, and no backdrop applied to matter (continuum, linear order). Tensor waves: at linear order the relations' cost is forced, given that moving places and reparametrising each place's time cost nothing (with locality and two derivatives), to linearised Einstein's, isotropic on the cubic graph up to (kℓ)² terms. GR's full exterior: Einstein–Hilbert, with Λ an input, is the only consistent continuation of the linear cost, given no backdrop at every order, locality and two derivatives, Lorentz-invariant interactions and the metric identification (continuum; the lattice's nonlinear cost open). GR's source inside matter (short of nuclear stress) and in the radiation era, given the walk on the curved network and P = P∞. The field is carried by the network's places, relations and squares, with a home for every part of the metric.
- *Clocks.* The clock rate, ω = (mc²/ħ)(1 − h), follows from the commitment cycle under the free-share rule; atomic-clock coherence excludes ticks that come by chance, if h counts every mass.
- *Matter* ([`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md)). Dirac's equation and spin ½ by identification with a known quantum walk; mass as the rate of commitments; charge as a carried sign. Two-body binding with Breit's terms, by identification: helium, positronium and hydrogen's 21 cm line at tree level. Resonance and recoil follow from the walk and the which-way phase. The exchange sign for two patterns, by identification (candidate): ±1 in three dimensions given a flat exchange factor, and (−1)^{2s} from the spinor's turn through the places' frames, so spin-½ patterns exclude and whole-spin ones merge. Pairs made from light and annihilated, by identification with lattice quantum electrodynamics (candidate); the weak interaction is absent.
- *Nucleus* ([`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md)). Colour as a split (C9, candidate): a whole split into parts alike in colour, three as the first count whose relations close a loop (the author's contrast principle), rotations of their colour labels an SU(3) redundancy. The colour field's cost and coupling are posited; the quarks' charges wait on a kind label (not ruled); confinement, the hadron spectrum and the nucleon's mass follow by identification with lattice QCD (candidate; conditional on C9, tuning, the colour field quantised with its posited cost and coupling, and the quark masses as inputs; confinement numerical, not proved), short of electromagnetic and isospin splittings (charges not ruled); the masses and 1/137 are not supplied.
- *Dark matter and matter over antimatter* (shapes, [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)). Dark matter as a pattern that renews with no channel, permitted, not predicted: given CO's present inventory a fermion, so, if the shape holds, not ultralight (m ≥ 0.13 keV from dwarf galaxies); and, given gravity's nonlinear rule, no MOND-like change, so galaxies' extra pull is matter. The first commitments set the net count of matter, so the photon-to-matter ratio becomes a property of the beginning's epoch, its value open.

**The frame problem, tuned for now.** The lattice matter walks on picks a rest frame. At tree level this costs only a bound on the hop, ℓ ≲ 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ m. Above tree level, loops would give each species its own top speed unless the bare speeds are tuned, to about 1 part in 10¹⁷ of the loop-sized (α/π) shift. Gravity's nonlinear rule rests on the same question, since it assumes Lorentz-invariant interactions. Every exit read is closed except tuning, accepted for now (the author's ruling), and a network whose coordinates do not commute ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md)). CO stands at tree level.

**What it predicts.** No collapse term: an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time. That is quantum mechanics' own prediction; it separates CO from the collapse models, not from quantum mechanics. CO's departure from quantum mechanics with gravity: gravity's classical source follows a body's engagements, not its unresolved state (the author's ruling, candidate). So an isolated superposition feels no gravity of its own, gravity entangles nothing (as in every classical-gravity model), and macroscopic bodies, engaged constantly, gravitate where they are (Page–Geilker 1981 passed); a mean source would, with the measurement update, signal faster than light. One outcome would falsify CO: two masses becoming entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto–Vedral 2017). Two possible signatures of its own: heavy doubler particles above 10¹⁴ GeV, beyond reach; and a stopping number N for measurement, a constant of CO's own (posited: not derived, set only by experiment), falsifiable through its universality: as the unreversed part of a many-body echo is made smaller against the coupling, the number of spins or excitations the disturbance has spread over when the echo is lost should stop growing at the same N in every system, where quantum mechanics has it keep growing, up to the system's size, once that part is small enough, and otherwise sets any ceiling by each system's own dynamics. Present data give N ≳ 50 under the stated readings ([the test](RECORD_OF_METHOD/MATTER_notes/N_signature_note.md)).
