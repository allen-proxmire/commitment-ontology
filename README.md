# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously.

**Current project: [`CO_2.0.md`](CO_2.0.md).** Two primitives, possibility and commitment. Two constants of its own, δ and P_max. From them: Newton's law, gravitational redshift, universal free fall, the black hole as starvation, the arrow of time, the beginning, inflation with its own exit, Friedmann's equation and the matter era from Newton plus inertia, a Λ-like term from births in the voids, all four classical tests of gravity with the exterior as the exponential metric, two kill tests about space, one in the strong field, and a rough bound on P_max. Open: the particle masses, geometry, the expansion curve. Everything about expansion is candidate, not ruled.

**[`CO_1.0_misdefined-primitives/`](CO_1.0_misdefined-primitives/)** is the first attempt, kept whole: six primitives, a lattice engine, 105 runs, and an audited record of what was borrowed, tried and dropped. It reproduced known physics by borrowing its structure and converged on interpretations others had reached. Its primitives were wrong in category (matter and record as kinds of thing; time as a place's count), which is why 2.0 restarts from scratch.

Independent, speculative, not peer-reviewed.
