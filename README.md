# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously. **A reconstruction of physics up to 1930 from two primitives.**

GR has no now; QM has no event. CO takes both as primitives. It must then say how there can be a now without a preferred frame, and how there can be events without spoiling interference.

**[`CO_plain_language.md`](CO_plain_language.md)** is the theory as it stands, for anyone. The three subject documents below report what stands, results only. **[`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/)** holds the working records: every route tried, every withdrawal with its reason, and the runs.

**[`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md).** From possibility and commitment, with two constants of their own: standard weak-field gravity and the four classical tests; GR's exterior and tensor waves, with Lovelock's theorem doing the work; three dimensions as a consistency condition of the rules; inflation with its own exit and the matter era. The radiation era is not derived, and there is no prediction that differs from GR.

**[`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md).** With one addition, that commitments happen at a rate: the amplitude rule given its premises, a four-component internal state (Meyer, Clifford), mass as the rate of commitments, and Dirac's equation with spin ½ by identification with a known construction. Charge is a carried sign; its strength, 1/137, is an input.

**[`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md).** The internal state carries charge and no other symmetry; the three lines of space are not colour. Two candidates, costs stated: the proton as three tiles bound by kinship, and entanglement as one pattern sampled twice. The masses, 1/137 and the count three each need something not in the inventory; CO stops at the nucleus.

Independent, speculative, not peer-reviewed.

CO makes no prediction of its own, and says so. It asks to be judged by the standard physics already applies to interpretations and to string theory: internal consistency, economy of assumptions, and recovery of what is known. Many-worlds adds no equation and is taken seriously as an ontology; string theory has produced no distinct accessible-energy prediction in forty years and is taken seriously for its consistency and its recovery of GR. By that standard, two primitives that recover physics up to 1930, with every withdrawal on record, belong in the conversation. 

The ontology has been in development for all of 2026. The intent is that it resonates as a logical story of how the universe operates, and for others to advance the ideas if possible.
