# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously.

**Current project: [`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md)** (the working record) **and [`CO_plain_language.md`](CO_plain_language.md)** (the theory as it stands, in plain language). Two primitives, possibility and commitment. Two constants of its own, δ and P_max. From them: Newton's law, gravitational redshift, universal free fall, the black hole as starvation, the arrow of time, the beginning, inflation with its own exit, Friedmann's equation with the matter and radiation eras from Newton plus inertia, Λ an input with its seat as the one constant the symmetry allows, all four classical tests of gravity with the exterior as the exponential metric, a strong-field prediction that differs from GR, and a rough bound on P_max. Gravitational waves: a scalar field radiates wrongly, so relations carry an occupancy and, with "sliding costs nothing", the cost of mismatch is forced to Einstein–Hilbert (Regge on the network), giving GR's quadrupole and tensor polarizations; three dimensions follow as a consistency condition, the only dimension in which every ruling holds and nothing has to be added, with the mechanism for locality supported by runs (a global slide-invariant cost on which relations are kept) and flatness shown to be dimension-blind, so that three is the consistency condition's to pick. Open: the particle masses, the values of δ, P_max and Λ, the quantum sector.

**2.0 stops here**, complete as far as the two primitives force it. The next step, the masses, needs a commitment to have an inside, which is the first addition to the inventory since the restart. That is [`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md), where so far: amplitudes with phases and the Born rule are forced; an internal state is forced (Meyer) and is four-dimensional (Clifford); mass is the rate of commitments; the continuum limit is Dirac's equation with spin ½ and g = 2; charge is a sign a pattern carries, with its strength from data; the particle masses and colour remain open. **2.1 stops there**, complete as far as its one addition forces; the masses, the field's strength and colour each need something new, and that is [`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md).

**CO 1.0**, the first attempt, is archived in the ED Generative repo (`Public_repo_archive/commitment-ontology_1.0/`) and kept whole: six primitives, a lattice engine, 105 runs, and an audited record of what was borrowed, tried and dropped. It reproduced known physics by borrowing its structure and converged on interpretations others had reached. Its primitives were wrong in category (matter and record as kinds of thing; time as a place's count), which is why 2.0 restarts from scratch.
