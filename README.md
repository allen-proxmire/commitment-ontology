# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously.

CO is an ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment, and a short list of qualities they are given (P1–P8, C1–C8, each graded in [`TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md`](TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md)). From them it reaches gravity and, largely by identification with known constructions, quantum mechanics up to 1930 for a fixed number of particles. It is incomplete, and says where.

*Possibility* sits at places and moves between related places. It is never made or lost in passing; what a draw takes from it becomes record, and new places are born with their share. *Commitment* is an irreversible event that engages possibility. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow.

GR has no global now; QM has no event. CO takes both as primitives, which places it among event theories (Haag's programme, Blanchard–Jadczyk) rather than collapse models. A commitment's now is its past light cone, nothing added. When a contact counts as an event is not yet answered.

**What goes in.** Two primitives and the qualities P1–P8, C1–C8, most of them candidate. Three constants of the theory's own (δ, P_max, Λ) and a birth share whose value is open. G, c, ħ, 1/137 and the particle masses as measured. From quantum mechanics: the Born rule as an identification, Lüders conditioning, the joint law of an entangled pair, and an interaction's coupling and pointer basis. Three exclusions from experiment: a fixed hop order, a scalar gravitational wave, a one-way hop.

**What comes out.**
- *Gravity* ([`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md)). The static weak field, derived from the rules: Newton's 1/r, the form of G, redshift, free fall, light bending and the Shapiro delay. Two choices are fixed by data: Mercury's precession (β = 1) and light bending (γ = 1). Tensor waves: at linear order the relations' cost is forced to linearised Einstein's, isotropic on the cubic graph, since moving places and resetting clocks are both free. GR's full exterior, given Lovelock's hypotheses. GR's source inside matter (short of nuclear stress) and in the radiation era, given the walk on the curved network and P = P∞. The field is carried by the relations, with a home for every part of the metric.
- *Clocks.* The clock rate, ω = (mc²/ħ)(1 − h), follows from the commitment cycle under the free-share rule; atomic-clock coherence excludes ticks that come by chance.
- *Matter* ([`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md)). Dirac's equation and spin ½ by identification with a known quantum walk; mass as the rate of commitments; charge as a carried sign. Two-body binding with Breit's terms, by identification: helium, positronium and hydrogen's 21 cm line at tree level. Resonance and recoil follow from the walk and the which-way phase.
- *Nucleus* ([`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md)). CO stops here: the masses, 1/137 and the count three each need something not in the inventory.

**The open problem.** The lattice matter walks on picks a rest frame. At tree level this costs only a bound on the hop, ℓ ≲ 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ m. Above tree level, loops would give each species its own top speed unless the bare speeds are tuned to about 1 part in 10¹⁷. No exit found so far removes it short of a change at the foundation ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md)). CO stands at tree level.

**What it predicts.** No collapse term: an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time. That is quantum mechanics' own prediction; it separates CO from the collapse models, not from quantum mechanics. One outcome would falsify CO: two masses becoming entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto–Vedral 2017), a prediction it shares with semiclassical gravity. Two possible signatures of its own, both distant: heavy doubler particles above 10¹⁴ GeV, and a stopping number N for measurement.

It asks to be judged as an ontology without a measurement dynamics is judged, the standard applied to Haag's programme and to the interpretations: internal consistency, economy of assumptions, recovery of what is known, and, if it comes to differ, a difference that can be tested.

The ontology has been in development for all of 2026. The intent is that it resonates as a logical story of how the universe operates, and for others to advance the ideas if possible.
