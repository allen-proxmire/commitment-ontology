# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously. **A reconstruction, from two primitives and the qualities CO gives them (P1–P8, C1–C8, graded), with some pieces imported, of gravity (in empty space and for dust; inside matter and in the radiation era by candidate rulings) and of quantum mechanics up to 1930 for a fixed number of particles, short of the exchange sign.**

*Possibility* sits at places and moves between related places, conserved in everything that passes and commits; each new place is born with its share (candidate). *Commitment* is an irreversible event that engages possibility. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow.

GR has no global now; QM has no event. CO takes both as primitives. That places it among event theories, with Haag's programme and Blanchard–Jadczyk's event-enhanced quantum theory rather than with the spontaneous-collapse models: a pattern is resolved only when another commitment engages its possibility in transit, never on its own, so a pattern left alone is not resolved at any observable rate (candidate). Taking both on carries three costs: a now seems to pick out a preferred frame, which relativity forbids; events seem to wash out interference, as measurements do, though quantum mechanics needs it kept; and CO must say when a contact is an event at all. The first it answers as relativity does, for the primitives (ruled): a commitment's now is its past light cone, point-relative, nothing added. This holds of the primitives; the lattice the walk lives on picks a frame (below). The second has a candidate for a single pattern (a commitment resolves exactly the possibility it engages) and a candidate for an entangled pair whose price is named but not paid. The third it does not yet answer.

**The open problem.** The lattice that matter walks on is free possibility in motion (candidate): classical, since free possibility has no amplitude, and finite in degree, so it picks a rest frame. At tree level this costs only a bound on the hop. Above tree level, matter's loops would give each species its own top speed unless the bare speeds are tuned to agree to about 1 part in 10¹⁷, permitted but unexplained. No exit read so far removes it short of a change at the foundation ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md)). CO therefore stands at tree level.

**What it predicts.** CO has no collapse term and predicts none: a pattern left alone is not resolved at any observable rate, so an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time (candidate; the free stock has no amplitude and is not branched, so the trace a renewal leaves in it carries no which-way information; the consistency of a Born-weighted dent with no back-action beyond the Schrödinger–Newton term through h, no-signalling included, is open). This is quantum mechanics' own prediction, not a new one; it separates CO from the spontaneous-collapse models, not from quantum mechanics.

Every experiment to date agrees with quantum mechanics and with the collapse models inside their surviving parameter window; parameter-free Diósi–Penrose is already excluded. Large-mass interferometry and levitated-optomechanics proposals would narrow that window; they would not separate CO from quantum mechanics.

One outcome would falsify it: two masses becoming entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto–Vedral 2017). That is semiclassical gravity's prediction too, shared rather than new. It rests on gravity's source being classical, which leaves two costs open: keeping the mean-field term from signalling, and keeping the source conserved when a body is resolved. Beyond it CO makes no firm prediction of its own; two possible signatures remain: heavy doubler particles above 10¹⁴ GeV (from the candidate Wilson term), beyond any reach, and a stopping number N for measurement (shape; N ≳ ~50).

It asks to be judged as an ontology without a measurement dynamics is judged, the standard applied to Haag's programme and to the interpretations: internal consistency, economy of assumptions, recovery of what is known, and, if it comes to differ, a difference that can be tested. 

The ontology has been in development for all of 2026. The intent is that it resonates as a logical story of how the universe operates, and for others to advance the ideas if possible.
