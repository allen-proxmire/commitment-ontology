# Commitment Ontology

Time is a count of irreversible events. Everything else is what follows when that is taken seriously.

**[`CO_plain_language.md`](CO_plain_language.md)** is the theory as it stands, for anyone. The working records, with everything tried and withdrawn, are the three subject documents below.

**[`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md).** Two primitives, possibility and commitment, and two constants of their own, δ and P_max. From them: Newton's law, gravitational redshift, universal free fall, the black hole as starvation, the arrow of time, the beginning, inflation with its own exit, Friedmann's equation with the matter and radiation eras, Λ as the one constant the symmetry allows, all four classical tests of gravity with the exterior as the exponential metric, and a strong-field prediction that differs from GR. Gravitational waves: a scalar field radiates wrongly, so relations carry an occupancy and, with "sliding costs nothing", the cost of mismatch is forced to Einstein–Hilbert (Regge on the network), giving GR's quadrupole and tensor polarizations. Three dimensions follow as a consistency condition, the only dimension in which every ruling holds and nothing has to be added; locality is supported by runs and flatness is shown to be dimension-blind. Complete as far as the two primitives force it.

**[`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md).** One addition to the inventory: commitments happen at a rate. From it: amplitudes with phases and the Born rule, forced; an internal state, forced (Meyer) and four-dimensional (Clifford); mass as the rate of commitments, with a maximum mass that sets the hop length; the continuum limit as Dirac's equation with spin ½ and g = 2; charge as a sign a pattern carries, with its strength from data. Complete as far as the one addition forces.

**[`NUCLEUS.md`](NUCLEUS.md).** What remains: the particle masses, the field's strength (1/137), and colour. Each needs something new. The three lines of space are shown not to be colour; an internal three is outside the inventory; a third primitive would be admissible on the proton contradiction and worth it only if it cascades. Exploratory. CO covers physics up to 1930, gravity, electromagnetism and the electron, and stops at the nucleus.

**CO 1.0**, the first attempt, is archived in the ED Generative repo (`Public_repo_archive/commitment-ontology_1.0/`) and kept whole: six primitives, a lattice engine, 105 runs, and an audited record of what was borrowed, tried and dropped. Its primitives were wrong in category (matter and record as kinds of thing; time as a place's count), which is why the current work restarted from scratch.

Independent, speculative, not peer-reviewed.
