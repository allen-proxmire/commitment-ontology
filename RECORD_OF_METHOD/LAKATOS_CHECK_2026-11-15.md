Tuning 4 itself (light's direction counter-terms, needed on the palindrome) was found and removed within the period; it is not counted.

**Exclusions found.**
- Cycling the nesting order by step, by three or six orders: no gap, extra massless species.
- The body-centred-cubic Dirac automaton as CO's step: doubled, symmetric only under half-turns about the axes, first-order direction-dependent dispersion.
- Choosing the nesting order by sublattice: not unitary on a product of whole-lattice operators.
- Induced light as the remover of light's speed counter-term: the finite parts differ, about 10⁻³ at physical masses.
- The fizz-blind reading of tuning 1: transit is what feels the lattice.

**Withdrawn.**
- The inner reflection as protector of the handednesses' speeds: misidentified; the protecting operator is CP-type and potential corrections break it.
- B2g's "about 25× smaller and negative": a fit artefact; the linear excess is zero.
- I1's "heavy species" explanation and "a positive log eating a negative term": the wrong sign came from corner species near quasienergy π.
- I2's extrapolated speed gap "4 × 10⁻⁴ to 8 × 10⁻³": mixed fits; about 10⁻³.
- Tuning 4's size of 0.7% in total: it is 0.5–0.8% per species, about 5% for the known charges.
- The electron Crab bound at r = ½ (3.8 × 10⁻³² m): an artefact of discarding negative quadratic terms.
- "r < 2/3 keeps the palindrome's pattern": half true; the palindrome had an electron band, r = ½ has none.
- A lattice-carrying lag inside the horizon: the lag was the measure's.

**Reading.** Two progressive additions, both removing tunings: the domain wall (mass) and the flow step (direction). The flow step is the larger move. It replaced the step itself, and the replacement paid twice more than it was brought for (P7f's window and the wrong-sign vacuum both went away). The cost is a new posited value, r, chosen with an eye on bounds, and a model-independent bound on ℓ ten times looser than before, with the old size restored only through a candidate synchrotron argument. Watch: r is a value chosen for its consequences; if it is later tuned to dodge a bound, count it as accommodating. Tuning 1 (speeds per species and handedness) is untouched.
