# L1b — a strictly local Wilson walk, and L1 rechecked on it (registered 2026-11-09)

**Why.** The adopted Wilson flip exp(−iβ[θ + rΣ(1 − cos k_j)]) is only quasi-local (global tick entry). A strictly local alternative: replace each conditional shift P_j by A_j = S_j⁺ F(w) S_j⁻ F(−w), with S_j⁺ moving only the α_j = +1 part forward, S_j⁻ only the α_j = −1 part back (P_j = S_j⁺S_j⁻), F(a) = exp(−iβa) on-site. At k = 0 the coins cancel; at k_j = π, S_j^± = ±α_j flips the coin's sign and the doubler is gapped (1D check: quasi-energy π − 2w at k = π). Every factor acts on one place or moves one part one place: strictly local. At w = 0 the walk is the adopted palindrome without the Wilson term. Step: A_x A_y A_z F(θ) A_z A_y A_x.

**Unknown.** (i) Whether all species but the one at k = 0 are gapped (both quasi-energy sectors) for some w. (ii) Whether a massive pattern still outruns the massless one, and by how much: L1 found s ≈ 0.24·r·mcℓ/ħ for the quasi-local walk.

**Measured.** w ∈ {0.2, 0.3, π/10}: gapless points on a 40³ grid (|quasi-energy| < 0.08 or > π − 0.08) at θ = 0. Then as L1: s(θ) = √(A(θ)/A(0)) − 1 at θ = 0.01, 0.02, 0.04 along axis, face and body diagonals; massless bending B/A²; branch splitting.

**Decision.** If (i) holds and s is linear in θ, the electron bound is recomputed from the new coefficient and replaces 3 × 10⁻³¹ m if the strictly local walk is adopted. If s vanishes at order θ, the tree-level electron bound goes and light's (10⁻²⁷ m) binds. If (i) fails for all w, the strictly local form is excluded and the quasi-local walk stands with its tails.

**Checks.** Pre-run: at w = 0, θ = 0 the speed is 2 and isotropic (the palindrome). Post-run: fit residuals.
