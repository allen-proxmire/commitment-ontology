# B6: the chiral anomaly on the walls (spectral flow) — fail conditions (16 Nov 2026, before the run)

B5's set-up (inner ring 28 places, walls ±0.5, r = ½, qB = 2π/120 along z). An electric field along z shifts every k_z at the rate qE. The anomaly in its integer form: across the whole zone of k_z, each wall's lowest Landau level crosses zero energy exactly once, in opposite directions on the two walls, and no other state comes near zero. Then a field E moves charge from one wall to the other through the ring at (qE/2π) per guiding centre per unit time: per unit volume (q²/4π²)E·B on each wall, opposite in sign (charge leaves one wall and arrives at the other; the total is kept).

| check | fail if |
|---|---|
| B6a: over k_z ∈ (−π, π] (64 points, offset from zero), states with |E| < 0.15 cross zero exactly once on each wall, with opposite slopes, at k_y = 0 and 0.7 | any other count, or the same slope on both walls |
| B6b: no state with |E| < 0.15 anywhere else in the zone that is not one of the two walls' lowest levels (no doubler crossing near k_z = π) | any such state |
