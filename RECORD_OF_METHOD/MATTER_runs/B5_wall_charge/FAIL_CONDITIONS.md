B4's set-up (inner ring 2L = 28 places, walls ±0.5, r = ½ in all directions, free fermions) with a uniform magnetic field along z entering every relation as the which-way phase (Peierls, Landau gauge A_y = Bx, one flux quantum through the 120-place x period: qB = 2π/120).
