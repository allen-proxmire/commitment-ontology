# B2h: photon splitting for domain-wall matter (desk, 15 Nov 2026)

Photon splitting (γ → e⁺e⁻ through lopsided splits) needs straight light and electrons whose dispersion bends down at high momentum; the threshold is E_th = C√(mₑc²·ħc/ℓ) with C ∝ |c₂|^(−1/4), c₂ the electrons' quadratic term (E = v k (1 + c₂ k²)), for a fixed split geometry.

- *Speed excess:* domain-wall matter has none that grows with its mass (B2g cold read, 1+1D), so the relevant case is the one already recorded with s absent (C = 3.8–4.1 for the Wilson walk's quadratic term).
- *Quadratic term (b2h_bend.py):* in 1+1D the massless wall mode bends exactly four times as much as CO's plain 1D walk (c₂ = −0.1152 against −0.0288, i.e. −sin²w/6 against −sin²w/24). If the same factor held in 3+1D, C would fall by 4^(−1/4) = 0.71, to 2.7–2.9, and LHAASO's 2.5 PeV photon would bound ℓ < 1.2×10⁻³¹ m (against 2.3×10⁻³¹ m), since ℓ_max ∝ C².
- *Not known:* the 3+1D wall mode's quadratic term (it needs the 4+1D walk). The coefficient enters as a quarter power, so the test's form survives either way: a confirmed photon far above the threshold excludes straight light with domain-wall matter too.
