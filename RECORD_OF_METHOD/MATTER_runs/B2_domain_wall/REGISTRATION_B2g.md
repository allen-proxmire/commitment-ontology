# B2g: registration (14 Nov 2026, before the run)

**Question.** The Wilson walk gives a massive pattern a speed excess s = κ θ (θ the mass in radians per step; κ = ½ sin w = 0.416 at w = 5π/16 in CO's 3D walk), which sets the Crab bound ℓ ≲ 3×10⁻³² m. Does the domain-wall walk's massive mode carry a speed excess linear in its mass, and with what κ_dw? κ_dw sets the bound on ℓ for domain-wall matter (ℓ_max ∝ 1/κ). If κ_dw ≈ 0, the electron Crab bound on ℓ goes away.

**Measure.** Strip, w = 5π/16, θ = ±0.5, L = 16 (undisturbed residual 8×10⁻⁴). Mass enters as the spin-turning wall-to-wall coupling of strength μ (rows L and 0, n̂₀ = (1,1,1)/√3). m = ½ the gap at the crossing. v(k) = dE/dk of the upper sub-gap level (central difference, step 10⁻⁴). Δv(k) = v_massive(k) − v_massless(k). Fit Δv(k) = s − A/k² over k ∈ [5m, 20m]; κ_dw = s/(m·c₀) against m (μ chosen so m ≈ 0.005–0.04).

**Calibration, same code.** CO's plain 1D Wilson walk A_x F(θ) A_x at the same w, m = θ: gives κ_1D, compared with the walk's own analytic value.

**Read-out (stated first).** κ_dw from a straight-line fit of s against m through the origin. Valid if R² > 0.99 and the calibration reproduces its analytic κ within 5%; otherwise the measure is not trusted. Also reported: the massless wall mode's speed against c₀ (a speed offset, tuning 1).

**Amendment (after a crash, before any domain-wall result).** The first run crashed at the first domain-wall point: with m ≈ 0.04 the fit window k ≤ 20m reaches past the bulk gap, where the sub-gap level no longer exists. The domain-wall range is changed to m ≈ 0.004–0.012 (μ = 0.015–0.04) and the sub-gap filter to |E| < 0.45. Only the calibration had been printed (ds/dm = 0.4045, against the analytic ½ sin w = 0.4157: within 3%, valid).
