# I5: scope of the direction dependence: registration (15 Nov 2026, before the run)

**Questions.** (1) Do light's permittivity and permeability on CO's walk have off-diagonal parts (four counter-terms if not, up to ten if so)? (2) Is the direction dependence particular to w = 5π/16, or present across the Wilson window?

**Method.** I4's pair method (filled–filled pairs dropped), 9-cell nested-cube quadrature, fit C₀ + c₂q̂² + c₄q̂⁴ at q = θ/20, θ/10, θ/5, θ = 0.01.
- (1) Potential varying along a face diagonal n̂ = (1,1,0)/√2, (1,0,1)/√2, (0,1,1)/√2 (block momenta k and k + q n̂): ε_n̂ = ½(ε_ii + ε_jj) + ε_ij, so ε_ij = ε_n̂ − ½(ε_ii + ε_jj) with the axis values at the same θ and w.
- (2) At w = 0.95 and 1.10 (inside π/4 < w < 3π/8): ε_x, ε_y, ε_z, and Z_B for B along y and along z.

**Outcomes (stated first).**
- (1) Off-diagonal present if any |ε_ij| > 10⁻³ (the diagonal offsets are 6×10⁻³ and 7.6×10⁻²); absent if all are below 10⁻⁴; otherwise inconclusive.
- (2) Present across the window if at both w some diagonal offset (ε_z − ε_x, ε_y − ε_x, or the B offset) exceeds 10⁻²; particular to 5π/16 if all offsets at both w fall below 10⁻³; otherwise mixed.
