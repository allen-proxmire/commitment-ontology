# Referee report: commitment-ontology — NUCLEUS.md, MATTER.md (doubler paragraph), runs R8–R10

## Summary

The documents under review make three kinds of claim: (a) computed statements about the lattice Dirac automaton (doublers, their removal, a commutant, a decay lifetime, a visibility bound); (b) arguments that colour cannot live in the inventory; (c) two "candidates" (Bell as "a pattern sampled twice"; the proton as a three-tile pattern). The computations of type (a) that were checked reproduce as far as the scripts go, but the central conclusion built on them — that two rulings leave the walk with "one species" and that the doubler item is "faced and closed" — is false at the parameters actually tested, and is an artefact of the 0.08 "gapless" threshold used in R9/R10. The arguments of type (b) are correct and textbook. The candidates of type (c) are relabellings of standard physics, candidly flagged as such in places, but presented with vacuous falsifiers and, in the Bell case, a "cost already paid" that does not exist in the public gravity document. Nothing in the reviewed material is a new result.

## Verified computations (independent re-derivation; scripts written outside the repo and run with python3 -I)

**(i) Doublers of U = M·P_z·P_y·P_x.** Confirmed. Massless, 12 points with quasi-energy 0: origin; (π,π,0), (π,0,π), (0,π,π); and the eight (±π/2)³. At each (±π/2)³ the hop product P_zP_yP_x equals ±γ₅ exactly (residual 0 to machine precision; signs: −γ₅ at (+,+,+), alternating with the product of signs). Since β anticommutes with γ₅, X = M(θ)(±γ₅) satisfies X² = 1, so the quasi-energies there are {0, 0, π, π} for every θ (checked at θ = 0, 0.3, 1.0, 1.5, 2.5). Dispersion about (π/2)³ at θ = 0.3 is conical with speed 0.9553 = cos θ along an axis and 0.957–0.963 along the diagonal: two-component massless modes with speed c cos θ, as the record states. *Omitted by the repo:* the same walk has 12 further points at quasi-energy π — (π,0,0)-type (3), (π,π,π), and the eight (±π/2)³ again — i.e. the "pseudo-doublers" of Gupta–Short, which the repo cites but never counts.

**(ii) Symmetrised ordering plus θ(k) = θ + r Σ(1 − cos k_j) leaves "exactly one gapless point."** True only under the 0.08 threshold; false as a statement about species. A local-minimum scan of the lowest |quasi-energy| band on a 40³ grid, refined by Nelder–Mead, gives:

- Strang form (r10b), θ = 0.3, r = 0.5: minima at the origin (E = 0.3000) **and at (π,π,π) with E = 0.1584** — an extra species *lighter than the test electron*. Analytic: at (π,π,π) the Strang hop product is −1 and W = 6, so E = |π − (θ + 6r)| = |π − 3.3| = 0.1584. Massless, r = 0.5: E(π,π,π) = |π − 3| = 0.1416, just above the 0.08 cut. R10's "exactly one gapless point massless, none massive" is a threshold artefact.
- Palindrome form (r10c; the ordering actually adopted in MATTER.md: x,y,z,flip,z,y,x), θ = 0.3, r = 0.5: origin E = 0.3000 **plus six minima at (π,π,±0.513π) and permutations with E = 0.3311** — six extra species 10% heavier than the physical one. Analytic at (π,π,π/2): hop product −α_zMα_z, W = 5, E = π − (θ + 5r) = 0.3416, with the true minimum slightly off the symmetric point. With θ = 0 and r = 0: the palindrome has **32** zero-energy points (every k with k_j ∈ {0, π} or pairs of ±π/2), not the "origin + three" implied by MATTER.md; with θ = 0.3, r = 0, all 32 sit at E = θ, degenerate with the physical species.
- Root cause: the "Wilson term in unitary form" is periodic. θ + rW with W ∈ [0, 6] wraps past π for r = 0.5, and the sign of the hop product at each doubler point decides whether the effective mass is θ + rW or π − θ − rW. For the palindrome at the physical θ ≈ 2.8 × 10⁻⁶ the lightest extra species sits at E = 0.62 (r = 0.5) or 0.87 (r ≈ 0.45) in hop units; the achievable maximum is ≈ 0.9, never larger. So the correct statement is "one light species plus doublers at the hop scale, for r in a window" — the standard Wilson statement — not "one species," and r = 0.5 is outside the window for both forms tested.
- R10's "with mass: 0" column is not a doubler count. For the naive reference, Strang and palindrome at θ = 0.3, r = 0, all doublers sit at exactly E = 0.3: "the mass term gaps everything" (R10_result.md) is a misreading of a threshold.
- r10c (the adopted walk) has no result note; its low-k check compares E(k) to 2√(θ² + k²), which is wrong. The measured dispersion is √(θ² + 4k²) (0.30267, 0.31634, 0.36094, 0.50087 at k = 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 vs 0.30265, 0.31623, 0.36056, 0.50000), i.e. the double step moves two hops per tick; the formula in the script would predict E(0) = 2θ.
- Q1b's L_z withdrawal is consistent with this (the sequential order is a screw), and is the one clean result of the ordering change.

**(iii) Damping lifetime.** Arithmetic confirmed: k = ℓ/ƛ_C = 2.85 × 10⁻⁶, decay per hop εk²/2 = 2.0 × 10⁻¹² at ε = 0.5, 2.7 × 10²⁶ hops/s, lifetime 1.81 × 10⁻¹⁵ s. The number scales as 1/ε and ε = 0.5 is not stated in MATTER.md or NUCLEUS.md. The exclusion itself is robust: the ratio of zone-edge decay (W = 2) to electron decay is 4/k² ≈ 5 × 10¹¹ independent of ε, so no ε removes doublers without removing the electron on a 10⁻¹⁵ s × (0.5/ε) timescale.

**(iv) R8.** Re-run reproduces exactly: cubic 8.88 species (108 modes below E_c = 0.5), random Delaunay nominal 245.7 (2892 modes), 758.8 at E_c = 0.3, 83.5 at E_c = 0.8. DOS normalisation A_single = V/π² is correct for a 4-component massless Dirac species counting both signs. Method weaknesses: hop weights are unit weights normalised only by the trace of the per-site tensor, not the Christ–Friedberg–Lee dual-volume weights; zeroth-order consistency Σ_j w_ij ê_ij = 0 is not enforced or checked, so the operator need not approximate α·∇ at all, and the broad E = 0 peak may be inconsistency of the discretisation rather than a doubling statement; one disorder sample; 11³ sites. The conclusion ("not the escape") is in the direction of the literature, but the registration's premise that "species count is a property of the lattice, not of the time-stepping" is refuted by the repo's own R9/R10, where the eight γ₅ modes are a property of the time-stepping.

**(v) Visibility bound.** V = (1 − δ)^N with N = mc²t/ħ. Independent numbers: electron 1 s, N = 7.76 × 10²⁰; C₇₀ (840 u, 5 ms), N = 5.94 × 10²⁴, bound δ < 1.7 × 10⁻²⁵; 25 kDa (10 ms), N = 3.54 × 10²⁶, bound δ < 2.8 × 10⁻²⁷. Quoted "δ ≲ 10⁻²⁷" and δ = 10⁻³² "inside by five orders" are consistent. The model (each renewal leaves an orthogonal which-path record of weight δ) is asserted, not derived; the bound criterion is V > 1/e.

**(vi) Commutant.** Confirmed: commutant of {α₁,α₂,α₃} on C⁴ is 2-dimensional (span of 1, γ₅); of {α_i, β} 1-dimensional; spin Casimir 3/4 on all four vectors (2 ⊕ 2). This is Schur's lemma applied to the irreducible Dirac representation and the reducible Cl₃ representation: textbook.

**(vii) String tension.** m_tile = m_p/3 = 0.3128 GeV; Compton length 0.631 fm; σ = m²/ħc = 0.496 GeV/fm; measured 0.9 GeV/fm ↔ √σ = 0.421 GeV; grain for 0.9 GeV/fm is 0.468 fm. Arithmetic correct. Note: `kinship_stretch_cost.py`, cited in NUCLEUS.md as the check, does not compute the m_p/3 number at all; it computes σ with a = hop length (1.6 × 10⁵ GeV/fm) and the grain needed for 0.9 GeV/fm. The 0.5 GeV/fm figure appears only in prose.

**Other scripts.** `hold_chirality_check.py` reproduces (1.1 × 10⁻¹⁴) but implements a renormalised explicit-Euler scheme (factor 0.25, non-unitary, normalised each step), not the automaton; the L↔R equality follows from an exact symmetry of the scheme and the number is round-off on an identity. `clifford_commutant.py` and `record_visibility_bound.py` reproduce.

## Errors found

1. **"With both, the walk has one species" (MATTER.md) and "Doublers. Faced and closed" (NUCLEUS.md) are false at the tested parameters.** Strang + Wilson (θ = 0.3, r = 0.5): extra species at (π,π,π) with E = 0.158 < 0.3. Palindrome + Wilson (the adopted walk): six extra species at E = 0.331 vs 0.300. Hidden by the 0.08 gapless threshold.
2. **"symmetrising the step removes the eight" and "three zone-edge doublers" misdescribe the adopted ordering.** The palindrome has 32 massless zero-sector points, not 4; the Wilson term must lift 31, and at r = 0.5 does not lift six of them adequately.
3. **The "Wilson term in unitary form" is periodic**, so doubler masses are bounded by ≈ 0.9 hop units and r must sit in a window (roughly r < (π − θ)/6 for the palindrome's W = 6 points, with the sign structure of the hop product deciding each point). Neither document mentions this; r = 0.5 violates it.
4. **Quasi-energy-π modes are never counted.** The sequential walk has 12 of them massless; the adopted walk at θ = 0.3, r = 0.5 has π-sector minima at 0.158 (π,π,π), 0.78 (six), 0.79 (twelve), 0.84 (three). Gupta–Short, cited by the repo, argue these "pseudo-doublers" threaten vacuum stability once interactions are added.
5. **"In every ordering that is symmetric under reversal, the mass term gaps everything" (R10_result.md)** is wrong: the doublers acquire mass θ, identical to the physical species.
6. **r10c's dispersion comparison formula is wrong** (2√(θ²+k²) instead of √(θ²+4k²)); the adopted walk's result is not written up.
7. **"Massless, the commutant is two-dimensional, 1 and γ₅ … The mass term reduces it to one" (NUCLEUS.md line 25, 48):** after adopting the alternation flip rate, the massless walk already has a β term at k ≠ 0; γ₅ is not a symmetry of the adopted walk even at θ = 0. "Mass alone reduces the two chiral U(1)s to one" is no longer true of the walk the documents adopt.
8. **"Cost: a preferred foliation, which must be the one the gravity sector already carries" (NUCLEUS.md; "the gravity sector's preferred frame" in the plain-language doc).** SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md and its record contain no foliation, khronon or preferred frame; they assert Lovelock → Einstein–Hilbert, Schwarzschild exterior, and "no prediction that differs from GR." A preferred foliation generically gives nonzero PPN α₁, α₂. The claimed cost is either unpaid or contradicts the gravity document.
9. **"Selection is on engagement, not on renewal"** is undefined under the repo's own table: Renewal is defined as "a commitment that *engages* adjacent free possibility." No separate notion of engagement is given, so the distinction that keeps a flying C₇₀ coherent for 10²⁴ renewals is a word, not a rule.
10. **"the Welch bound"** for "at most d mutually orthogonal vectors in dimension d" is a misattribution of elementary linear algebra. **"U(1) × U(2) × U(3), three abelian factors"**: U(2), U(3) are not abelian; the intended statement is three U(1) factors.
11. **"the gluon field is the ratio on kinships, as the which-way ratio on relations is the electromagnetic field":** a ratio (one phase per relation) is abelian; SU(3) needs a 3 × 3 unitary per link, as the repo's own record said when excluding the three-lines candidate.
12. **"A free quark would falsify this"; "Falsifier: a stable SU(N ≥ 4) gauge symmetry":** both are already known not to occur in nature and neither follows from CO (the Clifford-four argument says nothing about whether a separate tensor factor could be SU(4)). These are not falsifiers.
13. Minor: the lifetime 10⁻¹⁵ s is quoted without its ε = 0.5 dependence; `kinship_stretch_cost.py` does not compute the number it is cited for.

## Established-physics check

- **Nielsen–Ninomiya.** Correctly stated as requiring translation invariance (also locality, Hermiticity, exact chiral charge). Dropping translation invariance does void it, and the R8 outcome agrees with the literature that random-lattice fermions (Christ–Friedberg–Lee 1982) do not straightforwardly yield one species (Espriu–Gross–Rakow–Wheater 1986; Perantonis–Wheater 1988). Nothing new.
- **Wilson fermions.** The alternation flip rate θ + rΣ(1 − cos k_j) is the Wilson term; in exponentiated (unitary) form it is periodic, which the repo misses (Error 3). The standard consequence — explicit chiral-symmetry breaking — is not mentioned, and it undercuts the chiral-U(1) statements (Error 7).
- **Dirac quantum walks.** Gupta–Short (PRA 114, 012208, 2026) place the 3+1D conventional walk's doublers at p_j = ±π/2 — the repo's "eight γ₅ modes" — and separately identify quasi-energy-π pseudo-doublers; Bakircioglu–Arnault–Arrighi (arXiv:2505.07900) demonstrate doubling in QCAs and fix it by a covering map with flavour staggering, coexisting with NN. The repo's "discovery" that the eight modes belong to the sequential split-step and its "retirement" of them by Strang symmetrisation is a reinterpretation of a known feature using a standard numerical device (Strang 1968); Gupta–Short's stay-put family shows the alternation term is not "needed" but one of several sufficient choices. Bialynicki-Birula (1994) is cited correctly for the continuum limit.
- **Bell.** The options listed (nonlocal single event with foliation; superdeterminism; many-worlds) omit relativistic flash-GRW (Tumulka 2006), which has a flash ontology closely analogous to "a commitment belongs to the pattern" *without* a preferred foliation — so the asserted cost is not forced — and Bohmian foliations extracted from the wavefunction (Dürr et al. 2014). "A commitment belongs to the pattern, not the place" is the standard nonlocal-collapse position; it does not answer Bell, it selects the collapse branch and renames the measurement problem, as the record itself concedes.
- **Collapse bounds.** The visibility arithmetic is right; comparing one parameter against molecule interferometry with no independent handle on δ (MATTER.md: "δ has no observational handle of its own") is a consistency check five orders deep, not a test. CSL/GRW bounds from Fein et al. (2019) and Arndt et al. (1999) are the analogous standard analyses.
- **Constituent-quark scaling.** NCQ scaling of v₂ (Molnar–Voloshin 2003; STAR/PHENIX) is interpreted via quark coalescence, but is approximate, is violated at the 20% level in Pb–Pb at the LHC, and is partly reproduced by hydrodynamic mass ordering plus hadronic rescattering. Using a *preliminary* O–O result as the thing that "fixes what a third primitive must deliver," while ignoring deep-inelastic scattering, Bjorken scaling and three-jet events as the evidence for three separable carriers, inverts the weight of evidence.
- **String tension.** √σ ≈ 0.42 GeV ≈ Λ_QCD ≈ constituent mass is one-scale dimensional analysis in QCD and appears in flux-tube and string models (Isgur–Paton; Regge slope α′ = 1/2πσ). σ = m²/ħc with m = m_p/3 is that dimensional analysis with a tuned input; the factor two is the usual gap.
- **Known results restated:** all of (i), (vi), the Wilson mechanism, Strang symmetrisation, U(N) from N conserved channels, SO(3) ⊂ SU(3) not a factor, √σ ~ m_constituent. **Wrong:** Errors 1–9. **New:** nothing of substance; the Q1b withdrawal of L_z as a screw artefact of the sequential order is a correct and useful internal correction.

## Logic and grading

- **Do the rulings follow from the primitives?** No. "Hops in no preferred order" is justified by "nothing in the primitives ranks the three lines," but a cyclic order is not a ranking either, a palindrome with x outermost is still an ordering, and the genuinely order-free (simultaneous) walk is non-local. The alternation flip rate has no primitive behind it; MATTER.md admits both are "rulings … not derivations," then NUCLEUS.md calls the item "closed" and R10 grades both "needed." "Needed" is wrong on the repo's own definition: alternatives (Gupta–Short) exist, and the ruling as parametrised fails (Error 1).
- **Grading consistency.** "Ruled" is used for a free choice (flip rate), for a theorem (Schur, chirality-blindness) and for an experimental reading (sorts "excluded" from NCQ scaling). "Excluded" is used for a computed negative (R8, damping) and for an interpretive argument (sorts). "Closed" is used where the record says "pending the author's reading." The scheme is not applied consistently.
- **Bell.** Renames the measurement problem: "engagement" is left undefined relative to "renewal," which already engages. The one quantitative addition (the visibility bound) tests nothing.
- **Three tiles.** Adds no content beyond QCD relabelled: tile = constituent quark, kinship = colour string, "completes with whatever kin are near" = deconfinement, "colour change is a slide" = gauge freedom, σ = m² = dimensional analysis. The one structural statement (gluon field as "ratio") is wrong (Error 11). The document says this itself ("the standard account's ledger in CO's words").
- **Does the two-primitive thesis survive?** Not as stated. The section introduces a new kind of object (a tile, which "is not a pattern and does not renew alone"), a new kind of relation (kinship) that violates the stated principle that sliding costs nothing, a new completion rule, and a new energy-per-length costing. Calling these "a ruling on what possibility in transit can be" rather than a primitive is nomenclature; the bar the document sets for a third primitive (cascade) is bypassed by renaming rather than met.

## Overall grades

- **NUCLEUS.md: D+.** Correct textbook arguments (colour not spatial; Clifford four carries only U(1)), candid hedging in places, but the doubler closure is false at the tested parameters, the Bell "cost already paid" refers to a foliation absent from the gravity document, the three-tile section is QCD relabelled with one structurally wrong statement, and the falsifiers are vacuous.
- **MATTER.md doubler paragraph: D.** Describes a walk whose spectrum does not match the one adopted (4 vs 32 massless zero modes), claims "one species" where six near-degenerate species exist at the tested parameters, omits the periodicity of the unitary Wilson term, the π-sector, and the chiral-symmetry breaking; the content that is right is standard lattice physics.
- **Runs R8–R10: C−.** Scripts are short, honest about parameters, and reproduce exactly. R8's conclusion is directionally right with a weak method. R9's arithmetic is right. R10's headline conclusion is a threshold artefact: a 0.08 cut was applied where the modes of interest sit at 0.14–0.34, the "with mass" column measures nothing, and the adopted ordering's own run (r10c) is unwritten and checked against the wrong formula.

## Recommendation

The material is not publishable as a result and should not be read as closing anything. The quantitative core — that a symmetrised Dirac walk with a unitary Wilson angle has a single species — is contradicted by the walk's own band structure at the parameters tested, and the correct version of the statement (one light species plus cutoff-mass doublers, for r in a window, with uncounted quasi-energy-π modes) is the standard Wilson-fermion statement rather than a finding. The remaining sections restate known physics in new vocabulary, which the author partly acknowledges, but the acknowledgments sit beside words like "closed," "needed," "faced" and "falsifier" that claim more than the record supports, and beside a cross-sector cost (a preferred foliation) that the gravity document neither states nor could accommodate without giving up its "no deviation from GR" claim. The honest working record and reproducible scripts are the repository's strongest feature; the public documents overstate what they contain.
