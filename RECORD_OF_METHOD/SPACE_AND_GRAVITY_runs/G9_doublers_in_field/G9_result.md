# G9 — the walk's extra species in a field (13 Nov 2026)

**Question.** Under P7f, does every species of CO's walk respond to the field alike, its rest energy scaling with the clock (× N) and ignoring the stretch?

**Result** (g9_doublers.py, L1b's 3D walk, θ = 0.05, w = 0.3; g9_doublers.out). The light species (zone centre) behaves exactly: its rest energy scales by N and ignores the stretch. The extra species (zone corners) do not. For a 1% slower clock their rest energies change by +10.8% (one corner), +10.5% (two), −13.5% (three); for a 1% stretch by +10.6%, +10.4%, −13.4%. Equivalence needs −1% and 0.

**Why.** The extra species' masses come from the Wilson flip, and P7f uses the Wilson flip to set light's speed. A 1% change in light's speed moves w by about 0.03, which moves the extra species' masses about ten times more than the clock's 1%. One knob cannot carry both jobs. Setting w ∝ N would scale the extra species correctly but speed light up where clocks slow. The walk's other local knobs (the mass flip, an on-site phase, the directed hop) do not change light's speed without changing its mass or tilting its cone.

**The literal clock rule (slow places skip ticks) is not available as stated.** A hop moves amplitude between two places; if one place pauses while its neighbour steps, two amplitudes land in one slot and the step is no longer unitary. A pause has to be shared by the places a hop joins, which is a change to the walk's hops, not a local skip.

**Reading.** With P7f, the walk's extra species (predicted particles above 10¹⁴ GeV, OPEN_DIRECTIONS "Doublers") violate the equivalence principle at order one: some are pushed away from masses about ten times as strongly as matter is pulled, others pulled about fourteen times as strongly, and the stretch pushes them all. The shift (G7) also drags them the opposite way. The light species is unaffected. So either the extra species are not particles (a cancellation, or an account of them), or the field must reach the walk by a rule that scales every band with the clock. Grade: reading.
