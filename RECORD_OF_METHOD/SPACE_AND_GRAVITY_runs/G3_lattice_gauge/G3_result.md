# G3 result (held for the author) — no backdrop on the lattice carriers

Registered first ([`REGISTRATION.md`](REGISTRATION.md)); variant B and the control were added to it before they ran.

| placement of the carriers | exactly invariant costs (nearest-neighbour range) | long-wavelength limit |
|---|---|---|
| A: as P7 now has it (lapse and shear at places; stretch and one-way part on links) | 0 | — |
| B: shear moved to the faces | 0 | — |
| staggered: lapse and stretch at places; one-way part and place displacement on links; shear on the faces | 11 | 10 reduce to G1's family (residual ≤ 3 × 10⁻⁶); 1 vanishes at leading order (a pure lattice-scale term) |

Method: the most general quadratic cost with two lattice difference symbols times even cosine factors up to second degree (3850 terms), invariant under the cube's symmetries, time reversal, and the lattice gauge changes of each placement; counted by sampling; costs that vanish identically removed with an absolute threshold. The method was validated on the staggered placement, where invariants were expected.

**Reading.** As CO's carriers are placed now, "no backdrop" (moving places, reparametrising time) has no exactly invariant cost on the lattice within nearest-neighbour range; G1's result holds there only as a long-wavelength approximation. On the staggered placement it holds exactly on the lattice, and every invariant cost reduces to linearised Einstein–Hilbert (up to strength and speed) at long wavelength, isotropically. So exact no-backdrop on the lattice decides where each carrier lives: diagonal stretch and lapse at places, direction-reversing quantities (the one-way part, the displacement of places) on relations, shear on the squares between them.

**Not covered.** Longer-range costs for placements A and B (a cost of wider reach might exist); discrete time; the nonlinear rule.
