# How CO is measured

CO is incomplete by design. It is measured as a research programme in progress, not as a finished theory.

## 1. Progressive or degenerating (Lakatos)

Every addition (a posit, a re-ruling, a new reading) is judged by what it does after it is added:

| verdict | test |
|---|---|
| **progressive** | it explains more than it was added for: a consequence nobody asked it for, a second result it carries (consilience), a posit it removes, or a new contact with data |
| **neutral** | it repairs a contradiction or states clearly what was already meant, and adds nothing else |
| **accommodating** | it was added to fit a known result or close a gap, and so far does only that |
| **excluded** | it failed a test; the failure is kept, with its reason |

An accommodating addition is not a failure; it is a debt. It turns progressive when something it was not added for follows from it. A run of accommodating additions in one sector is the warning sign: that sector is being patched, not climbed.

## 2. The ladder table

Kept in [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md), one line per domain, updated when a rung is climbed or lost.

## 3. What this measure is not

It is not "is this worth a physicist's time?", a question for a finished theory. Outside readers are used for specific checks (an argument, a number, a claim's strength), not as a score.
