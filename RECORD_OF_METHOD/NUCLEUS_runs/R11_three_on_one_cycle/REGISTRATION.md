# R11 — one pattern three times on one cycle: does a three-fold structure arise, and does it do anything?

**Question.** Three copies of the Dirac walk sharing one flip cycle. Does a three-fold symmetry arise from the sharing (not posited), what charge does it define, and do the charge sectors differ dynamically (a cost for mismatch between copies)?

**Construction.** Internal space C⁴ ⊗ C³ (spinor ⊗ copy). Hops act on the spinor, identically on each copy. The shared flip passes the cycle around the copies: M = exp(−iθ β ⊗ T), T = (S + S†)/2 with S the cyclic shift of the three copies (the Hermitian form of "the flip moves to the next copy"). This is the one ruling-shaped choice in the construction and is flagged as such; without a shared flip the copies are independent and the symmetry is the trivial S₃.

**Expected (known by construction).** S commutes with the step, so the thirds-shift is a symmetry; its eigenvalues ω^k, k = 0, 1, 2, define a Z₃ charge (triality). **Unknown:** the dispersion in each sector, whether the sectors separate in mass, and whether any sector fails to propagate (which would be confinement from this alone).

**Outcome changes a ruling:** if the k ≠ 0 sectors propagate freely, the moiré shape gives the three and the centre but no confinement; if they do not, it gives more.
