**Derivation: what a relation must carry, and what its mismatch costs.**

1. *A ratio will not do.* Write ℓ_ij = ln R_ij. A ratio reverses with direction, ℓ_ji = −ℓ_ij: a 1-form on the network. With rest value ℓ = dφ (φ = ln P), the mismatch m = ℓ − dφ splits into a gradient part and a loop part. Ratio hopping with inertia gives, linearised,

Coupling neighbouring loops gives at best a curl-of-curl theory: Maxwell's, spin-1. A loop product ≠ 1 is a holonomy, not a curvature. Vector waves are excluded by LIGO–Virgo polarization and give dipole, not quadrupole, radiation. So the relation's number must be **symmetric** in direction: an occupancy, a length. Lengths along the directions a place has determine a quadratic form per cell, g_ij, and that is spin-2's home. ## The balance

Strict local hold-and-release gives no field outside matter: a renewal engages at its adjacency and releases at the same adjacency, net zero per cycle. A clock a metre above the ground would run at full rate. So matter must be a **net sink** of free possibility. A steady point sink into a conserved, diffusing stock gives

$$\nabla^2 P_{\text{free}}=\sigma\,\delta(\mathbf r)\ \Rightarrow\ P_{\text{free}}(r)=P_\infty-\frac{k}{r},\qquad k\propto\sigma ,$$

Newton's potential, linear and unscreened, from conservation alone. The question is what makes matter a sink. (At second order the difference-linear form fails; see Perihelion. The field is linear in ln P_free, which agrees at first order.)

**Pooling, in one sentence.** A renewal keeps a share of what is there: it retains δħ·(P_free/P_max) as the body's record and releases the rest. (Ruled in this form; the earlier form, a fixed δħ, is superseded. See Today's expansion for why.)

**The retained fraction is the record.** Of the three readings of "the rest," *destroyed* breaks conservation and leaves no ceiling (the engaged stock would not grow, so h could not reach 1); *leaves* is circulation under another name. *Stays engaged* keeps both, and says what a record is made of: possibility withdrawn from the free stock and held. "It is kept" means "it is still engaged." The hole is a body that is all record and no renewal. The count rising and the free stock falling are one process; the record only grows because δ > 0.

**What pooling costs.** δ is Space and Gravity's first constant that is not ħ and not borrowed, the same for every renewal. Sink strength is

$$\sigma=\delta\,mc^2\,\frac{P_{\text{free}}}{P_{\max}} ,$$

so in the weak field G ∝ δ/P_max, with no dependence on the floor P∞. In the strong field a body's sink is screened by its own depletion, (1 − h_self); linear and unscreened to first order, different from GR beyond it, untested.

$$\dot P_{\text{free}}=-\dot P_{\text{engaged}}=-\delta\sum_i m_ic^2 .$$

- A uniform fall in P∞ makes no gradient: no force, and **not Λ**. Λ is not sourced by the floor.
- With pooling as a share of what is there, the deficit around a body scales with the floor, so h(r) = (k/r)/P∞ does not depend on P∞. **G is constant.** The floor is unobservable: neither pooling nor any dilution of free possibility shows in G or in clock ratios. - With no infinities the free stock is finite, so the record has a finite capacity and the process has an end: everything engaged.

**Second leg.** "Possibility moves toward engagement" is a flow statement and needs the sink. If the sink failed, attraction would still follow from a static gradient: patterns hop on free possibility, hop slower where it is scarce, and so linger near matter. Not needed under pooling; noted.

## 2026-11-11 — the walk on a grown network, read against the quantum-walk literature (reading; a third carrier for shear)

## 2026-11-13 — gravity's lattice debts, a first reading (E: the nonlinear cost on the carriers; D: the walk on a curved network)

**E. The nonlinear cost on CO's carriers.**

*What CO has.* The carriers are staggered (G3): places carry the held share h (the lapse) and stretch; relations carry the one-way part (the shift) and displacement (edge lengths); squares carry shear (the angles between edges). In the continuum, the nonlinear cost is Einstein–Hilbert plus Λ, by consistent deformation (given its assumptions).

*The natural lattice form.* Regge calculus puts curvature on the codimension-two hinges as deficit angles, with cost S = Σ_hinges A_h ε_h (area times deficit angle). In 3+1 the hinges are the 2-faces: CO's spatial squares, and edges swept in time (timelike rectangles). (In a 3D spatial slice alone the hinges would be the edges.) Two matches, with their limits:
- The squares are hinges. Their deficit angles come from the 4D dihedral angles of the cells around them, though, not from the squares' own shear.
- A cubic cell is not rigid from its edge lengths alone; it also needs its face angles, which are the shear on the squares. So CO's carrier set (edge displacements plus square shears) fixes the cells' shapes as parallelepipeds. The hypercuboids of Bahr and Steinhaus are right-angled and flat (curvature needs frusta, Bahr, Rabuffo and Steinhaus), so CO's parallelepiped cells go beyond the existing cuboidal models.
Candidate form: the nonlinear cost is Regge's, Σ_squares A ε, on CO's carriers, reducing to the linear cost G1 already has at weak field. This is a reading, not checked.

*The known obstacle.* In four dimensions the Regge action breaks the continuum's diffeomorphism symmetry, which is CO's "no backdrop", on curved solutions. The breaking is quadratic in the curvature (Bahr and Dittrich 2009, (Broken) gauge symmetries and constraints in Regge calculus, arXiv:0905.1670). Discrete actions with exact symmetry ("perfect actions") are known in three dimensions, with or without Λ, not in four. So on the lattice, P7e's no backdrop at every order would hold up to corrections of relative order (ℓ²R)²: the same footing as P7e already has for interacting matter ("up to lattice corrections"). It matters only where curvature reaches the hop scale, at the singularities, which CO's "no infinities" already says it must treat differently.

*Grade.* Shape: a Regge-type cost on CO's squares, its no backdrop broken at order (ℓ²R)², invisible short of hop-scale curvature. Owed: writing the cubic Regge action in CO's carriers and checking that it reproduces G1's linear cost (a computation, not registered: the linear limit is expected).

with each piece read from a carrier:
