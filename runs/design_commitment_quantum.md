## Note I (design note): when does a pattern commit? CO's quantum sector (calculation outline)

*26 October 2026.*
- *This note sets out:*
  - *what CO says about quantum mechanics now;*
  - *what the earlier papers' state-reduction work did, and what a reading of it against established physics found;*
  - *what any commitment rule must satisfy, with today's measured bounds;*
  - *the candidate rules CO's primitives suggest, each with the one scale it uses;*
  - *the tests that would kill each.*
- *It is an outline. No engine run was made. The estimates are order of magnitude; each is marked as such and is to be redone as a registered calculation before anything is built on it.*

**1. Why.**
- **The gravity sector is near a natural stopping point.**
  - Runs 85–90 showed that Note H's carrier can hold GR's constraint structure through order a² in the kinetic sector, and that this forces relativistic waves.
  - Run 91 (the potential sector) is the remaining consistency test. Those results test a GR-shaped carrier: they cannot give CO an output that was not put in.
- **The quantum sector is where CO says something of its own.** QM is what happens before ticking, and a pattern stays a pattern until it commits. That is an objective commitment criterion.
- **The field it belongs to has sharp, current experiments:**
  - matter-wave interference of 170 kDa clusters;
  - underground spontaneous-radiation searches;
  - LISA Pathfinder's noise floor.

  So a criterion with a number in it is testable now.
- **The target, stated before any work:** derive the commitment threshold from the primitives as a number, and say which scale it shares with gravity (p_s, P∞, the hop, ε). One parameter fixed in two domains is the strongest form of consilience the ontology can offer.

**2. What CO says now.**
- **Quantum mechanics is the study of what happens before ticking** (stated 23 October; ONTOLOGY §1).
  - Patterns of possibility have not committed.
  - A pattern keeps no clock of its own: the time that elapses in its phase, mc²τ/ħ, belongs to the committed world around it.
  - A measurement is a pattern meeting the committed world and being forced to commit to one channel.
- **The commitment trigger** (stated 25 October): a pattern stays a pattern until it commits, either
  - **(a)** by interacting with the committed world, or
  - **(b)** by reaching an internal complexity at which it has to commit.

  Gravity-collapse models that rest on spacetime fluctuations without an account of what spacetime is are not the route (stated 25 October).
- **Patterns** hop on their place's ticks and need free capacity there (R6). Massive patterns obey
$$
-N^4\nabla^2\psi+m^2N^2\psi=\omega^2\psi .
$$
- **Entanglement:** a dissolved relation goes back to possibility as a two-part pattern (stated October).
- **The vacuum never commits, and clocks are matter** (rulings of 15 October). Matter is commitment that keeps re-making itself.
- **Commitment already appears in the record twice:**
  - in R11, where infalling matter commits where the possibility per place falls to 2p_s;
  - in Run 74, where δu, how long commitment takes as seen from far away, is not fixed by the rules.
- **The scales, all from data:**
  - p_s ≳ 4×10⁵ units per place (Run 24);
  - P∞/p_s ≳ 1.6–1.9×10⁵ (Run 66), so P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰ units per place and ε = p_s/P∞ ≲ 6.3×10⁻⁶;
  - the hop, about 10^2.5–10^3 ℓ_P ≈ 5×10⁻³³–1.6×10⁻³² m (the engine; an open tension with ℓ_P).

**3. The earlier papers' state-reduction work, and what its reading found** (reading of 25 October; unreviewed).
- **What it did.**
  - It placed the earlier ontology beside Diósi and Penrose's gravity-related collapse.
  - It argued that the arrow, gravity's time and the pointer basis are one primitive.
  - It reproduced τ ∝ ħ/E_G as a scaling, with E_G identified with Penrose's self-energy rather than computed.
  - It argued that a coarse, environment-set channel basis escapes the 2020 radiation bound.
- **Five points, all of which carry over to CO:**
  1. **A branch-clock-rate mechanism conflicts with interferometry.** If two branches running at different clock rates must commit once their relative action reaches ħ, then every interferometer with a height difference should decohere. They don't:
     - neutrons keep tens of radians of relative phase m g Δz T/ħ (Colella, Overhauser and Werner 1975);
     - atoms keep millions;
     - optical-clock superpositions keep ΔE t/ħ ~ 10¹⁵ rad.

     **CO's rule must not be "different clock rates force commitment".** The mainstream effect of that kind is time-dilation decoherence of internal degrees of freedom (Pikovski et al. 2015), which needs no new physics and has a different scaling.
  2. **A well-defined preferred time removes Penrose's reason for collapse.** His argument needs the absence of one. So CO's commitment must come from its own mechanism, not from supplying Penrose's missing time.
  3. **Immunity from the radiation bounds needs channels that commute with the Hamiltonian.** For a dissipative channel with jump operators Ĵ_α = g_αΠ̂_α,
$$
\frac{d\langle \hat H\rangle}{dt}=-\tfrac12\sum_\alpha g_\alpha^2\,\big\langle[\hat\Pi_\alpha,[\hat\Pi_\alpha,\hat H]]\big\rangle ,
$$
     which vanishes for every state only if [Π̂_α, Ĥ] = 0. The scale that sets the threshold and the scale that sets the channels' coarseness must be one number.
  4. **Bell's theorem.** A commitment that is local in Bell's sense gives CHSH ≤ 2, and experiment gives 2√2. **The nonlocality has to live in the pre-commitment pattern.** No-signalling alone is not an advantage, since GRW and CSL have it too.
  5. **Renaming is not computing.** Writing E_Δ = E₁₁ + E₂₂ − 2E₁₂ as "Str − Coh" names the difference self-energy; it does not compute it.

**5. Commitment, operationally.**
- **Route (a), meeting the committed world.**
  - When a pattern interacts with matter (which is commitment), the interaction leaves a record.
  - Standard decoherence theory describes the same event: the environment acquires which-path information.
  - So route (a) must reproduce Q5's rates. If it does, it adds an objective endpoint (a record is kept) but **no new number**. That is a consistency requirement and a relabelling risk, not a prediction.
- **Route (b), internal complexity.** This is the only place a new number can come from. An isolated pattern, with nothing committed around it, commits on its own.
- **Which channels: a CO-native argument for the energy basis.**
  - A commitment is a tick, so what a commitment records is a count of ticks: a phase advance, which is energy (§2: the phase's elapsed time belongs to the committed world).
  - If commitment records only that, its channel projectors are energy projectors, [Π̂_α, Ĥ] = 0. The heating rate of §3, point 3, then vanishes identically, and Q2 is met by construction.
  - Position localisation would then come only from route (a): a detector whose own energy states are correlated with position.
  - **To be shown, not assumed.** In particular, a centre-of-mass superposition of a free particle with equal energies in both branches would never commit by route (b).

**6. Candidate readings of "internal complexity", each with its one scale.**

*All estimates are order of magnitude.*
- **(i) Possibility load per place: commitment when a pattern's own draw lowers the possibility at its places by about p_s.**
  - With R2's presence clock, δP/P∞ = |Φ|/c², so the condition is
$$
\frac{Gm}{c^2R}\gtrsim\varepsilon\lesssim6\times10^{-6}.
$$
  - For the 170 kDa clusters, Gm/(c²R) ≈ 5×10⁻⁴¹, so nothing commits in any laboratory. (For comparison, the Sun's surface value is 2×10⁻⁶.)
  - This reading is R11's core rule applied to patterns: one rule for black-hole cores and for patterns, which is a unification within CO.
  - In the laboratory it predicts standard QM: consistent, not distinctive.
- **(ii) A fixed count of displaced possibility.**
  - Commitment would come once the two branches differ by n units of possibility in total.
  - For the 170 kDa clusters, displaced by 100 nm, with the hop at 10³ ℓ_P, the difference is about
$$
P_\infty\frac{Gm}{c^2}\,\frac{2\pi\,\Delta x^2}{\ell^3}\sim10^{44}\ \text{units}.
$$
  - Any natural n (1, or a few) commits every superposition at once, and Q1 excludes that.
  - A large n would be put in by hand. This reading is excluded unless the count is fixed by something else.
- **(iii) A Diósi–Penrose rule with CO's draw, regularised at CO's own short scale.**
  - CO's draw is Newtonian at weak field, so the branches' difference self-energy is Penrose's E_G.
  - CO's only native short length is the hop, about 10⁻³² m, more than twenty orders of magnitude below the 2020 bound R₀ > 0.54×10⁻¹⁰ m. **Excluded.**
  - A coarse regulator would need a second, environment-set scale, which is not CO-native.
- **(iv) A mass threshold from CO's scales.** CO's dimensionless scales combine with the Planck mass m_P = 1.31×10¹⁹ Da into mesoscopic masses:

| combination | value (from the gravity bounds) |
|---|---|
| m_P / P∞ | ≲ 2.0×10⁸ Da |
| m_P / p_s | ≲ 3.3×10¹³ Da |
| m_P ε | ≲ 8.2×10¹³ Da |
| m_P / 10³ (the hop) | ≈ 1.3×10¹⁶ Da |

  - These sit between today's interference record (1.7×10⁵ Da) and the range of levitated nanoparticles and proposed space tests (about 10⁸–10¹¹ Da), where superposition experiments are being prepared.
  - **This is dimensional analysis, not a derivation.** It becomes a prediction only if a rule derived from the primitives produces one of these combinations.

**7. The consilience target.** Suppose a derived rule gave m_c = m_P/P∞, with patterns heavier than m_c committing by route (b).
- **Quantum side.** Q1 needs m_c > 1.7×10⁵ Da, so P∞ < 7.6×10¹³ units per place.
- **Gravity side.** The pulsar triple and clock agreement give P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰ (Runs 24, 66).
- **The window.** 6.4×10¹⁰ ≲ P∞ ≲ 7.6×10¹³: three decades, with one parameter bounded from two domains.
- **Planned experiments decide it.** Interference at 10⁸ Da would push the upper bound to 1.3×10¹¹, against the lower bound of 6.4×10¹⁰. Either a commitment is seen near that mass, or this reading is excluded.
- **And ε follows.** With P∞ fixed, ε = p_s/P∞ fixes the strong-equivalence violation Δ = εC/(1 + ε) (ONTOLOGY §5, item 3), and the neutron-star prediction becomes a number.

This is the shape of an output that was not put in. The rule that gives m_c has to come first.

**8. The tests, in order (each to be registered with a kill rule).**
- **I1. Route (a) as decoherence** (a calculation).
  - Model a pattern meeting committed matter in CO's terms (gas collisions, thermal photons).
  - Derive the coherence loss, and compare it with standard collisional decoherence (Q5).
  - **Kill:** a rate that misses the measured one.
- **I2. What a commitment records.**
  - Derive from the primitives whether a commitment's channels are energy projectors, i.e. counts of ticks.
  - If they are, the heating rate in §3 vanishes. If they are not, compute the centre-of-mass heating for the Gran Sasso germanium and for LISA Pathfinder.
  - **Kill:** heating above Q2's bounds.
- **I3. Bell from a dissolved relation** (an engine run).
  - Model a two-part pattern from one dissolved relation, committed at two distant places.
  - Show CHSH = 2√2, with each side's marginals independent of the other's setting.
  - **Kill:** CHSH ≤ 2, or signalling.
- **I4. The threshold** (a calculation).
  - From the hold rule (a commitment holds while a fixed amount of possibility is present at its places) and the pattern rule, derive when an isolated pattern must commit.
  - Register which combination of scales it gives before computing its value.
  - **Kill:** a threshold below 1.7×10⁵ Da (Q1); a rule that decoheres clock-rate differences (Q6); or heating above Q2.

**9. What would count as remarkable, and what would not.**
- **Remarkable:**
  - a threshold mass, or a rate, derived from the primitives that lands inside today's window and shares P∞ (or p_s, or the hop) with gravity;
  - equally, a CO-native reason why the threshold is the core rule (i), with a second consequence that standard QM lacks.
- **Not remarkable:**
  - route (a) reproducing decoherence;
  - energy-basis immunity, which standard QM shares;
  - E_G re-expressed in CO's vocabulary;
  - a threshold whose scale is chosen to fit.

**10. Open questions carried from 25 October.**
- What "internal complexity" means in CO's terms. The 170 kDa clusters' 7,000 hot atoms do not commit their centre of mass, so it is not a count of constituents.
- Whether one scale sets both the threshold and the channels' coarseness.
- Whether the channels are compatible with energy, so that commitment does not jostle charges and trip the spontaneous-radiation bounds.
- How the nonlocality lives in the pre-commitment pattern.
- What fixes δu (Run 74), and whether it is the same scale.
