# Tests: how CO could fail

These are consistency windows and kill tests, not derived predictions. Only claims with a window and a kill test are listed. None is a number CO derives: each rests on CO's rules plus bounds from data.

## 1. Route (b)'s collapse window
A solid's gravity is laid down only by its matter's own random commitments: gravity sourced by collapse events, of the Tilloy type. The rate and coarseness must lie in
$$
5.6\times10^{-36}<\kappa_b<\min\!\big(0.948\,r_C^5,\ 7.3\times10^{-19}r_C^2,\ 7.6\times10^{-20}r_C^2\big),\qquad 0.09\ \mu\text{m}\lesssim r_C\lesssim 51\ \mu\text{m}
$$
(r_C in metres). The terms come from:
- the floor: LISA Pathfinder's noise, which the test masses' own deposits must not exceed;
- the top: SNO, intergalactic-gas heating, and relic-neutrino heating (from 9.7 μm);
- the cap: Lee et al.'s 52 μm inverse-square test.

It rests on GRW-type symmetrisation; without it the window closes. The per-nucleon rate is λ = κ_b m_N c²/ħ ≥ 8×10⁻¹² s⁻¹.

**Kill tests.**
- **An inverse-square test below 50 μm.** The source's mass is smeared at √1.5 r_C, so the signal switches on steeply rather than as a Yukawa tail.

| set-up | reaches r_C at 10⁻³ precision |
|---|---|
| Lee's geometry, 52 μm gap | 30 μm |
| Lee's geometry, 10 μm gap | 16 μm |
| a source pattern 30× finer (38 μm wavelength), 10 μm gap | 3.5 μm |

- **A free test mass about 3.5×10³ quieter than LISA Pathfinder,** which would close the window at its floor.
- **A top edge about 3.5×10⁷ tighter.**

**Not tests.** Levitated-particle and cantilever bounds on CSL do not apply: GRW-type symmetrisation has no coherent N² gain. The best force sensor falls short of route (b)'s heating by about 4×10⁵ in power.

## 2. No gravitationally mediated entanglement
Gravity is laid down only by records, and the medium holds no branches. **Kill test:** two masses entangled through gravity alone (the Bose–Marletto–Vedral class of experiment). The prediction is shared with every collapse-sourced gravity model.

## 3. Saturation's onset map (candidate)
Under the capacity primitive (route (c)), a body commits at the rate c/r_C once its density passes ρ_K. That gives an onset edge in size at laboratory densities: r_C ≳ 0.23–0.44 μm in general, 0.85–1.44 μm near germanium's density, and 2.45–2.79 μm near liquid xenon's. Much of this lies within a factor of a few of present limits.
- **The free number.** The map has one, the medium's crossover p_c, with
$$
8\times10^{4}\lesssim p_c\lesssim 5.6\times10^{27}\,aK^2 .
$$
- **The upper bound** is the interference wall, h_wall = 3/(0.99π)(m_P/M)² aK², which falls with cluster mass M.
- **Kill tests:**
  - X-ray and germanium limits at the densities above;
  - LISA-type acceleration noise;
  - interferometry with heavier clusters.

## 4. Gravity is GR's
With ε = 0, CO's gravity has no departure from GR outside matter: PPN β = γ = 1, no preferred-frame effects, GR's black holes. **Kill test:** any confirmed departure from GR in the solar system, in pulsars or in gravitational waves.

## What CO does not predict
- No value of p_c, r_C or κ_b.
- No dimension.
- No particle mass.
- No coupling.
- Λ is taken from data. CO's one claim about the vacuum is that its energy does not gravitate (R5).
