# Quantum runs

This folder holds the write-up sections of the quantum-sector work: tests of when a pattern of possibility commits, what a commitment records, and how quantum matter draws.

The design note that sets out the requirements, the candidate rules and the tests is Note I, [`../runs/design_commitment_quantum.md`](../runs/design_commitment_quantum.md).

**Standing rulings** (ONTOLOGY §1):
- a commitment is stamped with the local count (26 October);
- a pattern draws only through its records, which come from both routes (27 October):
  - route (a), at the surroundings' resolution;
  - route (b), a random "commit and continue" of every pattern at a rate λ with coarseness r_C;
- the medium holds no branches (27 October).

So gravity cannot entangle masses. A positive test of gravitationally mediated entanglement would refute these rulings.

**Route (b)'s rulings** (27 October, for Run 92):
- it acts on every pattern;
- its rate is per radian of phase, λ = κ_b E/ħ;
- each record deposits the draw E_TK/λ_tot;
- its units are electrons and nucleons;
- radiation's gravity follows the radiation per population;
- after the run, the rate is the operator κ_b Ĥ/ħ, and r_C lies below 1.5×10¹⁸ m.
