## Working note: the line between matter and pattern, and what it does to route (b) (status, 27 October 2026)

*A dated status note, not a ruled reading. It records rulings made on 27 October during Reading I6, and the consequences found by independent checks. The numbers in §3 are unreviewed as a whole: a final independent review and Reading I6's final ruling are pending. The working draft of Reading I6 is in `reading_I6_matter_and_pattern_DRAFT.md`.*

### 1. Rulings made (27 October)

1. **The conditional picture** (Allen: "close to how I imagine CO's quantum to be"). A free body between records is a pattern, its phase turned by the committed world's count (A4). Matter keeps its rhythm, and a record strikes the beat.
2. **A renewal records nothing** (the restated A3′). It keeps no place, no count and no deposit beyond the body's records.
   - Records are placed by their interaction kernel.
   - Route (b) is exempt and localises at r_C.
   - Alternatives fail: a renewal that localised at any scale would heat matter (about 7×10⁴ W per nucleon at 10⁻¹¹ m); one that kept a count would carry which-path information in time; one that deposited gravity would remove the lunar and gas bounds.
3. **The clock chain, option (a).** Run 24's numbers stand as engine results. Their premise for physical clocks, a clock's self-dip, lapses, because renewals carry no deposit. That reopens:
   - p_s ≳ 4×10⁵;
   - gravity's P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰;
   - Note I's lower bound on ε;
   - the hop of about 10^2.5–10³ ℓ_P, and with it P-G-Closure (ℓ_ED = ℓ_P).

   The pulsar triple's P∞/p_s ≳ 1.6×10⁵ stays.
4. **Deposits, option (iii).** A gravity deposit lands only where a record fixes position, at that record's own Born outcome, over its kernel width.
   - Records that fix only internal states, such as how a solid's vibrations are shared, make no deposit.
   - The alternatives fail: drawing the place with an update heats matter; drawing it without one is nonlinear in the state, the (S)/Gisin signalling Reading I0 excluded.
5. **Reading (b) excluded.** A record that fixes a body's position lays down only the struck part's gravity, as T1 implies.
   - A whole-body deposit would put deposits at the far end of a rod at a time the near-end party chooses: (T2)-type timing.
   - It also has no linear placement. A fixed shape template is excluded by the measured gravity of a vibrating beam (Brack et al. 2022).
6. **A body's own route-(b) deposits act on it,** as M3 implies.
7. **A deposit's gravity acts as an impulse at the moment of the record, and a single deposit has no reaction partner** (ruled 28 October, "as recommended"). Both are as the record already has them: Run 92's gravity-from-records phase rule and B9 give an impulse, and the medium holds the draw. So LISA Pathfinder's floor stands at 3.6–5.6×10⁻³⁶.

### 2. What follows

**Solids gravitate through route (b).** A solid's interior atoms are recorded only internally, so their gravity is laid down by route (b) alone, smeared at r_C:
$$
g(d)=\frac{GM}{d^2}\Big[(1-f)+f\,w(d/r_C)\Big],\qquad f=\frac{\lambda_b}{\Gamma_{\rm ext}+\lambda_b}\to1 ,
$$
with w the fraction of a Gaussian of standard deviation √1.5 r_C enclosed. Newton holds only at d ≫ r_C. This is the structure of GRW flash gravity (Tilloy 2018).

Three consequences:

- **The premise that a solid's draw lands at fine resolution lapses.** Runs 92–95 stand as calculations, but these registered readings rest on that premise:
  - **Run 93's B13**, and with it a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴;
  - **Run 92's window:** r_C from 2.3×10⁻¹⁰ to 1.5×10¹⁸ m is now closed above 5×10⁻⁵ m. Its laboratory band (2.6×10⁻⁵⁶ < κ_b < 9×10⁻⁴⁶ at 100 nm) lies below the new floor, and so does every κ_b candidate it placed. B9's "at most 5×10⁻²⁴" is replaced by the floor;
  - **Run 93's reading** "under every ruling to date, route (b) is viable";
  - **Run 94:** the smallest a·P∞, 3.66×10²⁴, falls away. B15 stands as a calculation, but lies wholly in excluded r_C;
  - **Reading I0 ruling 2's corollary,** that the 52 μm tests do not bound r_C. They are now the binding cap.
- **The short-range cap.** The 52 μm inverse-square test (Lee et al. 2020) requires r_C ≲ 5×10⁻⁵ m, uncertain by about ±20% (`checkGamma2/`).

**A lower edge from gravity's graininess** (`checkGamma2/`; re-derived independently in `checkFloor/` and `checkFinal/`). A solid's gravity arrives in deposits of μ = ħ/(κ_b c²). LISA Pathfinder's test masses, including the pull of each mass's own deposits, then require
$$
\kappa_b\ \ge\ \frac{4\hbar J}{c^2M_{\rm TM}^2\,{\rm ASD}^2}\approx3.6\text{–}5.6\times10^{-36},\qquad J=\int\rho_{\rm src}\langle\tilde g_x^2\rangle\,dV .
$$

**Symmetrisation.** Under the ruled GRW-type symmetrisation (`checkSym/`), identical units within r_C share a localisation. Heating, emission and break-up fall by
$$
R=\frac{1}{2n(2\pi r_C^2)^{3/2}}\approx\frac{1}{31.5\,n\,r_C^3},
$$
so the suppression 1/R is about 4×10¹¹ to 10¹⁹ at r_C = 1–100 μm (1.05×10¹⁶ at 10⁻⁵ m in D₂O, 4.1×10¹⁴ in liquid xenon). An exact lattice sum confirms it. So SNO, XENONnT and Earth's heat no longer bind there, and 'indep' (nucleons and electrons) and 'com' (atoms) have the same window. Run 92's note that the labelled bounds overstate "by up to the number of like units within r_C" understates the factor, which is about 7.5 times that number.

**Photon bounds** (`checkXray/`). A hit deflects a photon by σ_α = 1/(√2 r_C k) per axis, and the hits along a path number N = κ_b k_obs χ(z_S), with χ the comoving distance and the rate counted in the cosmic frame (M2).
- With χ in place of cT, HST's B5 becomes κ_b < 5.2×10⁻³⁴.
- X-ray imaging of distant quasars, from halo limits and spectral slopes, closes the window for r_C ≲ 2×10⁻⁵ m. The margin is 10–100× below about 10⁻⁵ m, and about 1 near 2×10⁻⁵ m.

### 3. The current window (reviewed 28 October, `checkFinal/`: "holds with changes")

$$
3.6\text{–}5.6\times10^{-36}\ <\ \kappa_b\ \lesssim\ 6.2\times10^{-36},\qquad 4\times10^{-5}\ \lesssim\ r_C\ \lesssim\ 5\times10^{-5}\ \text{m},
$$
at Chandra's loosest supported criterion, so a·P∞ ≳ 1.6×10³⁵. At the tighter criteria it is closed.

- **The deposit-width convention.** This assumes model92's convention: deposits spread over √1.5 r_C. With (iii)'s "kernel width" read as |L̂|² (total spread r_C), or with centre sampling alone, the short-range cap moves to 63–88 μm, and κ_b may reach 2.5–4.8× the floor. So until the convention is ruled, the result is r_C ≈ 51–88 μm at the cap, at 1–5× the floor.
- **Photons.** Every photon bound assumes route (b)'s rate counts phase in the cosmic frame (M2, open since Run 92). If it counts along the worldline, photons never commit. Then the window widens to r_C ≈ 8×10⁻⁸ to 5×10⁻⁵ m, with 3.6×10⁻³⁶ < κ_b < 2.8×10⁻²⁷, and a·P∞ ≳ 3.5×10²⁶.
- **The floor is conservative.** It counts LISA Pathfinder's whole 1.74 fm s⁻² Hz⁻¹ᐟ² plateau as room for route (b). Armano et al. attribute that plateau to residual-gas noise. If only 1.2 fm were left, the floor would be 7.6×10⁻³⁶, and the window would close under every Chandra criterion.

**The Chandra core criterion, pinned to published data** (`checkXray2/`).
- **The model.** Chandra's point-spread function is modelled as three Gaussians matching its encircled energy (0.733, 0.916, 0.954 and 0.967 at 0.5″, 1″, 2″ and 5″). Hits are counts-weighted over 0.5–2 keV.
- **The data.** Clean z = 3.5–4.6 quasars (Lopez et al. 2006; Bassett et al. 2004) allow an excess of about 0.1 of the soft counts beyond 1″ from statistics alone, and 0.2–0.3 once the model's systematics are included.
- **Below about 4×10⁻⁵ m the strip is closed** under every one of those criteria (by 2–7× at 2×10⁻⁵ m).
- **Near the cap, 4–5×10⁻⁵ m, it is undecided.**
  - An excess of 0.1 closes it, by 1.6–2.8×.
  - An excess of 0.2 leaves a sliver of 1.1–1.3×.
  - An excess of 0.3 leaves it open from about 3.5×10⁻⁵ m. Even then κ_b is capped at 6.2×10⁻³⁶ at the cap, so a·P∞ ≳ 1.6×10³⁵.
- **What would decide it.** At the floor, route (b) would at least double the soft-band core of every z ≈ 4 quasar, adding a symmetric blur of 0.4–0.7″ at the cap. That resembles Chandra's own aspect-blur correction. The test that would decide it compares high-redshift quasars with nearby stars on the same detector, epoch and band, and it has not been published.
- **CMB up-conversion does not bite in the strip.** It reaches only about 10⁻³ of the far-infrared background there, and stays below the FIRAS spectral-distortion limits. The earlier estimate of it was about 60–100× too high.
- **The LISA floor's dependence** (`checkFloor/`). The floor, 3.6–5.6×10⁻³⁶, is confirmed under the impulse model that Run 92 uses. If a deposit's field persisted instead, there would be no floor at all. Without the self term (that is, with action–reaction per deposit), it would be about 1–2×10⁻³⁶. Both are to be ruled.

### 4. Open

- **Ruled 28 October:**
  - M3's λ_tot counts only records that deposit ("yes"). Without that, an interior unit's mean draw would fall by λ_b/λ_int ≈ 10⁻²⁴ and solids would lose their gravity;
  - the deposit-width convention is model92's, √1.5 r_C, as every registered run uses ("keep model92's"). The alternatives (cap 63–88 μm, up to 5× the floor) are reported alongside.
- **Under trial, not ruled:** M2's frame for photons. Allen, 28 October: "let's try along its worldline first". Read that way, route (b)'s rate counts phase along a pattern's own worldline, mc²τ/ħ (A4), which is zero for light. Photons would then commit only through route (a), and the photon bounds would vanish. What the trial does to everything else was checked (`checkWorldline/`; unreviewed):
  - **The record is split.** A4, R6 and Run 16 support it: under R6 the phase along the group path gains mN/γ per tick, which is mc²dτ/ħ. M2 ("ω in the cosmic rest frame") and Reading I4's (S-i) derivation contradict it. I4 would need its stock counted by rest energy and its hold summed along the worldline.
  - **Cosmology does not exclude it.** After decoupling, CMB photons are still recorded by Thomson scattering on leftover electrons and, later, on reionised gas. Under M3, with λ_tot read as the instantaneous rate of depositing records, the population's mean draw stays exact. A reading in which photons' gravity switched off after last scattering would be excluded (θ* off by about 9σ, early ISW by about 21σ), but the rulings do not imply it.
  - **No neutrino-mass prediction** (withdrawn after review, `checkWorldline2/`). A massless neutrino is still recorded by route (a) weak scattering after decoupling, so its population's mean draw is exact, as photons' is. What remains is a validity condition, untestable by about 8 decades: m₁ ≳ 10⁻¹⁴–10⁻¹⁰ eV, for the weak-field condition at z ≈ 200–4000.
  - **It requires nucleon units.** Quark units would leave gluon energy undeposited.
  - **The window under the trial:**
$$
3.6\text{–}5.6\times10^{-36}\ <\ \kappa_b\ <\ \min\!\big(0.94\,r_C^5,\ 6.7\times10^{-20}\,r_C^2\big),\qquad 8.3\times10^{-8}\ \lesssim\ r_C\ \lesssim\ 5.1\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}.
$$
    The upper edge is SNO (B10, under GRW-type symmetrisation) below 4×10⁻⁷ m, and IGM heating (B7) above. So κ_b ≲ 1.7×10⁻²⁸ at the cap, and a·P∞ ≳ 5.7×10²⁷. The final review's 2.8×10⁻²⁷ missed B7.
  - **What it would change:**
    - M2: the rate becomes κ_b M̂c²/ħ per unit proper time;
    - M3: μ = γ²ħ/(κ_b c²);
    - the photon statements of Run 92 (B5, B6′) and Reading I0;
    - Reading I4's derivation and ruling 2.

    The X-ray and HST photon bounds become moot.
  - **Unreviewed candidate:** heating of the dilute gas in Leo T could move the upper edge by about 40× tighter to about 5× looser.
  - **Review (`checkWorldline2/`):**
    - The cosmology stands. The mean draw is exact for any time-varying rate (Campbell's theorem; Monte Carlo along the real Thomson history: 1.00000), provided λ_tot is ruled as the instantaneous rate of depositing records at the record's own time and place.
    - The window is confirmed: 8.3–9.0×10⁻⁸ ≲ r_C ≲ 5.1×10⁻⁵ m, κ_b ≤ 1.74×10⁻²⁸ at the cap, a·P∞ ≳ 5.75×10²⁷. But its top edge rests entirely on IGM heating (B7), which is good only to about an order of magnitude: κ_b at the cap lies in about 0.9–16×10⁻²⁸.
    - **Reading I4 does not survive as a derivation.** κ_b = 1/(aP∞) can be kept only by redefining it per radian of proper phase.
    - **A third reading, one step from I4, has not been compared:** λ = κ_b mc²/ħ per coordinate time.
- **A LISA Pathfinder noise budget for the 2–10 mHz plateau,** which could close the window without new Chandra work.

- A comparison of high-redshift quasars with nearby stars in archival Chandra data, which decides the cap region.
- A final independent review of the whole chain.
- Reading I6's final ruling. Until then, ONTOLOGY's statements that rest on B13 (a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴) and on Run 24's self-dip (p_s, P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰, the hop) are to be read as conditional.
- How a solid's draw is laid down at fine resolution, if at all: only route (b) and external position records do so under the rulings.
- If the strip survives, a registered run: r_C of 20–50 μm, testable by short-range gravity and X-ray imaging.
- If it closes, the chain (iii), T1 and route (b) cannot all stand, and one gives way.
