### 3. What renewal cannot be

Under the record as written, every massive thing renews about E/(aħ) times a second: 1.4×10²⁴ s⁻¹ per nucleon at a = 1, and 7.8×10²⁰ s⁻¹ per electron. The checks found that a renewal can be none of the following:

- **A localisation at the hop.** It would fix a body's position 10²⁰–10²⁸ times a second (E1), and tell apart branches at different counts (E2, on Note I §5's grounds). Coarse renewal at a fixed scale would need L ≳ 4×10¹² m for Kovachy's atoms, which Reading I4's (α) excludes throughout the window.
- **A localisation at any interaction scale.** At r = 10⁻¹¹ m, a localisation 1.4×10²⁴ times a second heats matter at about 7×10⁴ W per nucleon (Run 92's heating formula), against Earth's 8×10⁻¹² W/kg (E6). Run 92's B8 at κ = 1/a would need r ≳ 3×10¹⁰ m, and B10 would need r ≳ 3×10¹⁵ m.
  - It also destroys collective modes whatever their atom count. A mode's per-atom amplitude goes as 1/√N, so the summed rate is about Γħ/(4mωr²).

| system | rate × duration |
|---|---|
| Lee | 3×10¹¹ |
| Chu | 1.5×10²³ |
| Kotler | 6×10²⁵ |

  (E3; the check's computation.)
- **A kept count.** If a body with no resolved place still counted its renewals, two arms would differ by Δφ/a = mc²Δτ/(aħ) beats. That is which-path information carried in time. QM's clock visibility is an overlap and can revive; a kept integer count cannot (E2).
- **A gravity deposit.** If renewals deposited at E/(aħ), then λ_a ≈ 10²⁴ s⁻¹. That is Reading I1's rejected §5 reading (iii), not the deposit option (iii) below, and Run 93's G3: B13 and B15 vanish, and so does a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴.
- **Switched off in flight.** The third draft tried this. It fails E3 for committed bodies, and the rule depended on future records.

### 4. The restated amendment (A3′; ruled in direction 27 October; unreviewed)

**A3′. A renewal keeps no place, no count and no deposit beyond what the body's records already hold. It is not a localisation at any resolution.**
- **Records are placed by their kernel.** A route-(a) record fixes what its interaction resolves, through the standard kernel (Reading I1 ruling 1).
- **Route (b) is exempt.** It localises at r_C by its own primitive scale (Reading I5), with no interaction needed, as Runs 92–94 assume.
- **Deposits (option (iii), ruled 27 October).** An M3 deposit lands only where a record fixes position: at the outcome of that record's own Born update, spread over the record's kernel width. Records that fix only internal things, such as how a solid's vibrations are shared, make no deposit.
  - The other options fail.

| option | what happens | why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| (i) | the deposit's place is drawn and localises the body, at width w, at every record | at atomic widths it heats rock at about 10²⁰ W/kg; surviving B8 and B10 needs w ≳ 6×10⁴ m and ≳ 9×10⁹ m |
| (ii) | the place is drawn and the state is left alone | the dynamics becomes quadratic in ρ, ρ → Σ_x Tr(Π_xρ) U_xρU_x†, which signals in principle. This is the (S)/Gisin cost that Reading I0 gave as its reason for (C). At about 2×10¹⁶ deposits per flight, it also converges to (S)'s mean field |

  - A deposit split by branch is excluded (the medium holds no branches).
  - A finite spread is needed: a point deposit gives an infinite self-kick.
  - For gas, a collision fixes position to the coherence length it leaves (about 5.5×10⁻¹⁰ m for the relative motion), against a core of 2.3×10¹⁵ m. So Run 94's premise, that deposits land at resolution ≪ r_C, holds, and B15 stands.

**What follows.**
- **A free body in flight is a pattern.** Between interactions its renewals record nothing: no place, no count, no deposit. Its phase is turned by the committed world's count (A4). Its draw comes only from route (b), at ħ/κ_b per hit, smeared at r_C. This meets E1 and E2. Margalit's internal clock is a pattern whose visibility is QM's overlap.
- **A committed body with its centre of mass in superposition** (Pedalino's 77 K cluster in flight) has internal records (phonons, electrons), but they leave the centre of mass coherent and, under (iii), make no deposits. In flight its draw comes from route (b) alone, as for a free body; the centre of mass is a pattern for gravity too.
- **Committed bodies' collective modes stay coherent** because renewals localise nothing. Their coherence is limited only by route (a)'s kernel and record rates: a mode is coherent while Γ_rec·t ≪ 1. This meets E3 and E6.
- **What "unconditional renewal" means now.** For a free body it has no observable content: it is a label. Allen's rhythm is physical in two places:
  - in the phase (A4), which turns at mc²/ħ for every massive thing;
  - in records, which strike the beat when a body meets committed matter.

  Between records, the beat is the phase turning.
- **The rule is local and causal.** It depends only on the records present, not on later ones.

### 7. What a record is

**C3.**
- **A record is irreversible uptake.** Which-path information is a record when committed matter takes it up irreversibly at the interaction. That is judged locally, from the recorder's kind and state (degrees of freedom that thermalise), not from its later fate. This keeps Reading I1 ruling 3 (at the interaction), keeps ruling 4, and amends ruling 1 by adding irreversibility.
- **The recorders are:**
  - a thermalised population whose members' collisions recur (G1's gas: each H₂–H₂ collision is a record, keeping B15's λ_a);
  - a solid's thermal phonons and conduction electrons: they record internal states and decohere what they resolve, but under (iii) make no deposit (§5);
  - a detector atom that avalanches.

  These records resolve mode occupations through route (a)'s kernel, not atomic positions. So Lee's optical mode is not decohered by the diamond's thermal records beyond its measured decay.
- **The pattern carriers are classified by kind:** a single excitation out of equilibrium, such as an emitted photon or Lee's optical phonon. A carrier is recorded at its first interaction with a thermalised population (for Lee's phonon, its decay at about 1.4×10¹¹ s⁻¹).
- **A carrier commits only itself.** When a pattern carrier is absorbed, it commits only itself, and the original pattern is updated under T1, with no record, stamp or deposit. Every record and deposit is local. A unit recorded by route (b) alone deposits E/λ_b = ħ/κ_b, independent of the state; the rest deposit E_TK/λ_tot in operator form. So no deposit or record has a timing that a remote party controls, and (R-a3) and (T2) stay excluded.
- **Coherence loss comes from entangling evolution, record or not.** Records decide commitment, deposits and outcomes.
  - For hot C₇₀: a photon reaches the wall in about 0.3 ns, milliseconds before C₇₀ reaches the detector. T1 updates C₇₀ to a conditional state whose phase depends on the photon's direction. The Born-weighted average over where the photon is absorbed gives the kernel sinc(kd) = 0.127 (at d = 1 μm, λ = 0.83 μm). No-signalling makes this independent of where and when the photon is absorbed.
  - So a body is never committed by its own emission, and E5 holds.
  - Note I §5 ("interactions that leave no which-path record must leave the pattern coherent") and R-a1 need the matching wording: entangling interactions decohere, records commit.

### 8. Definite outcomes

**C4.**
- An outcome is fixed when the which-path information is taken up irreversibly by committed matter (a detector's atoms), through route (a), within the detector's response time.
- So GRW's lower bounds on the collapse rate do not carry over to κ_b. Those bounds come from requiring the model's own hits to make outcomes definite: λ > 5×10⁻¹⁹ s⁻¹ (GRWm) and about 5×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹ (flashes) (Feldmann and Tumulka 2012; Toroš and Bassi 2018).
- The condition is that committed matter is present around every measurement.

### 10. What this means

- **The record's renewal, taken as an event that records anything, conflicts with interference (E1), clocks in superposition (E2), bulk quantum memories (E3) and the heating bounds (E6).**
- **The restated A3′ resolves all four.** A renewal records nothing beyond the body's records. Records are placed by their kernel. Route (b) places at r_C. Deposits are Born-sampled.
  - A free body is then a pattern between commitments.
  - Committed bodies' collective modes stay coherent while their records are slow.
  - Allen's rhythm is the phase between records and the records themselves.
- **With C2 and C3,** Runs 92, 94 and 95 stand as registered. **Run 93's B13 loses its premise** (§5): the smallest a·P∞ the window allows falls from 3.66×10²⁴ to about 1.3×10¹³, while B15 keeps a·P∞ ≳ 2×10²⁷ above about 1.7×10¹⁵ m.
- **Through C2, it reopens the lattice record's clock chain** (p_s, gravity's P∞ bound, the hop), and puts ℓ_ED = ℓ_P back in play.
- **It is a consistency resolution, not a prediction.** It reproduces decoherence theory's account and was shaped with E3, E5 and E6 known. The test that separates CO here is still gravitationally mediated entanglement. A prediction could come from §9's internal-complexity reading.
