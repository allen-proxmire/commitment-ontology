**The question** (Note I §8, I4). From the hold rule (R2) and the pattern rule, derive route (b)'s rate λ = κ_b E/ħ and coarseness r_C for an isolated pattern, and register which combination of scales it gives before computing its value.

Run 92 supplies the target. In the primary case W is open for r_C from 2.32×10⁻¹⁰ m to 1.5×10¹⁸ m. At 100 nm, for example,
$$
2.6\times10^{-56}<\kappa_b<9.0\times10^{-46}.
$$

**What the record does not say:**
- whether a route-(b) record holds under R2 (Note I §6 (vi): unassessed);
- what a pattern's own possibility is, in units;

### 2. Allen's statement (27 October)

**A pattern's commitment is filled by the possibility the pattern itself carries** ("this seems obvious"). So R2's hold, for a route-(b) record, sums the pattern's own possibility, not the medium's.

### 3. Readings, each judged against M2 by the same standard

**(α0) R2 as written, with the pattern recommitting at each hold's end.**
- **Steps beyond the record:** two:
  1. R2 applies to a route-(b) record;
  2. the pattern recommits at once, as matter renews.
- **Rate:** the hold lasts ħ_t ticks whatever is committing, so
$$
\lambda=\frac1{T_{\rm hold}}=\frac{c}{\hbar_t\ell},
$$
  independent of the pattern's energy.
- **Against M2:** it is not per radian, so it **conflicts with M2 and the operator ruling**.
- **Against the data:** with r_C = ħ_tℓ (below), λ = c/r_C, which the data allow for r_C ≳ 4.3×10¹³ m. Radio interferometry (RadioAstron) sets that edge.
- **Against §2:** the hold is filled by the medium's possibility, not the pattern's, so §2's statement excludes this reading.
- A pattern that recommits at every hold would also be matter by §2's definition: Note I's reading (v), whose plain form interference of clock atoms rules out (Margalit et al. 2015).

**(α) Note D's energy relation applied to the pattern's own energy.**
- **Steps beyond the record:** three:
  1. R2 applies;
  2. the pattern recommits;
  3. the hold is set by the pattern's energy through the inferred E = aħ/T_hold, so it commits once per a radians.
- **Rate:**
$$
\kappa_b=\frac1a\in[0.16,\,10].
$$
- **Against M2:** per radian.
- **Against the data:** **excluded everywhere.** κ_hi is at most 6.5×10⁻⁸ anywhere in W, at the ceiling, so the exclusion is by 6.4–7.2 decades there and by 44–45 decades at 100 nm.

**Under §2's statement, how much possibility a pattern carries decides the rate.**

**(S-i) A stock in proportion to its energy, counted in pair-equivalents.**
- **The content:** a pair of energy E_pair takes one unit from each of its places. So a pattern of energy E carries
$$
q=\frac{2E}{E_{\rm pair}}
$$
  units at the commitment's places.
- **The hold:** it fills after 2ħ_tP∞/q ticks. With E_pair = aħ/(ħ_t t_tick), ħ_t cancels:
$$
\lambda=\frac{\omega}{a\,P_\infty},\qquad \kappa_b=\frac{1}{a\,P_\infty}.
$$
- **Steps beyond the record:**
  1. R2 applies;
  2. the pattern recommits at each hold's end;
  3. §2's statement;
  4. the content counted in pair-equivalents, which uses Note D's inferred E = aħ/T_hold;
  5. the whole pattern's content counts toward the hold, not only what sits at the commitment's two places.

  If only the share at two places counted, a pattern spread over n places would fill its hold n/2 times more slowly, and the pattern's extent would enter κ_b.
- **Against M2:** per radian, consistent with M2.
- **The combination of scales.** It is a CO-native combination with one scale, the vacuum's possibility per place, and a. P∞ is also gravity's scale: the strong-equivalence violation goes as ε = p_s/P∞. So this is the form Note I §1 set as the target, one scale in two domains.
- **Against Run 92.**
  - **At P∞'s gravity limit (6.4×10¹⁰),** κ_b = 1.6×10⁻¹¹/a. That lies in W only for r_C ≳ 0.94×10¹⁶ m (a = 2π), 2.3×10¹⁶ m (a = 1) or 7.4×10¹⁶ m (a = 0.1), up to the ceiling. All of this is in Run 92's conditional region, and a = 0.1 exceeds Q7's estimate (1.8×10⁻¹¹).
  - **At smaller r_C, W instead requires a larger P∞,** which the data allow, since P∞ is bounded only from below:

| r_C | κ_hi | a·P∞ needed |
|---|---|---|
| 2.3×10⁻¹⁰ m (W's lower end) | 4.9×10⁻⁵¹ | ≳ 2×10⁵⁰ |
| 100 nm | 9.0×10⁻⁴⁶ | ≳ 1.1×10⁴⁵ |
| 1 mm | 9.0×10⁻³⁸ | ≳ 1.1×10³⁷ |
| 1 m | 9.8×10⁻³⁴ | ≳ 1.0×10³³ |
| 10⁸ m | 3.3×10⁻²⁸ | ≳ 3×10²⁷ |
| 10¹⁶ m | 2.9×10⁻¹² | ≳ 3.5×10¹¹ |

  - So under (S-i), whatever sets r_C turns Run 92's window into a lower bound on P∞.
- **If the content is counted by the Tolman–Komar weight instead of the energy,** radiation carries twice its energy. κ_b would then be 2/(aP∞) for photons and (1 + v²)/(aP∞) for moving matter, not one κ_b for every pattern, which M2 requires. So the energy, not the weight, is the count consistent with M2.

**(S-ii) A fixed stock per quantum.**
- **The content:** each quantum carries q units, whatever its energy. One route to this is A5's amplitudes √P with a conserved charge. Another is the write-up's §4, item 4, a rate ∝ |amplitude|² × local bandwidth, normalised by H.
- **Rate:** λ_tick = q/(2ħ_tP∞), independent of energy.
- **Against M2:** **conflicts with M2.**
- **Against the data:** with r_C = ħ_tℓ, λ = qc/(2P∞r_C), which the data allow from about 10¹⁰ m up to the ceiling.

**(S-flow) The pattern's draw per tick fills the hold** (the first draft's (β)).
- **Rate:** S = 2ω_tick/a per tick gives κ_b = 1/(aħ_tP∞).
- **Problems:**
  - it counts a flow, which is the withdrawn flow clock's form, where R2 counts presence;
  - ruling (C) removes a pattern's per-tick draw: it draws only through its records.

$$
\kappa_b r_C=\frac{\ell}{aP_\infty}=6.5\times10^{-44}\text{–}5.1\times10^{-43}\ \text{m}
$$
  (with the hop consistent with a). It enters W at r_C ≈ 0.05–0.2 mm at P∞'s limit.

### 4. What sets r_C

Under (S-i), ħ_t cancels from κ_b, so R2 gives the rate but no length. The candidates for r_C:
- **the commitment's two places, r_C ≈ ℓ:** below the model's floor (5.15×10⁻¹⁶ m), and excluded on extrapolation by the weak-field edge, which is about 10⁻⁵ there;
- **the distance light crosses in one hold, r_C = ħ_tℓ = aħc/E_pair** (Note D's inference): the length set by one renewal's energy. With (S-i) this gives two independent scales:
$$
(\kappa_b,\ r_C)=\Big(\frac{1}{aP_\infty},\ \frac{a\hbar c}{E_{\rm pair}}\Big);
$$
- **the pattern's own extent:** not fixed by any rule.

### 5. What this means for Test I4

- **R2 as written gives a rate per hold, not per radian.** It conflicts with M2 and with §2's statement, not with the data.
- **With §2's statement, a stock in proportion to energy (S-i) gives κ_b = 1/(aP∞).** This is a derivation from R2, five steps beyond the record, of a per-radian rate set by the vacuum's possibility per place. It is the only reading here that is consistent with M2, the operator ruling and §2 together.
- **It does not give a value.** P∞ is bounded only below, a is not fixed, and r_C is not fixed by R2. What it gives is a relation: W is reached at coarseness r_C if a·P∞ ≳ 1/κ_hi(r_C). That is a·P∞ ≳ 10⁴⁵ at 100 nm, or the gravity limit only for r_C ≳ 10¹⁶ m.
- **It becomes a test** if a rule fixes r_C, through E_pair, the pattern's extent or another length. W then bounds P∞ from below, and a registered I4 could ask whether that bound is consistent with every other use of P∞ in the record.

### 6. A side finding: which count turns a pattern's phase

Suppose a pattern's phase is turned by the engine's live per-tick count (Run 16's exploratory arm), with noise independent from place to place, as in the engine's product measure. Then the phase noise at the hop scale heats matter. Per tick,
$$
\mathrm{var}\,N=\frac{\ln(1+P_\infty/p_s)\,(p_s+P_\infty)}{P_\infty^2}\approx1.9\times10^{-10},\qquad m_{\rm tick}=4.8\times10^{-17}\ \text{(a nucleon, a tick of }10^{-32}\text{ m}/c),
$$
which gives about 10²¹ W per nucleon. The estimate is the check's, confirmed to order of magnitude here: 8×10²⁰ W.

Earth's heat flow allows about 10⁻³⁸ W per nucleon. So the live count would need P∞ ≳ 10⁷⁰.

Run 16's registered arm turned the phase with the time-averaged count, and that has to be the reading that stands. Run 16's remark that the live-count noise "does not conflict with experiment" considered dephasing, not heating. This is an open item for A4 and R2, beyond I4.

### 7. Choices for a ruling

1. **Stock or flow:** the possibility a pattern carries (S-i, S-ii) or its draw per tick (S-flow). §2 and ruling (C) point to a stock.
2. **How much stock:** in proportion to the energy, counted in pair-equivalents (S-i), or a fixed amount per quantum (S-ii, which conflicts with M2).
3. **Whether a route-(b) record recommits at each hold's end,** and whether commitments are then periodic or random. Route (b) is ruled random, and B6's shot-noise edge assumes Poisson statistics. One option: the hold sets the mean rate, and commitments are random at that rate.
4. **The value of a:** 0.1, 1 or 2π.
5. **What sets r_C** (§4).
6. **Whether to register I4 now,** as the derivation κ_b = 1/(aP∞) and the bound on P∞ it implies at each r_C, or to wait for a rule for r_C.
7. **Which count turns a pattern's phase** (§6): the time-averaged count, or another rule with the noise correlated over large scales.

### 8. Rulings (27 October, "as recommended")

1. **A stock, not a flow:** the possibility a pattern carries fills its commitment's hold.
2. **In proportion to its energy,** counted in pair-equivalents (S-i). So
$$
\kappa_b=\frac{1}{a\,P_\infty}.
$$
3. **The pattern recommits at each hold's end, at random:** the hold sets the mean rate, and commitments are random at that rate.
4. **a is not ruled.** 0.1, 1 and 2π are all reported.
5. **What sets r_C stays open.**
6. **I4 is not registered** until a rule fixes r_C. Its bound on P∞ (§3, the S-i table) is arithmetic on Run 92's window.
7. **A pattern's phase is turned by the time-averaged count,** not the live count (§6; a ruling on Note I's A4).
