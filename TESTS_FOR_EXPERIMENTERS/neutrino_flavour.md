# A proposed check: neutrino flavour change that grows with energy

*For high-energy neutrino telescopes and atmospheric-neutrino detectors (IceCube and IceCube-Gen2, KM3NeT/ARCA and ORCA, Baikal-GVD, Super-Kamiokande and Hyper-Kamiokande). One page; detail in [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/prediction_map.md`](../OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/prediction_map.md).*

**The question.** Do muon neutrinos crossing the Earth at TeV energies change flavour more than standard oscillations allow, by an amount that grows with energy?

**Two answers.**
- *Standard physics:* the oscillation phase between two mass states is Δm²c⁴L/(4Eħc), which fades as energy rises; above a few hundred GeV, ν_μ cross the Earth almost unchanged.
- *The Commitment Ontology* (a proposed framework in which time is a count of irreversible events, with matter on a lattice of spacing ℓ): a massive particle's top speed on the lattice is raised slightly, by s = ½ r·mcℓ/ħ (r = ½; still below light's). Two neutrino mass states then differ in energy by

$$\Delta E=\frac{\Delta m^2c^4}{2E}+E\,\frac{r\,(m_3-m_2)\,c^2}{2E_\ell},\qquad E_\ell=\frac{\hbar c}{\ell}.$$

The second term grows with energy, is the same in every direction and at every time of day, is the same for antineutrinos, and acts through the standard mixing angles: an energy-dependent addition to Δm²₃₂, P(ν_μ → ν_τ) = sin²2θ₂₃ sin²(ΔE·L/(2ħc)).

**The mark.** Extra ν_μ disappearance for up-going neutrinos, rising with energy and path length as E·L, with the standard mixing's shape. In the dimension-four language of Lorentz-violation searches it is a real, isotropic μτ coefficient |c_μτ| = r(m₃ − m₂) sin 2θ₂₃/(4E_ℓ), its texture tied to the mass matrix.

**Where things stand.** IceCube's atmospheric ν_μ (reconstructed energies 0.4–18 TeV) show none: |Re c̊⁽⁴⁾_μτ| < 2.7 × 10⁻²⁸ at 90% (IceCube Collaboration, Nature Physics 14, 961 (2018)). Under the framework this bounds the lattice spacing at ℓ ≲ 1.0 × 10⁻³² m for m₃ − m₂ = 0.041 eV (normal ordering, lightest mass near zero; 8.5 × 10⁻³³ m for inverted ordering; 1.5 × 10⁻³¹ m if the masses are nearly equal). At that spacing the extra term equals the standard one near 1.5 TeV.

**What a result would mean.**
- Each factor 10 tighter on |Re c̊⁽⁴⁾_μτ| cuts the bound on ℓ by 10 (and raises the framework's photon ceiling, see [`photon_ceiling.md`](photon_ceiling.md), by √10).
- An isotropic, energy-growing ν_μ disappearance aligned with the standard mixing, with no sidereal variation, would be the framework's mark.
- The framework is silent on how neutrinos are made and detected (it has no weak interaction); the check assumes neutrinos are patterns on its lattice with ordinary (Dirac) masses.
