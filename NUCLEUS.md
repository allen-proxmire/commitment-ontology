# Nucleus

What the two primitives do not reach. The working record is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## The three open problems

The particle masses, the strength of the electromagnetic coupling (1/137), and colour. Each needs something not in the inventory.

## Colour

Colour is a three-component internal arrow whose orientation is a convention at each point; the gluon field compares conventions between neighbouring points. The only three-valued structure the primitives supply is spatial (the three lines through a place), and an SU(3) acting on the triad rotates with space: SO(3) is a subgroup of SU(3), not a factor, so no internal remainder survives. **The three lines of space are not colour.** An internal three-dimensional space is outside the inventory; the three conditions of possibility (free, in transit, engaged) are not interchangeable and cannot be one either.

## The bar for a third primitive

A third primitive is admissible on a named contradiction with the world: the rules make a proton an elementary pattern, while experiment makes it composite, bound by a force the rules lack, with most of its mass in the binding. It is worth admitting only if it cascades, bringing a second open problem with it. In the standard theory, colour with one coupling does cascade to the whole hadron spectrum; it does not reach the lepton masses or 1/137. The one geometric route to an internal three is Kaluza–Klein's: places with an inside.

What a third primitive must deliver: separable carriers, not three labels on one carrier. The evidence is deep-inelastic scattering (a single quark absorbing a photon incoherently, Bjorken scaling) and the gluon jets of three-jet events, which also show a gluonic degree of freedom carrying about half the proton's momentum; constituent-count scaling of flow is weaker and approximate (coalescence-interpreted; violated at the 20% level in lead collisions). A colour that is an orientation of a single pattern cannot give separable carriers. This excludes possibility in sorts as the candidate and asks for a binding whose release depends on the surroundings, and for whatever carries the other half of the momentum.

### Sorts of possibility, already costed

The obvious candidate is possibility in sorts: N kinds of possibility, indistinguishable under the rules, conserved together. That candidate has been run, in an earlier program by the same author, and its cost is known.

- **What it buys.** N indistinguishable, jointly conserved sorts force the gauge group U(N) = (SU(N) × U(1))/Z_N by uniqueness. Posit three sorts and SU(3) follows. This is a textbook result (the symmetry group of N identical conserved channels), restated; it is not a derivation of colour.
- **What it does not buy.** The count N is the input. No rule selects {1, 2, 3}; a symmetric N-sort multiplet is stable for every N, so nothing picks three. And the counts as posited give U(1) × U(2) × U(3), with three U(1) factors where the standard theory has one. The route does not land on the standard group without a further rule.
- **The one structural lead.** The three multiplicities reduce to one number: the most mutually orthogonal sorts an internal state of dimension d can carry is d, so d = 3 gives groups up to SU(3) and nothing stable above it. The question "why three sorts" becomes "why is the internal state three-dimensional." The matter sector already fixes an internal state of dimension four (Clifford), so the check is whether that four can carry the three. It cannot. The four is an irreducible representation of the Clifford algebra; by Schur's lemma the only operators commuting with the walk's dynamics (α₁, α₂, α₃, β) are scalars, so the only internal symmetry the four supports is U(1), charge. (Massless, the commutant is two-dimensional, 1 and γ₅: two chiral U(1)s. The mass term reduces it to one; so does the alternation term adopted in the matter sector, which breaks chiral symmetry explicitly as Wilson terms do.) Under rotations the four splits as 2 ⊕ 2, spin ½ twice, with no triplet. So the Clifford four is exhausted by charge; colour would need a new tensor factor, an internal three added to the inventory, not found in it. This sharpens the stop: two primitives give exactly one internal symmetry, U(1), and no more. (A stable SU(N ≥ 4) gauge symmetry would contradict the reduction, but none is seen, so this is consistency, not a test.) Check script: [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_notes/clifford_commutant.py`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_notes/clifford_commutant.py).
- **Independent confirmation of one closure.** That run also found the internal three distinct in kind from the spatial three, not reducible to it. Same conclusion as above, reached by a different route.

Net: sorts cascade exactly as far as SU(N) and stop. By the bar set here, a third primitive of this form is not yet earned.

## Three-tile patterns

*Shape, after one cold reading; not yet a candidate.* A quark is not a pattern. A proton is: a pattern of three **tiles**, a kind of pattern different from the electron's one tile. The relations inside such a pattern are its **kinships**, distinct from the relations between places. Whether this is a ruling on what possibility in transit can be or a third primitive by another name is open: it adds a tile (not a pattern), a kinship (a relation that does not slide free), a completion rule and a cost per length. Its inside is marked by the ruling made for entanglement: the tiles of one pattern commit together.

- **What stands.** Colour as the bookkeeping of which tile completes with which, with the relabelling of that bookkeeping a slide that costs nothing: that is gauge redundancy, correctly. A tile changing colour by emitting a gluon is a different thing, a physical process with energy and momentum, which the shape does not describe. The comparison of bookkeeping between places is a gauge connection; it lives on relations between places, and for a three-way matching it is a 3 × 3 unitary of unit determinant (SU(3); the determinant phase is the extra U(1) the sorts costing flagged).
- **What the shape lacks, and must have.** Gluon degrees of freedom. Gluons carry colour, act on each other, carry about half the proton's momentum, and are the thing that confines: pure SU(3) with no quarks at all has a string tension (√σ ≈ 440 MeV on the lattice) and a glueball spectrum. Any account in which the held quanta along a kinship are the tiles' renewals predicts zero tension without tiles and is falsified by that. The shape has no account of gluon self-coupling, of the running of the coupling, or of why it is strong at 1 fm and weak at 0.01 fm.
- **Confinement, what the reading gives.** Under the ordinary transit rule a pattern of size L renews once per round trip, mc² ≈ πħc/L, a cost that falls with separation (the bag model's zero-point term): a bound state, not confinement; excluded. A linear cost needs the kinship to be engaged possibility along its length, one held quantum per grain a: E = (ħc/a²) L, which is the strong-coupling flux string (Kogut–Susskind 1975) with the lattice spacing renamed. The grain for the measured tension is a ≈ 0.47 fm, Λ_QCD in disguise; setting a to a "tile mass" of m_p/3 gives σ ≈ 0.5 GeV/fm against 0.9, but that is a one-scale coincidence, not a mechanism: the law σ ∝ m_tile² is falsified by the flavour-universality of the tension (the same σ fits charmonium and bottomonium), the constituent mass and √σ are both Λ_QCD by dimensional transmutation, and a tile has no mass under the matter sector's own definition (mass is the renewal rate of a pattern; a tile does not renew alone). The tension is a new constant for CO, as it is Λ_QCD for the standard theory.
- **"Never commits alone."** Holds as confinement at low temperature. In the quark–gluon plasma quarks are deconfined, colour-charged, screened and interacting singly; and in deep-inelastic scattering a single quark absorbs a photon alone. "A tile never commits alone" is therefore false of the plasma and of DIS as stated; what it can mean is "no tile is ever found alone at the end," which is the standard statement.
- **What it costs.** The unselected three, the tension, and the new objects above. What it is: the standard account's ledger in CO's words, with the gluon column missing.

## The wall, stated

*Result.* On relations CO's inventory carries one real scalar (occupancy and ratio: the symmetric and antisymmetric parts of one number, which is all a scalar on a link can be) and one non-commuting matching, the frame (which line is which; the α's; gravity's self-coupling). On patterns it carries one complex phase (charge) and the four, which carries only charge. Every three-valued thing in it is a spatial vector. The nucleus needs, on each relation, a three-component *complex spatial scalar* with a unitary matching: colour must leave space alone, so it cannot be the frame, and it must be unitary, so it cannot be a real internal triad (places with a real inside give SO(3), not SU(3)). There is no complex three-component scalar anywhere in two primitives. Four routes from the inventory to that three were tried and closed, each by the same finding, that the three had been put in by hand and then found there: possibility in sorts (the count unselected; three U(1)s); the Clifford four (carries only charge, by Schur); the three lines of space (SO(3) is a subgroup of SU(3), not a factor); tiles with a tile mass (falsified by flavour-universality of the tension and by pure-glue confinement). Nothing in two primitives selects a three, and nothing in two primitives self-couples.

What a third primitive would have to be, and what it costs: a label with a three-way matching carried on the relations inside a pattern (a kinship). Given that label, the rest follows by uniqueness at the standard theory's price, one coupling and one scale: SU(3) from the matching; self-coupling because the comparison is carried by patterns, which carry stock, so comparisons of carriers are comparisons of stock (candidate reading, below); confinement's shape from a held, non-sliding kinship. It cascades to the hadron spectrum and no further: not to the masses, not to 1/137, not to the now. By the bar set above, it is not earned. **CO's only non-abelian matching is space's; the nucleus needs one that is not space's; CO stops at the nucleus, and this is why.** The earliest point at which the rules say "one" where the nucleus needs "three" is the amplitude rule: possibility in transit carries one complex amplitude. A relation carrying a complex triplet, with its own addition rule, would be the object; nothing supplies it, and nothing forbids it. Any future route starts there, not at the nucleus. Read against the rules: the amplitude rule fixes the algebra of one amplitude and says nothing about how many a transit carries; the internal-state ruling sets a lower bound (the four) and admits further factors. So the third primitive has a final form, *possibility in transit carries a complex triplet*: admissible, unselected, and the single posit from which U(3), the gluon column and confinement's shape follow at the standard price. Six closed routes converge on it; none produced it from the inventory.

The count is measured, not free: the e⁺e⁻ → hadrons ratio (proportional to the number of colours times the sum of squared quark charges, checked across energy ranges) and the π⁰ → γγ rate (proportional to the number squared) both give three, with charges ⅔ and −⅓. Any route to the nucleus must give three distinct carriers with those charges; a two or a four would be a falsification, not a near miss.

*Candidate reading, the gluon column.* In CO a comparison between places is carried by possibility in transit, a pattern. A comparison of charge is carried by a pattern with no sign (the photon), so comparisons of it are not comparisons of charge: abelian. A comparison of a three-way matching would be carried by a pattern whose internal state is a pair of matching-labels; such a carrier carries the thing it compares, so the field acts on itself: the adjoint. Self-coupling is then not a second posit beyond the three; it follows from carriers being patterns. (The standard fact: a non-abelian gauge field is in the adjoint because the charge is a matrix.) What this does not do is supply the three.

## Three kinds of wall

The walls around the nucleus are not all the same kind, and only one kind can be got round.

- **Silences.** Nothing selects N = 3. Not a theorem; the primitives do not speak. Passable by one thing only, a new rule, and the cascade bar decides whether it is earned.
- **Representation theorems.** The Clifford four carries only U(1) (above). Schur's lemma has no lattice premise. A relational lattice does not touch it; a time-dependent dynamics (hold-and-release) can only shrink a commutant, never enlarge it.
- **Continuum no-gos.** Nielsen–Ninomiya assumes a fixed translation-invariant lattice; Coleman–Mandula assumes Poincaré invariance and an S-matrix. Neither premise holds at the hop scale, so both have loopholes on a relational lattice. The Coleman–Mandula loophole is where "the three lines of space are colour" could have lived; at the hop scale the triad is three link labels, and mixing them with phases is the walk's own coin, which is the Clifford structure, which is the second wall. The loophole is real; using it lands on Schur.

One loophole with content was checked and is empty. If hold-and-release treated the two chiralities differently, the held phase would carry structure the transit phase lacks. It does not: hold scales hop and flip alike, the flip is parity-even, and the hop rule (symmetric occupancy, antisymmetric ratio 1-form) has no handedness. A chiral asymmetry would need an axial 1-form, which the inventory lacks. Checked numerically on the three-dimensional walk with a hold bump: L→R and R→L transfer equal to 10⁻¹⁴ ([`hold_chirality_check.py`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_notes/hold_chirality_check.py)). Ruled: hold-and-release is chirality-blind. (The check used a normalised explicit scheme, not the automaton; the equality is a symmetry of the scheme, and the number is round-off on an identity.)

## Two walls, faced

- **Bell.** A commitment is a local, irreversible, definite event, and Born is read as an identification. Two entangled patterns committing at spacelike separation correlate beyond anything a local rule gives; no lattice property evades this, since Bell has no lattice premise. Candidate: an entangled pair is one pattern sampled twice; a commitment belongs to the pattern, not the place, and one event increments the count at both ends. Cost: not yet paid. A single event at two spacelike places needs either a preferred ordering (a foliation, which the gravity sector does not have and which would generically give nonzero preferred-frame parameters against its no-deviation-from-GR result) or a flash-type ontology in which the event is Lorentz-invariantly defined without one (Tumulka 2006). Which, is open. Checked: a commitment resolves exactly the possibility it engages, so a renewal, which engages free possibility, resolves nothing about the pattern, and the only free-flight trace is the retained record δħ per renewal, giving fringe visibility V = (1 − δ)^N after N renewals; molecule interferometry (C₇₀, 25 kDa) bounds δ ≲ 10⁻²⁷ and δ ≈ 10⁻³² sits inside it ([`record_visibility_bound.py`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_notes/record_visibility_bound.py)). A bound passed, not a prediction. That a commitment resolves only what it engages, free possibility in a renewal and a pattern's transit in a measurement, is a reading of the rules (candidate; working record), not a derivation. Candidate.
- **Doublers.** Faced, not closed. The Dirac automaton as published carries three zone-edge doublers and, in its sequential step ordering, eight further massless modes. A relational lattice alone does not remove them (R8); a one-way non-unitary hop removes them and the electron with them (R9, excluded); symmetrising the step removes the eight (R10) and a flip rate rising with alternation, the Wilson term in unitary form, lifts the rest to the hop scale for r in a window (R10, corrected in R10d after review). That is the standard Wilson statement: one light species plus heavy copies at the cutoff. Since the hop is physical, the copies are predicted particles, and their absence bounds the hop length from above. Two candidate rulings and one bound, recorded in the matter sector. Open.

## Status

**CO is a reconstruction of physics up to 1930 from two primitives.** It covers gravity, electromagnetism and the electron, and stops at the nucleus.
