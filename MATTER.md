# Matter

Results only. The working record, with every route tried and every withdrawal, is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## The addition

To the primitives and rules of [`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md), one statement: **commitments happen at a rate.** Matter has a phase, where it is in its cycle, and a pattern in transit carries it.

## Results

**Amplitudes.** Two routes bring amounts p₁, p₂ to a place with phase difference Δθ. Conservation (the cross term averages to zero over unrelated phases), scalelessness (invariance under a common scaling) and associativity (three routes combine the same whichever pair first) leave one rule:

$$p=\left|\sqrt{p_1}\,e^{i\theta_1}+\sqrt{p_2}\,e^{i\theta_2}\right|^2 .$$

Forced given those premises (Goyal, Knuth and Skilling 2010); the premises carry the square-root structure.

**The Born rule.** The thing that commits is identified with the thing that is conserved in passing: the chance of a commitment at a place is the possibility there, and the chances sum to one. An identification, which a reader may call the postulate restated.

**The internal state.** A walk of amplitudes that is homogeneous (sliding costs nothing), unitary and moves cannot be scalar (Meyer 1996). With three directions whose shift operators anticommute and a mass step anticommuting with all three, the smallest representation is C⁴: Dirac's α and β; chirality × spin is its labelling.

**Mass.** The mass step changes the internal state without moving it, with amplitude i·mcℓ/ħ per hop: Feynman's checkerboard weight (the Wilson term below acts across neighbouring places). There is a heaviest pattern, one flip per hop, and its Compton wavelength is the hop length:

$$\ell=\frac{\hbar}{m_{\max}c},\qquad \ell\lesssim 1.3\times10^{-31}\ \text{m}.$$

The bound is set by the electron's speed (below), and with ℓ = ℓ_P√(4π/δ) it gives δ ≳ 2 × 10⁻⁷. The heaviest pattern is then above 10¹⁵ GeV.

**The Dirac equation.** Possibility hopping at c with the four-component state, flipping at the mass rate, has the Dirac equation as its continuum limit (Bialynicki-Birula 1994). Spin ½ follows. The magnetic moment g = 2 follows from Dirac's equation with minimal coupling to the which-way field; that coupling is a candidate (below). The rules are identified with a construction known to yield Dirac's equation; this is not an independent derivation of it.

One needed step, one candidate ruling, and one open item. On the cubic lattice the automaton as published carries extra low-energy species (fermion doubling; Nielsen–Ninomiya 1981; Karsten and Smit 1981): at the zone edges and half-zone points and, in the sequential x-then-y-then-z ordering, eight further massless modes at (±π/2, ±π/2, ±π/2), where the hop product is ∝ γ⁵ (Gupta and Short 2026 place them there too). Needed (i): the step symmetrised as x, y, z, flip, z, y, x (Strang 1968); this removes the eight γ⁵ modes, and data require it: the sequential step gives two branches at k ± ℓk_xk_yk_z/|k|, a term linear in energy that depends on handedness, which Crab electron bounds exclude for any hop above the Planck length (run L1). Candidate (ii), the Wilson term in strictly local form: each conditional shift is split into two half-shifts with an on-site turn between them, A_j = S_j⁺ F(w) S_j⁻ F(−w), where S_j⁺ moves the α_j = +1 part one place forward, S_j⁻ moves the α_j = −1 part one place back, and F(a) = exp(−iβa); the step is A_x A_y A_z F(θ) A_z A_y A_x. At k = 0 the turns cancel; at a zone edge the half-shift anticommutes with β and the turns add, giving the extra species mass. For w ≳ 0.3 the walk has one light species, at k = 0, and every other species is gapped in both quasi-energy sectors (run L1b); every factor acts on one place or moves one part one place. Explicit chiral-symmetry breaking comes with it. Because the hop is physical, those extra species are physical particles with the electron's quantum numbers and mass of order wħ/(ℓc), above 10¹⁴ GeV at the bound below. **Lorentz invariance.** The Wilson term gives a massive pattern a limiting speed above the massless one by s ≈ 0.14·mcℓ/ħ at w = 0.3, the same in every direction (run L1b); with light at the walk's c (candidate), Crab electrons (s < 5 × 10⁻²⁰) need ℓ ≲ 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ m (needed). Photon time of flight gives the weaker ℓ ≲ 10⁻²⁷ m. Once loops are added, a lattice with a preferred frame generates species-dependent speeds of order α/π (Collins et al. 2004); nothing in the rules suppresses them. Open. The one-way (non-unitary) hop was tested and excluded: it damps a pattern at a rate ∝ k² per hop and gives the electron a lifetime of ≈ 10⁻¹⁵ s at ε = 0.5 (scaling as 1/ε; no ε removes the doublers without removing the electron). A relational lattice alone does not remove the doublers (Christ, Friedberg and Lee 1982; Perantonis and Wheater 1988). Runs R8–R10d in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_runs/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_runs/).

**Charge (candidate).** A relation carries, besides its occupancy, a which-way phase that reverses with direction, e^{iA}: a U(1) 1-form, distinct from the real ratio of possibility that drives hopping (candidate ruling). Shifting it by dχ is a redundancy that acts on amplitudes as ψ → e^{iqχ}ψ (candidate ruling). With sliding costing nothing, the unique quadratic cost is Maxwell's. Charge is a sign a pattern carries on the relations it uses: each hop carries the relation's phase, e^{±iqA}, which is minimal coupling and gives g = 2 at tree level (up to an O(mcℓ/ħ) lattice artefact); A₀ enters as an on-site phase per step; the antiparticle is the same pattern with the opposite sign; a traversed loop is a magnetic dipole, not a charge. The field's strength, 1/137, is from data: the which-way stiffness in quanta per hop.

**Statistics.** The walk is symmetric under the swap of the carried sign, C. Ruled: the generator is odd under C, so a pattern and its anti-pattern renew at the same rate in opposite senses of the internal turn. On a stepped walk the frequency is an angle defined mod 2π; this ruling fixes its zero, selecting the branch |ε| < π everywhere the band does not meet ±π (it does meet ±π in the doubler sector, where the ruling fails). With the branch fixed, the one-pattern walk, second-quantised in the standard way (site modes with canonical brackets, positive Fock metric, vacuum the generator's lowest state), admits only anticommutators (Pauli 1940). Fermi statistics is checked compatible with the sector; it is not forced by it, since those three inputs are adopted from field theory and lattice field theory has no spin–statistics theorem. What two patterns do on one stock remains open.

## Negative results

No classical current gives g = 2 (the loops give g = 1); spin from orbital circulation is not ħ/2 for the allowed loops; the lattice loop is not the electron's orbit (the Dirac walk's rest-frame current has no hexagonal structure). The loop was the ladder to the internal state; the walk is what stands.

## Open

Why each particle commits at the rate it does (the masses: no selection mechanism; counting arguments give no ratios); the value of 1/137; the anomalous moment (needs loops); statistics (what two patterns do on one stock: Fermi statistics is compatible with the walk but not forced; no rule for two patterns, so neither exclusion nor condensation is reached; open, see `OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`); the two-pattern problem (candidate ruling): what a relation carries is one number per configuration of what passes, not one number; the which-way field is then quantum, its longitudinal flux fixed by the charge configuration (Gauss per configuration) and the Coulomb term acting on the pair amplitude. The one-number reading is mean-field and the helium ground state excludes it (−77.9 eV against −79.0 eV). With the ruling, two Dirac walks have the two-body Dirac–Coulomb operator as continuum limit and helium's −79.0 eV (Hylleraas 1929) by identification; the construction is standard and CO supplies only the ruling. Ruled with it (candidate): the bound/emitted split. The part of the which-way field fixed at the same moment by the positions, currents and spins of what passes is bound and acts on the pair amplitude, each pattern's own part counted in its mass; the part that has left its sources is emitted, and is light. At order v²/c² the bound part adds the Breit interaction, and Coulomb plus Breit is frame-independent to that order. By identification: positronium's levels −6.80 eV/n² and the magnetic part of its hyperfine split, α⁴mc²/3 = 116.8 GHz (4/7 of the leading 204.4 GHz); hydrogen with recoil (reduced mass, the isotope shift) and its 21 cm line, 1418.8 MHz at tree level with the proton taken as one pattern and g_p imported (1420.406 MHz measured; the gap is mainly the electron's anomalous moment, which needs loops). Positronium's annihilation part and lifetimes need number change, which is priced and held (`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`). Costs: the instantaneous term is frame-dependent beyond order v²/c²; Brown–Ravenhall and the fixed-number Coulomb self-energy at the hop are inherited; the covariant coupled walk between commitments remains open, and the engagement/route criterion with it. Ruled with the two-pattern ruling (candidate): a carried quantity is branched with what sets it, if what sets it has amplitude. The amplitude rule gives amplitude to possibility in transit, on routes; free possibility has no cycle, so no phase, so no amplitude. The which-way flux, set by transit, is branched; the free stock and the clock field h are classical. The record is two things: the count a pattern carries (phase, not a register) and the trace its renewals leave in the free stock, a dent of δħ at the place of each renewal, chosen with intensity (mc²/ħ) ψ̄ψ, which is ω|a|² for a pattern at rest (ω|a|² itself would not slow a moving pattern's renewals), and resolving nothing about the pattern (possibility is a continuous amount in the rules: C3 keeps δħ, a continuous amount, nothing counts it in grains; the author's ruling). The dents are independent draws from |a|² (posit: a draw is not tied to where the pattern is later found; the stock's having one value does not by itself give this), so they carry no which-way information; δ is a constant of gravity only and a pattern left alone is not resolved. A quantum free stock (vacuum spread) is set aside by the ruling that free possibility has no amplitude; and is excluded observationally: by the dent and capacity alone (a spread σ ≳ 7 × 10¹³ P_max would be needed against σ ≲ P_max), and with clock coherence by about 10¹⁵. Cost: the dent process is a classical point process driven by |a|²; the nearest established relative is Tilloy–Diósi 2016, which keeps the back-action CO drops. The dents act back on the amplitude through h, a Schrödinger–Newton nonlinearity at G as measured, so no-signalling rests on the entanglement reading (local source unconditioned by a far event until it is in the local past cone) and is open with it. A pattern's successive renewals are then independent draws across its whole support, with no trajectory. Measurement (candidate ruling, C8): a commitment may engage a quantum of possibility in transit as part of the ħ it engages (C4 fixes the total, not the source), its rate or transit changing by that quantum's frequency and direction; the pattern whose transit it was is resolved by it; what shares a joint amplitude with it (an entangled pair, a probe correlated with a system) is conditioned on the outcome, not resolved (the Lüders update, imported with the joint law; the author's ruling, candidate). Only through a channel, a final state of the engager, in renewal rate or transit, matching the quantum's frequency and direction within its width; at Born's chance (the place of an engagement, given one, is drawn from |a|²; it resolves in the channel's basis (the interaction's pointer basis, imported with the coupling): the engaged pattern's place only as finely as the engager's channel is localised, so a detector pixel resolves position and a body's internal mode does not (the author's ruling, candidate); the stopping number counts a chain's breadth, not passages over time (the author's ruling, candidate, on a shape; breadth counted in quanta (ruled, candidate), how it is taken in a superposition owed; the place of an internal commitment is an independent draw that records nothing, as the dents are (ruled, candidate)); the channel's coupling, imported, sets the amplitude of passage through the channel, and a rate only where its final states form a continuum, not the chance that a passage is a commitment: passage through an open channel can be coherent and reversible, as in vacuum Rabi oscillation); a channel is a final state with its width Γ, and engagement follows its line shape, κ(Δ) ∝ (Γ/2)²/(Δ² + (Γ/2)²) near the line, off step by Δ (the author's ruling, candidate); a sharp edge would be excluded by measured line wings, which fall smoothly and stay nonzero many widths off line; with no final state, or a zero matrix element, none (κ = 0). It supplies the clause the measurement reading lacked (C1 names free stock as food and is silent on transit). Not ruled: which contacts through a channel are engagements rather than routes (a chain of passages with a primitive stopping number, bounded below by NMR reversals of clusters of up to ~7000 correlated spins (~10–10², flip-flop echoes, which count patterns; under quanta counting not yet a bound: ruled, a drive that only steers between passages counts and one that is a partner in each passage does not, so double-quantum NMR does not enter), with passages counted as quanta handed on (ruled; a single-phonon swap into a large resonator is one), or the emergent class the premise excludes); where the resolved pattern's possibility elsewhere goes; (resolution through correlation, as in QND photon counting, is read as repeated conditioning of a joint amplitude, one cut per engaged probe).
