# Matter

Results only. The working record, with every route tried and every withdrawal, is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## The addition

To the primitives and rules of [`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md), one statement: **commitments happen at a rate.** Matter has a phase, where it is in its cycle, and a pattern in transit carries it.

## Results

**Amplitudes.** Two routes bring amounts p₁, p₂ to a place with phase difference Δθ. Conservation (the cross term averages to zero over unrelated phases), scalelessness (invariance under a common scaling) and associativity (three routes combine the same whichever pair first) leave one rule:

$$p=\left|\sqrt{p_1}\,e^{i\theta_1}+\sqrt{p_2}\,e^{i\theta_2}\right|^2 .$$

Forced given those premises (Goyal, Knuth and Skilling 2010); the premises carry the square-root structure.

**The Born rule.** The thing that commits is identified with the thing that is conserved: the chance of a commitment at a place is the possibility there, and the chances sum to one. An identification, which a reader may call the postulate restated.

**The internal state.** A walk of amplitudes that is homogeneous (sliding costs nothing), unitary and moves cannot be scalar (Meyer 1996). With three directions whose shift operators anticommute and a mass step anticommuting with all three, the smallest representation is C⁴: Dirac's α and β; chirality × spin is its labelling.

**Mass.** The mass step changes the internal state without moving it, with amplitude i·mcℓ/ħ per hop: Feynman's checkerboard weight. There is a heaviest pattern, one flip per hop, and its Compton wavelength is the hop length:

$$\ell=\frac{\hbar}{m_{\max}c},\qquad \ell\lesssim 1.1\times10^{-18}\ \text{m (at or below the top quark's)},$$

which with ℓ = ℓ_P√(4πP_max/δ) gives δ ≈ 10⁻³² for P_max of order a few quanta. The relation renames one unknown as another; δ has no observational handle of its own.

**The Dirac equation.** Possibility hopping at c with the four-component state, flipping at the mass rate, has the Dirac equation as its continuum limit (Bialynicki-Birula 1994). Spin ½ follows. The magnetic moment g = 2 follows from Dirac's equation with minimal coupling to the which-way field; that coupling is a candidate (below). The rules are identified with a construction known to yield Dirac's equation; this is not an independent derivation of it.

Two candidate rulings and one open item. On the cubic lattice the automaton as published carries extra low-energy species (fermion doubling; Nielsen–Ninomiya 1981; Karsten and Smit 1981): three at the zone edges and, in the sequential x-then-y-then-z ordering, eight further massless modes at (±π/2, ±π/2, ±π/2), where the hop product is ∝ γ⁵ (Gupta and Short 2026 place them there too). Candidate (i): the three hops are taken in no preferred order, the step symmetrised as x, y, z, flip, z, y, x (Strang 1968); this removes the eight γ⁵ modes, but a palindrome is still an ordering and alternatives exist, so it is a choice, not a consequence of the primitives. The massless symmetrised walk has 32 zero modes. Candidate (ii): the flip rate rises with alternation between neighbouring places, θ(k) = θ + r Σ_j(1 − cos k_j), the Wilson term in unitary form; since the angle is periodic, r must sit in a window, r ≲ 0.35. With both, the walk has one light species with the Dirac dispersion at low k and extra species at masses of order r to 0.9 in hop units, in both the quasi-energy-0 and quasi-energy-π sectors: the standard Wilson-fermion statement, not a removal, and explicit chiral-symmetry breaking comes with it. Because the hop is physical, those extra species are physical particles with the electron's quantum numbers and mass ≈ r ħ/(ℓc); their absence below ≈ 100 GeV bounds the hop, ℓ ≲ 6 × 10⁻¹⁹ m for r = 0.3, below the top-quark bound. Open. The one-way (non-unitary) hop was tested and excluded: it damps a pattern at a rate ∝ k² per hop and gives the electron a lifetime of ≈ 10⁻¹⁵ s at ε = 0.5 (scaling as 1/ε; no ε removes the doublers without removing the electron). A relational lattice alone does not remove the doublers (Christ, Friedberg and Lee 1982; Perantonis and Wheater 1988). Runs R8–R10d in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_runs/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/NUCLEUS_runs/).

**Charge (candidate).** A relation carries, besides its occupancy, a ratio that reverses with direction: a 1-form. With sliding costing nothing, the unique quadratic cost is Maxwell's. Charge is a sign a pattern carries on the relations it uses; the antiparticle is the same pattern with the opposite sign; a traversed loop is a magnetic dipole, not a charge. The field's strength, 1/137, is from data: the which-way stiffness in quanta per hop.

**Statistics.** The walk is symmetric under the swap of the carried sign, C. Ruled: the generator is odd under C, so a pattern and its anti-pattern renew at the same rate in opposite senses of the internal turn. On a stepped walk the frequency is an angle defined mod 2π; this ruling fixes its zero, selecting the branch |ε| < π everywhere the band does not meet ±π (it does meet ±π in the doubler sector, where the ruling fails). With the branch fixed, the one-pattern walk, second-quantised in the standard way (site modes with canonical brackets, positive Fock metric, vacuum the generator's lowest state), admits only anticommutators (Pauli 1940). Fermi statistics is checked compatible with the sector; it is not forced by it, since those three inputs are adopted from field theory and lattice field theory has no spin–statistics theorem. What two patterns do on one stock remains open.

## Negative results

No classical current gives g = 2 (the loops give g = 1); spin from orbital circulation is not ħ/2 for the allowed loops; the lattice loop is not the electron's orbit (the Dirac walk's rest-frame current has no hexagonal structure). The loop was the ladder to the internal state; the walk is what stands.

## Open

Why each particle commits at the rate it does (the masses: no selection mechanism; counting arguments give no ratios); the value of 1/137; the coupling that gives g = 2; statistics (what two patterns do on one stock: Fermi statistics is compatible with the walk but not forced; no rule for two patterns, so neither exclusion nor condensation is reached; open, see `OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`); the two-pattern problem (candidate ruling): what a relation carries is one number per configuration of what passes, not one number; the which-way field is then quantum, its longitudinal flux fixed by the charge configuration (Gauss per configuration) and the Coulomb term acting on the pair amplitude. The one-number reading is mean-field and the helium ground state excludes it (−77.9 eV against −79.0 eV). With the ruling, two Dirac walks have the two-body Dirac–Coulomb operator as continuum limit and helium's −79.0 eV (Hylleraas 1929) by identification; the construction is standard and CO supplies only the ruling. Costs: the transverse part of the same field must be light (no separate pattern); the instantaneous term is frame-dependent; Brown–Ravenhall and the fixed-number Coulomb self-energy at the hop are inherited; the covariant coupled walk between commitments remains open, and the engagement/route criterion with it. Ruled with it (candidate): a carried quantity is branched with what sets it; the flux and the clock field are set by transit and are branched. The record is two things: the count a pattern carries (phase, not a register) and the trace its renewals leave in the free stock, which is set by where the pattern renews and is branched with its place; h is fed by the trace. The trace is a which-way mark, a displacement of δħ·(P_free/P_max) in amount per renewal (possibility is a continuous amount in the rules: C3 keeps a fraction, nothing counts it in grains; the author's ruling). Its dephasing is Γ ≈ (δħ/σ)²ω, σ the spread of the free stock at a place, ≈ δ²ω ≈ 10⁻⁴⁰ s⁻¹ per nucleon on the vacuum-spread posit. δ is a constant of gravity only and a pattern left alone is not resolved at any observable rate. Vacuum spread (candidate): the no-collapse statement requires the free stock at a place to be a quantum variable in a superposition of amounts, width σ ≳ 10⁻¹⁶ ħ; the rules do not supply this. A sharp amount per branch is excluded: it would localise every pattern at resolution ℓ at its Compton rate, and no atom would be stable. A classical stock, dented at one place per renewal by Born chance, is consistent with observation and is excluded here only by the branched-field ruling (candidate). The posit gives the free stock an independent state and a conjugate variable at each place, which the gravity sector's hypothesis that the occupancies are the only dynamical field does not allow as written. Open.
