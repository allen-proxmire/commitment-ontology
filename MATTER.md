## The addition

To the terms and rules of [`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md), one statement: **commitments happen at a rate.** Matter has a phase, where it is in its cycle, and a pattern in transit carries it.

## One pattern

**Amplitudes.** Two routes bring amounts p₁, p₂ to a place with phase difference Δθ. Conservation, scalelessness and associativity leave one rule (Goyal, Knuth and Skilling 2010):

$$p=\left|\sqrt{p_1}\,e^{i\theta_1}+\sqrt{p_2}\,e^{i\theta_2}\right|^2 .$$

**The Born rule.** The thing that commits is identified with the thing conserved in passing: the chance of a commitment at a place is the possibility there. An identification, which a reader may call the postulate restated.

**The internal state.** A homogeneous, unitary walk that moves cannot be scalar (Meyer 1996). With three anticommuting shifts and a mass step anticommuting with all three, the smallest representation is C⁴: Dirac's α and β.

**Mass.** The mass step turns the internal state without moving it, at the rate mc²/ħ (Feynman's checkerboard weight, in the continuum). On the earlier palindromic walk the heaviest pattern flipped once per hop, ℓ = ħ/(m_max c); on the flow step below the heaviest mass is limited only by the tick, τ_f(m + 6r) < π, so that relation is not carried. Bounds on the hop: LHAASO's 3.7 PeV photon from Cygnus X-3 (9 kpc), which has not split (photon splitting, weakest direction), IceCube's atmospheric neutrinos (candidate, below) and the Crab Nebula's synchrotron cutoff (candidate, at the standard nebular field):

$$\ell\lesssim 2.4\times10^{-31}\ \text{m}\quad(\text{photon}),\qquad \ell\lesssim 1.0\times10^{-32}\ \text{m}\quad(\text{neutrinos}),\qquad \ell\lesssim 3.2\times10^{-32}\ \text{m}\quad(\text{synchrotron}).$$

With ℓ = ℓ_P√(4π/δ) these give δ ≳ 6 × 10⁻⁸, δ ≳ 3 × 10⁻⁵ and δ ≳ 3 × 10⁻⁶.

**The Dirac equation.** Matter's step is one tick of the lattice Dirac flow with a Wilson term (candidate; chosen):

$$U=e^{-i\tau_f H},\qquad H=\sum_j\alpha_j\sin k_j+\beta\Big(m+r\sum_j(1-\cos k_j)\Big),\qquad r=\tfrac12\ \text{(posited)},$$

with τ_f a convention (tick length against flow time). Possibility moves only between related places under H; a tick reaches n places with amplitude falling about as τ_fⁿ/n!, within the fast-fading reach the 12 Nov locality ruling allows (candidate). Its continuum limit is the Dirac equation (Wilson 1974). Spin ½ follows; g = 2 follows with minimal coupling to the which-way field. This identifies the rules with a known construction; it is not an independent derivation.

**The step's symmetry.** The step is symmetric under every rotation and reflection of the cube as an operator identity, so every two-index response of the vacuum (permittivity, permeability, speed tensors) is the same in every direction (run C3i). The earlier palindromic hop sequence (x, y, z, flip, z, y, x) broke that symmetry in loops through its nesting order and would have needed four direction counter-terms for light, held to 1 part in 10³⁰ (run I4); the step removes them. The sequential order gives a handedness-dependent term that Crab electrons exclude (run L1). The Wilson term gives the lattice's extra species masses 2r, 4r and 6r in units of ħ/(ℓc), above 10¹⁴ GeV, leaving one light species. Those heavy species are not particles (needed). Their rest energy is mostly the Wilson turn, which is part of the hop and not a commitment (chosen, candidate); by C1 and C4 they are then not matter. As particles they would violate the equivalence principle: in a field they fall at about 0.6 of matter's rate (run F1d; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md), Doublers).

**Lorentz invariance.** The Wilson term gives a massive pattern a speed excess s = ½ r·mcℓ/ħ, and the lattice a quadratic term in the group velocity, c₂(n̂) = 3(r²/8 − Σ_j n_j⁴/6): −0.406, −0.156 and −0.073 along an axis, a face diagonal and a body diagonal at r = ½. An electron's speed relative to straight light peaks at (mcℓ/ħ)(r/2 − √(2|c₂|)), negative in every direction, so electrons never outrun light; their speed is capped just below it, and the Crab synchrotron glow, which needs electrons at γ ≈ 7 × 10⁹, gives the synchrotron bound above (2.6 × 10⁻³¹ m at a 1 mG field). The excess is kept: the speed counter-terms do not absorb it (chosen). For two neutrino mass states it adds a flavour-changing term that grows with energy, ΔE = Δm²c⁴/(2E) + E·r(m₃ − m₂)c²/(2E_ℓ), E_ℓ = ħc/ℓ, the same in every direction and aligned with the standard mixing; IceCube's atmospheric ν_μ through the Earth (0.4–18 TeV) limit it to |c_μτ| = r(m₃ − m₂) sin 2θ₂₃/(4E_ℓ) < 2.7 × 10⁻²⁸, so ℓ ≲ 1.0 × 10⁻³² m at m₃ − m₂ = 0.041 eV (candidate; neutrinos taken as patterns on the step with Dirac masses, the weak interaction being absent; 1.5 × 10⁻³¹ m if the masses are nearly equal). Neutrinos never outrun light or electrons on the step, so bounds on faster-than-light neutrinos do not apply. In free fall the excess appears twice: a massive pattern falls at g c²(1 + rm) (run F1a). A bound pattern's limiting speed is a momentum-weighted average over its parts (Gagnon and Moore 2004), so its excess comes from its parts' mass flips, not its whole mass; the colour field's massless carriers add none. Materials then differ far below MICROSCOPE's 10⁻¹⁵ (run B1 on the earlier walk, by that import; the step's excess is smaller by a factor of about 0.6). Once loops are added, a lattice with a preferred frame generates species-dependent speeds of order α/π (Collins et al. 2004); nothing in the rules suppresses them. They are held equal by hand-set counter-terms, one per species and handedness, the same in every direction (chosen, for now; README, the frame problem).

**The mass counter-term (tuning 2), candidate removal.** A Wilson term breaks chiral symmetry, so loops would shift each mass additively by order (α/π)ħ/(ℓc). Bound to a wall in a short inner direction (an inner ring of 2L places carrying two walls in the mass, ±0.5), matter's step binds one one-way mode at each wall; together they are a light species. A disturbance that gives the plain step a mass of order its strength leaves the wall pair's mass falling exponentially with L: on the step, e^(−0.41L) undisturbed and 0.43–0.48 per place under disturbances of 40% of the bulk gap (runs B3, B3b; a disturbance as large as the gap can break it); the same held on the earlier walk (runs B2–B2f). A physical mass enters as a spin-turning wall-to-wall coupling. The two handednesses' speeds are not protected (speed counter-terms per handedness). Candidate (chosen). In three space dimensions (the step with a fourth, inner direction; free fermions, run B4) the light states are one left-handed fermion on one wall and one right-handed on the other, at light's speed, the leftover mass falling at 0.405 per place; interactions not yet included.

**Charge.** A relation carries a which-way phase that reverses with direction, e^{iA}: a U(1) link. Its unique quadratic cost is Maxwell's. Charge is a sign a pattern carries on the relations it uses; each hop carries e^{±iqA}, which is minimal coupling and gives g = 2 at tree level. The antiparticle is the same pattern with the opposite sign. The strength, 1/137, is from data.

**Statistics.** Two patterns of one kind reach the same configuration by a direct and an exchanged route. In three dimensions, with no coincidence and a flat exchange factor (no stray phase around the network's elementary squares, once the which-way phase is removed), the relative factor can only be ±1 (Leinaas and Myrheim 1977). Which sign: if the exchange factor is identified with the places' frames carrying the internal state through the swap (posited, candidate), the swap deforms into one pattern turning a full turn against the other, and since the walk's state is a spinor the factor is (−1)^{2s}: −1 for spin ½ (electrons, helium-3: exclude), +1 for whole spin (helium-4, rubidium-87, photons: merge). Assumes the nucleons are walk patterns and the photon turns with whole spin. For three or more patterns, parastatistics is not excluded. The walk second-quantised in the standard way admits only anticommutators (Pauli 1940), consistent with this.

## Two patterns

**What a relation carries.** One number per configuration of what passes, not one number. The one-number reading is mean-field, and helium excludes it (−77.9 eV against −79.0 eV). With per-configuration values, two Dirac walks have the two-body Dirac–Coulomb operator as their continuum limit, and helium's −79.0 eV (Hylleraas 1929) by identification.

**Bound and emitted.** The part of the which-way field fixed at the same moment by the positions, currents and spins of what passes is bound and acts on the pair amplitude; the part that has left its sources is emitted, and is light. At order v²/c² the bound part adds the Breit interaction. By identification:
- positronium's levels, −6.80 eV/n², and the magnetic part of its hyperfine split, α⁴mc²/3 = 116.8 GHz;
- hydrogen with recoil, and its 21 cm line, 1418.8 MHz at tree level (measured 1420.406 MHz; the gap is mainly the electron's anomalous moment, which needs loops; the proton's moment imported).

Number change (identification with lattice QED, candidate; costs: the filled states' vacuum energy, and loops, which bring the frame problem): the walk read as a field with filled negative states, so the which-way coupling makes a pair from light carrying at least 2mc² and ends one releasing light, conserving charge and net count; lifetimes that change kinds need the weak interaction, which CO lacks.

**Branching.** A carried quantity is branched with what sets it, if what sets it has amplitude. Possibility in transit has amplitude; free possibility has no cycle and so none. The which-way flux is branched; the free stock and the clock field are classical.

**The dents.** Each renewal leaves a dent of δħ in the free stock, drawn independently from |a|², carrying no which-way information. A pattern left alone is not resolved. The dents do not act back on an unresolved pattern: gravity's source follows engagements (SPACE_AND_GRAVITY, "Classical source"), so there is no Schrödinger–Newton term. The nearest established relative is measurement-and-feedback classical–quantum dynamics (Tilloy–Diósi 2016, without gravity's own engagements).

## Measurement

**Engagement.** A commitment may engage a quantum in transit as part of the ħ it engages, its rate or motion changing by that quantum's frequency and direction. The pattern whose transit it was is resolved; what shares a joint amplitude with it is conditioned (the Lüders update, imported with the joint law; the coupling and pointer basis also imported).

**Channels.** Only through a channel: a final state of the engager, with its width Γ. Engagement follows the line shape, κ(Δ) ∝ (Γ/2)²/(Δ² + (Γ/2)²) near the line; with no final state, or a zero matrix element, there is none. A rate needs the final state to be broad against the coupling, 4|V|²/(ħ²Γ) for |V| ≪ ħΓ/4; for |V| ≫ ħΓ/4 passage stays coherent, as in vacuum Rabi oscillation. The line between them is sharp: an engager's quantum coupled to a final state of width Γ oscillates back and forth when |V| > ħΓ/4 and only leaks away when |V| < ħΓ/4 (for a source of width γ, the line is |V| = ħ|Γ − γ|/4), the exceptional point (for γ = 0 also the usual strong–weak coupling boundary; with γ, strong coupling is often quoted as |V| > ħ(Γ + γ)/4). The frequency and direction matching (with recoil) follow from the walk and the which-way phase. The place of an engagement is drawn from |a|², resolved only as finely as the channel is localised.

**Which contacts are engagements** (chosen, candidate). A contact through a channel is an engagement when its final state is broad against the coupling (|V| < ħΓ/4: the contact does not oscillate back) and the chain it opens reaches N quanta; before that it is a passage and can be undone. Engagements trigger both the Lüders update and gravity's source. N is a constant of CO's own (posited): not derived, set only by experiment. Exchanges between patterns inside one body are passages, and they count toward a chain's breadth (the earlier N rulings). So a single pattern left alone is never resolved, but a body of many patterns commits internally once a chain inside it reaches N; the place of such a commitment is an independent draw that records nothing about where the body is (chosen), so its interference is unaffected, and gravity's source stays where the last outside engagement put it.

**What stays open.** The value of N, a chain's breadth in quanta, bounded below by NMR reversals of correlated-spin clusters. Where the resolved pattern's possibility elsewhere goes.

**Bound states on a fixed source.** One electron on a fixed Z = 1 source has the Dirac–Coulomb operator as its continuum limit; its levels are Dirac's, with the electron mass and 1/137 as input.
