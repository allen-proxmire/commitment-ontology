## The addition

To the terms and rules of [`SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md`](SPACE_AND_GRAVITY.md), one statement: **commitments happen at a rate.** Matter has a phase, where it is in its cycle, and a pattern in transit carries it.

## One pattern

**Amplitudes.** Two routes bring amounts p₁, p₂ to a place with phase difference Δθ. Conservation, scalelessness and associativity leave one rule (Goyal, Knuth and Skilling 2010):

$$p=\left|\sqrt{p_1}\,e^{i\theta_1}+\sqrt{p_2}\,e^{i\theta_2}\right|^2 .$$

**The Born rule.** The thing that commits is identified with the thing conserved in passing: the chance of a commitment at a place is the possibility there. An identification, which a reader may call the postulate restated.

**The internal state.** A homogeneous, unitary walk that moves cannot be scalar (Meyer 1996). With three anticommuting shifts and a mass step anticommuting with all three, the smallest representation is C⁴: Dirac's α and β.

**Mass.** The mass step turns the internal state without moving it, with amplitude i·mcℓ/ħ per hop (Feynman's checkerboard weight). The heaviest pattern flips once per hop, so its Compton wavelength is the hop length:

$$\ell=\frac{\hbar}{m_{\max}c},\qquad \ell\lesssim 1.3\times10^{-31}\ \text{m}.$$

With ℓ = ℓ_P√(4π/δ) this gives δ ≳ 2 × 10⁻⁷ and a heaviest pattern above 10¹⁵ GeV.

**The Dirac equation.** Possibility hopping at c with the four-component state, flipping at the mass rate, has the Dirac equation as its continuum limit (Bialynicki-Birula 1994). Spin ½ follows; g = 2 follows with minimal coupling to the which-way field. This identifies the rules with a known construction; it is not an independent derivation.

**The step.** The step is symmetrised, x, y, z, flip, z, y, x (Strang 1968). The sequential order gives a handedness-dependent term that Crab electrons exclude (run L1). A strictly local Wilson term, splitting each shift into two half-shifts with a turn between, gives the lattice's extra species a mass of order wħ/(ℓc), above 10¹⁴ GeV, and leaves one light species (run L1b). Those heavy species are physical particles with the electron's quantum numbers.

**Lorentz invariance.** The Wilson term gives a massive pattern a limiting speed above light's by s ≈ 0.14·mcℓ/ħ (at w = 0.3), the same in every direction. Crab electrons (s < 5 × 10⁻²⁰) then need ℓ ≲ 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ m. Once loops are added, a lattice with a preferred frame generates species-dependent speeds of order α/π (Collins et al. 2004); nothing in the rules suppresses them. This is CO's open problem.

**Charge.** A relation carries a which-way phase that reverses with direction, e^{iA}: a U(1) link. Its unique quadratic cost is Maxwell's. Charge is a sign a pattern carries on the relations it uses; each hop carries e^{±iqA}, which is minimal coupling and gives g = 2 at tree level. The antiparticle is the same pattern with the opposite sign. The strength, 1/137, is from data.

**Statistics.** Two patterns of one kind reach the same configuration by a direct and an exchanged route. In three dimensions, with no coincidence and a flat exchange factor (no stray phase around the network's elementary squares, once the which-way phase is removed), the relative factor can only be ±1 (Leinaas and Myrheim 1977). Which sign: if the exchange factor is identified with the places' frames carrying the internal state through the swap (posited, candidate), the swap deforms into one pattern turning a full turn against the other, and since the walk's state is a spinor the factor is (−1)^{2s}: −1 for spin ½ (electrons, helium-3: exclude), +1 for whole spin (helium-4, rubidium-87, photons: merge). Assumes the nucleons are walk patterns and the photon turns with whole spin. For three or more patterns, parastatistics is not excluded. The walk second-quantised in the standard way admits only anticommutators (Pauli 1940), consistent with this.

## Two patterns

**What a relation carries.** One number per configuration of what passes, not one number. The one-number reading is mean-field, and helium excludes it (−77.9 eV against −79.0 eV). With per-configuration values, two Dirac walks have the two-body Dirac–Coulomb operator as their continuum limit, and helium's −79.0 eV (Hylleraas 1929) by identification.

**Bound and emitted.** The part of the which-way field fixed at the same moment by the positions, currents and spins of what passes is bound and acts on the pair amplitude; the part that has left its sources is emitted, and is light. At order v²/c² the bound part adds the Breit interaction. By identification:
- positronium's levels, −6.80 eV/n², and the magnetic part of its hyperfine split, α⁴mc²/3 = 116.8 GHz;
- hydrogen with recoil, and its 21 cm line, 1418.8 MHz at tree level (measured 1420.406 MHz; the gap is mainly the electron's anomalous moment, which needs loops; the proton's moment imported).

Annihilation and lifetimes need number change: needed (pairs are made and annihilated in the laboratory and in the hot early universe), its rule not written.

**Branching.** A carried quantity is branched with what sets it, if what sets it has amplitude. Possibility in transit has amplitude; free possibility has no cycle and so none. The which-way flux is branched; the free stock and the clock field are classical.

**The dents.** Each renewal leaves a dent of δħ in the free stock, drawn independently from |a|², carrying no which-way information. A pattern left alone is not resolved. The dents act back through h, a Schrödinger–Newton term at G as measured. The nearest established relative is Tilloy–Diósi 2016.

## Measurement

**Engagement.** A commitment may engage a quantum in transit as part of the ħ it engages, its rate or motion changing by that quantum's frequency and direction. The pattern whose transit it was is resolved; what shares a joint amplitude with it is conditioned (the Lüders update, imported with the joint law; the coupling and pointer basis also imported).

**Channels.** Only through a channel: a final state of the engager, with its width Γ. Engagement follows the line shape, κ(Δ) ∝ (Γ/2)²/(Δ² + (Γ/2)²) near the line; with no final state, or a zero matrix element, there is none. A rate needs the final state to be broad against the coupling, 4|V|²/(ħ²Γ) for |V| ≪ ħΓ; for |V| ≫ ħΓ passage stays coherent, as in vacuum Rabi oscillation. The frequency and direction matching (with recoil) follow from the walk and the which-way phase. The place of an engagement is drawn from |a|², resolved only as finely as the channel is localised.

**What stays open.** Which contacts through a channel are engagements rather than passages: a chain with a stopping number N counting its breadth in quanta, bounded below by NMR reversals of correlated-spin clusters. Where the resolved pattern's possibility elsewhere goes.

**Bound states on a fixed source.** One electron on a fixed Z = 1 source has the Dirac–Coulomb operator as its continuum limit; its levels are Dirac's, with the electron mass and 1/137 as input.

## Negative results

No classical current gives g = 2 (loops give g = 1); spin from orbital circulation is not ħ/2; the lattice loop is not the electron's orbit. The one-way hop is excluded: it gives the electron a lifetime of about 10⁻¹⁵ s. A relational lattice alone does not remove the doublers (Christ, Friedberg and Lee 1982).
