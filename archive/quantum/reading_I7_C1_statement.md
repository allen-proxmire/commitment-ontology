## Reading I7, candidate C1 in full: route (b) placed by the medium

*28 October 2026. Allen: "do C1". Written out before any value was computed, then checked (`checkPre97/`) and registered in Run 97, where its computable parts are tested (`run97_section.md`). §5 is superseded by PREREG_97: C1a turned out to be a renaming with no test, and C1b is tested against a species window and the relic-neutrino bound.*

### 1. The new statement

**(C1) A route-(b) commitment, having no partner to resolve it, is placed by the medium: as sharply as the volume of places whose vacuum possibility amounts to one hold.**

Reading I6 ruling 2 already says a commitment is placed only as sharply as its interactions resolve, and ruling 3 exempts route (b), which "localises at r_C". C1 supplies the r_C that ruling 3 leaves open; it does not extend ruling 2 to route (b), whose exemption stands. It is a new ruling; the record does not have it. A second new statement comes with it: R2 counts a hold's possibility at a commitment's two places over ticks, and C1 counts it over a volume of places.

### 2. Consistency with the statement that a pattern's own possibility fills its hold

Reading I4 §2 (Allen, 27 October): a route-(b) hold is filled by the possibility the pattern itself carries. C1 does not change that. It separates two roles:
- **filling** (when the commitment completes): the pattern's own stock, as ruled, which gives κ_b = 1/(aP∞);
- **placing** (how sharply the record is fixed): the medium, which C1 adds.

### 3. Two readings of "one hold"

R2's hold is H = 2ħ_tP∞: possibility present at a commitment's two places, summed over ħ_t ticks. "The volume of places whose vacuum possibility amounts to one hold" can be read two ways.

**(C1a) One hold, at one moment.** The places whose vacuum possibility *present in one tick* adds up to H:
$$
N_VP_\infty=H=2\hbar_tP_\infty\quad\Rightarrow\quad N_V=2\hbar_t .
$$
The hold's two places over ħ_t ticks are traded for 2ħ_t places over one tick. The volume N_Vℓ³ is then matched to the localisation's width r_C by a convention fixed before computing, for example:
$$
\text{(i) equal Gaussian volume: }(2\pi)^{3/2}r_C^3=N_V\ell^3;\qquad \text{(ii) a ball of radius }r_C:\ \tfrac{4\pi}{3}r_C^3=N_V\ell^3 .
$$
The two differ by a factor (4π/3)^{1/3}/(2π)^{1/2} ≈ 0.64 in r_C.
- **Species:** one r_C for every species, as Reading I5 ruled (D4 met).
- **Scales:** r_C depends on ℓ and ħ_t, not on P∞. So C1a does not tie the coarseness to the rate's scale.

**(C1b) One hold, over the hold's own duration.** The places whose vacuum possibility, *summed over the route-(b) hold's duration*, adds up to the hold. With the hold completing at λ = κ_bE/ħ (Reading I4), the arithmetic gives
$$
N_V=\frac{2\hbar_t\,\tau\,E}{a\,P_\infty\,\hbar}=\frac{2E}{E_{\rm pair}P_\infty},\qquad E_{\rm pair}=\frac{a\hbar}{\hbar_t\tau},
$$
with τ = ℓ/c the tick: the volume of vacuum that holds as much possibility as the pattern carries. **C1b is candidate C2.** It ties r_C to P∞, but it depends on the unit's energy E, so electrons and nucleons get different r_C (D4 fails unless a ruling allows it).
