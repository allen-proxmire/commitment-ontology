# CO on one page

*Grades: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), needed (forced by data or consistency), posited (decided, not derived), excluded, shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak); "the author's ruling" marks a choice.*

**What it is.** An ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment. Possibility sits at places on a network and moves between related places; a commitment is an irreversible event that engages it. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow. Matter moves as a quantum walk on the network: one local step taken in mirror order, with a small turn between half-steps that gives the lattice's extra copies of each particle a mass at the hop scale (w = 5π/16, posited).

**What it reaches.**
- *Gravity.* Newton's law, redshift and free fall; light bending, the Shapiro delay and Mercury's precession once data fix γ = 1 (Cassini) and β = 1 (a fit to Mercury). At linear order Einstein's cost is forced, given no backdrop, locality and two derivatives. Beyond it, Einstein's equations are the only consistent continuation (Wald; Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux), given no backdrop at every order and Lorentz-invariant interactions (continuum; the nonlinear cost on the lattice is open). At weak field, Regge's form of the full cost reduces on the lattice to the linear cost (Euclidean; the cell's cut open).
- *Gravity on CO's own walk.* With the field entering the walk through its flips and a directed hop (the author's ruling, candidate), weak-field tests in one and two dimensions match Einstein's: free fall, every mass alike; light bending twice the clock-only value once γ = 1; frame dragging. Patterns that source a field evolving on the lattice attract as Newton says, only after the field arrives (1D and 2D, semiclassical, weak field). With strong fields entering as the shift alone (the author's ruling, candidate), a black hole holds on the 2D walk, and on a radial reduction of the walk a collapsing ball of matter forms one, its shells crossing their horizons within 0.6% of GR's times. These agree with GR; no new number.
- *Quantum mechanics and matter.* The walk has the Dirac equation as its long-wavelength limit, with spin ½, by identification with a known quantum walk. Maxwell's equations come from the which-way phase on relations. The Born rule is an identification, not a derivation. Helium, positronium, the 21 cm line, and pair creation (candidate) come by identification with standard constructions. Colour is a split into three parts (C9, the author's ruling, candidate: odd is forced, three is the least choice), its field's cost and coupling posited; the hadron spectrum and the nucleon's mass come by identification with lattice QCD (candidate; conditional on C9, the tunings and the quark masses as inputs). The weak interaction is absent.
- *Cosmology.* A beginning in which every place is over capacity and commits at once, ending as an inflation-like epoch (shape). The ratio of matter to light measures how far over capacity the first commitments were (shape; its value open). Friedmann's eras. The first ripples: a silence.

**What goes in by hand.** Two primitives and their qualities (P1–P8, C1–C9, each posited or candidate). Four constants of CO's own (δ, P_max, Λ and the stopping number N) and a birth share. Measured inputs: G, c, ħ, 1/137, the masses. From quantum mechanics: Lüders conditioning, the joint law of an entangled pair, an interaction's coupling and pointer basis. For gravity beyond linear order: locality, two derivatives, Lorentz-invariant interactions. Tunings, accepted for now: counter-terms that keep every species' top speed equal; a counter-term per fermion mass, unless matter is a domain-wall walk (candidate, shown in one space dimension without interactions); and an improved lattice form for every field between places (light, gluons, gravity), so that none slows at short wavelength (the window needed by cosmic rays, the zero posited); and counter-terms by direction for light's electric and magnetic terms, since the palindrome's nesting order breaks the cube's symmetry in loops (needed).

**How it could fail.**
- *Photons that split.* In standard physics a photon never decays. In CO, light runs straight while electrons bend slightly at short wavelength, so photons above a threshold split into electron pairs: about 10 PeV if the hop is at its upper bound and the speed counter-terms leave the mass-dependent speed excess in place (higher if the hop is smaller). A confirmed photon above about 300 PeV excludes CO's form of light at every allowed hop, whatever the counter-terms do. LHAASO's highest so far is 2.5 PeV. A candidate test, conditional on a composite's speed excess following its parts (an imported result); light's straight form is itself a tuning.
- *Gravity that entangles.* Gravity's source in CO follows a body's engagements, not its unresolved state (the author's ruling, candidate), so two masses cannot become entangled through gravity alone, as in every classical-gravity model. Observing it (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto and Vedral 2017) would falsify CO's gravity.
- *A universal stopping number.* Measurement completes once a contact's chain reaches N quanta, N a constant of CO's own (posited, set only by experiment; C8, the author's ruling, candidate). As a many-body echo is reversed more exactly, the number of spins the disturbance has spread over when the echo is lost should stop growing at the same N in every system; quantum mechanics has it keep growing to the system's size. A system-dependent ceiling would falsify it. Present data give N ≳ 50.

**Bounds the data already set.** Hop length ℓ ≲ 3 × 10⁻³² m (Crab electrons), so δ ≳ 3.5 × 10⁻⁶; the hop's order symmetric; no scalar gravitational wave; no one-way hop; no empty newborn places.

**What it does not do.** No masses, no 1/137, no weak interaction, no reason for three generations; three colours only as a choice. No tested prediction that differs from established physics, and no number matched where the standard theories are silent. Its results come through standard theorems: where a quality is a choice, the result is a reconstruction, not a derivation. The walk's gravity runs are small and in one and two dimensions (the collapse a radial reduction); three dimensions and strong fields on the walk are open. The tunings are its weakest point: a network that picks a frame needs them, no mechanism in CO yet removes them, and CO stands at tree level.
