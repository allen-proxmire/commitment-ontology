# CO on one page

*Grades: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), needed (forced by data or consistency), posited (decided, not derived), chosen (a reading the rules leave open, picked), excluded, shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak).*

**What it is.** An ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment. Possibility sits at places on a network and moves between related places; a commitment is an irreversible event that engages it. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow. Matter moves by one step per tick: a short flow of the lattice Dirac Hamiltonian that treats every direction of the cube alike, with a Wilson term giving the lattice's extra copies of each particle a mass at the hop scale (chosen, candidate; r = ½ posited). The lattice is a stand-in for the web of relations: the simplest web to compute on, not a claim that the web is a regular grid with a rest frame of its own.

## Where each result comes from

- Gravity on CO's own step, in one and two dimensions (the field entering through the clock, the hop's stretch and a directed drift; chosen, candidate): free fall with every mass alike up to the lattice factor (1 + rm), m in hop units; light bending twice the clock-only value, also past a point mass; two patterns whose fields cross at light's speed and pull as Newton says; a radial fall through a horizon on the path of a body dropped from infinity; the shift's gravitomagnetic and Coriolis pushes; a 2D black hole, light escaping from outside its horizon and swept in from inside; a ball of matter sourcing its own drift, collapsing toward GR's dust path, closer as the cloud grows, and crossing its horizons on time (radial reduction); a spinning ring dragging the frames inside it in its own sense (2D analogue of Thirring's). In three dimensions, in a weak uniform field: free fall the same along a cube axis and a body diagonal to 10⁻⁵, and light bending twice the clock-only value, also past a point mass.

**What needs the lattice.** The lattice adds two things to a web of relations: regular axes, and a rest frame (one tick common to every place).
- *Neither a lattice nor a smallest length:* one-way time, the clock rate, redshift and free fall, the moving clock, gravity's source following engagements, the stopping number N and its test, the shared commitment in a nucleon; Einstein's equations by identification (continuum; assumes Lorentz-invariant interactions, which a web with a frame does not give for free).
- *A smallest length ℓ:* G's form, δc³ℓ²/ħ (the 4π is the cubic lattice's); a place's capacity; the first ball's count.
- *The lattice as the web computed on* (candidate that another web with the same long-wavelength form gives the same; shown for a free massless pattern on a few uneven webs in two dimensions, [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_web.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_web.md)): Newton's 1/r and three dimensions, Dirac's and Maxwell's equations, the atoms, confinement by identification, linearised Einstein, the gravity runs on matter's step. The extra copies of each particle are here too: an uneven web alone does not remove them.
- *A rest frame* (every web run so far has one): the photon ceiling, the neutrino flavour term, the synchrotron bound, the bounds on ℓ and δ drawn from them, free fall's factor (1 + rm), the tunings below.
- *Regular axes as well:* the ceiling's sky pattern; isotropy only up to (kℓ)².

Now is the commitment (reading). Two things share a now only when they commit together: in an engagement (C8), or in the one commitment of a bound whole. A common tick is a now for the whole lattice; what orders two commitments on a web without one is open, and no run has been made without a common tick.

**Tunings.** These come with the lattice's rest frame; that a web without one is free of them is not shown. The lattice's frame is kept in the stand-in (the speed tuning, chosen): it carries the photon and neutrino tests, and the one exit on the lattice (coordinates that do not commute) would remove the photon test, in Snyder's form the neutrino test too, along with places definite in every direction.
- The lattice picks a rest frame. Its loops would give species different top speeds; counter-terms set by hand keep them equal to the 10⁻²⁰ the data require (one per species and handedness, the same in every direction). CO stands at tree level; the one exit known on a lattice is a network whose coordinates do not commute.
- A counter-term per fermion mass, unless matter is the mode bound to a wall in a short inner direction (candidate; one-handed at each wall in three space dimensions, free fermions; no interactions yet).
- A straight lattice form for every field between places (light, gluons, gravity), so none slows at short wavelength (the window needed by cosmic rays, the zero posited).

**Shapes.** G's form, G = δc³ℓ²/(4πħ), resting on a relation's stiffness equalling the hop's conductance; a beginning in which every place is over capacity and commits at once, ending as an inflation-like epoch; the ratio of matter to light set by that beginning (its value open); dark matter as a pattern that renews with no channel (given CO's present inventory a fermion, so, if the shape holds, not ultralight, m ≥ 0.13 keV; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)).

**Silences.** The weak interaction, three generations, the first ripples.

## How it could fail
- *Gravity that entangles.* Gravity's source follows a body's engagements, not its unresolved state (chosen, candidate), so two masses cannot become entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto and Vedral 2017).
- *A stopping number that isn't universal.* Measurement completes once a contact's chain reaches N quanta (N posited; C8 chosen, candidate). As a many-body echo is reversed more exactly, the number of spins the disturbance has spread over when the echo is lost should stop growing at the same N in every system; quantum mechanics has it keep growing to the system's size. A system-dependent ceiling would falsify it. Present data give N ≳ 50. No wall in size is predicted: a body left alone is not resolved in position however heavy, as in quantum mechanics. This rests on commitments inside a body recording nothing of where it is (candidate); the interference of sodium clusters above 170 kDa, each with far more than N quanta inside (Pedalino et al., Nature 649, 866 (2026)), makes that clause needed.
- *Photons that split* (candidate test of the lattice, not of CO's core: it needs the web of relations to have a rest frame of its own, as the lattice has; the sky pattern needs the lattice's regular axes as well). Light runs straight while electrons bend slightly at short wavelength, so photons above E_th = C√(mₑc²·ħc/ℓ) split into electron pairs, C = 2.9–5.1 by direction: about 9–16 PeV at the candidate neutrino bound, higher if the hop is smaller. The ceiling is the same for every source in a direction, whatever its distance, and varies across the sky by up to 1.75 in the cube's pattern (if the lattice's axes hold their direction along the path, which is not shown; if they wander, one ceiling for the whole sky, the lowest, and the figures here take their axis values: 1.2 × 10¹⁷ eV, ℓ ≲ 7.8 × 10⁻³² m). A confirmed photon above about 2.1 × 10¹⁷ eV excludes CO's form of light on the lattice at every allowed hop (ℓ cannot be below ℓ_P√(4π) ≈ 5.7 × 10⁻³⁵ m, since δ ≤ 1). LHAASO's highest so far: 3.7 PeV. Conditional on a composite's speed excess following its parts (an imported result); light's straight form is itself a tuning.

## Bounds and exclusions the data already set

- If the web has the lattice's rest frame: hop length ℓ ≲ 2.4 × 10⁻³¹ m, δ ≳ 6 × 10⁻⁸ (LHAASO's 3.7 PeV photon from Cygnus X-3 has not split; needed); candidate ℓ ≲ 1.0 × 10⁻³² m, δ ≳ 3 × 10⁻⁵ (IceCube's atmospheric neutrinos show no extra flavour change growing with energy; given a massive pattern's speed excess kept, chosen, and neutrinos as patterns on the step with Dirac masses, the weak interaction being absent, the lightest near zero).
- Excluded: a sequential hop order (on the earlier walk), a scalar gravitational wave, a one-way hop, a newborn place left empty, ticks that come by chance (atomic-clock coherence, if h counts every mass).

## What it does not do

Quantum mechanics only for a fixed number of particles (with pair creation by identification, candidate). No weak interaction, no reason for three generations; three colours only as a choice. No tested prediction that differs from established physics, and no number matched where the standard theories are silent. Where a quality is a choice, the result is a reconstruction, not a derivation. The gravity runs on matter's step (CO's lattice Dirac walk) are in one and two dimensions, with free fall and light bending in a weak uniform field in three; three dimensions beyond that (a spinning body's exterior and Kerr among them) and strong fields beyond a 2D hole and a radial collapse are open. The tunings are its weakest point.
