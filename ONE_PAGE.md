# CO on one page

*Grades: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), needed (forced by data or consistency), posited (decided, not derived), chosen (a reading the rules leave open, picked), excluded, shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak).*

**What it is.** An ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment. Possibility sits at places on a network and moves between related places; a commitment is an irreversible event that engages it. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow. Matter moves by one step per tick: a short flow of the lattice Dirac Hamiltonian that treats every direction of the cube alike, with a Wilson term giving the lattice's extra copies of each particle a mass at the hop scale (chosen, candidate; r = ½ posited).

## How it could fail
- *Gravity that entangles.* Gravity's source follows a body's engagements, not its unresolved state (chosen, candidate), so two masses cannot become entangled through gravity alone (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto and Vedral 2017).
- *A stopping number that isn't universal.* Measurement completes once a contact's chain reaches N quanta (N posited; C8 chosen, candidate). As a many-body echo is reversed more exactly, the number of spins the disturbance has spread over when the echo is lost should stop growing at the same N in every system; quantum mechanics has it keep growing to the system's size. A system-dependent ceiling would falsify it. Present data give N ≳ 50.
- *Photons that split* (candidate test; a side effect of one choice, keeping a lattice with a real frame, not part of CO's core). Light runs straight while electrons bend slightly at short wavelength, so photons above E_th = C√(mₑc²·ħc/ℓ) split into electron pairs, C = 2.9–5.1 by direction: about 9–16 PeV at the candidate neutrino bound, higher if the hop is smaller. The ceiling is the same for every source in a direction, whatever its distance, and varies across the sky by up to 1.75 in the cube's pattern. A confirmed photon above about 2.1 × 10¹⁷ eV excludes CO's form of light at every allowed hop (ℓ cannot be below ℓ_P√(4π) ≈ 5.7 × 10⁻³⁵ m, since δ ≤ 1). LHAASO's highest so far: 3.7 PeV. Conditional on a composite's speed excess following its parts (an imported result); light's straight form is itself a tuning.

## Bounds and exclusions the data already set

- Hop length ℓ ≲ 2.4 × 10⁻³¹ m, δ ≳ 6 × 10⁻⁸ (LHAASO's 3.7 PeV photon from Cygnus X-3 has not split; needed); candidate ℓ ≲ 1.0 × 10⁻³² m, δ ≳ 3 × 10⁻⁵ (IceCube's atmospheric neutrinos show no extra flavour change growing with energy; given a massive pattern's speed excess kept, chosen, and neutrinos as patterns on the step with Dirac masses, the weak interaction being absent, the lightest near zero).
- Excluded: a sequential hop order (on the earlier walk), a scalar gravitational wave, a one-way hop, a newborn place left empty, ticks that come by chance (atomic-clock coherence, if h counts every mass).

## What it does not do

Quantum mechanics only for a fixed number of particles (with pair creation by identification, candidate). No masses, no 1/137, no weak interaction, no reason for three generations; three colours only as a choice. No tested prediction that differs from established physics, and no number matched where the standard theories are silent. Where a quality is a choice, the result is a reconstruction, not a derivation. The gravity runs on matter's step (CO's lattice Dirac walk) are in one and two dimensions, with free fall and light bending in a weak uniform field in three; three dimensions beyond that (a spinning body's exterior and Kerr among them) and strong fields beyond a 2D hole and a radial collapse are open. The tunings are its weakest point.
