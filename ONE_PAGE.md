# CO on one page

*Grades: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), needed (forced by data or consistency), posited (decided, not derived), excluded, shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak); "the author's ruling" marks a choice.*

**What it is.** An ontology with two primitives, possibility and commitment. Possibility sits at places on a network and moves between related places; a commitment is an irreversible event that engages it. Time is the count of commitments, matter is what keeps re-committing, and gravity is the pull toward where commitments run slow. Matter moves by one step per tick on the network, a short flow of the lattice Dirac Hamiltonian that treats every direction of the cube alike; a small Wilson term gives the lattice's extra copies of each particle a mass at the hop scale (r = ½, posited).

**What it reaches.**
- *Gravity.* Newton's law, redshift and free fall; light bending, the Shapiro delay and Mercury's precession once data fix γ = 1 (Cassini) and β = 1 (a fit to Mercury). At linear order Einstein's cost is forced, given no backdrop, locality and two derivatives. Beyond it, Einstein's equations are the only consistent continuation (Wald; Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux), given no backdrop at every order and Lorentz-invariant interactions (continuum; the nonlinear cost on the lattice is open). At weak field, Regge's form of the full cost reduces on the lattice to the linear cost (Euclidean; the cell's cut open).
- *Gravity on CO's own step.* With the field entering matter's step through the clock, the hop's stretch and a directed drift (the author's ruling, candidate), tests in one and two dimensions match Einstein's: free fall, every mass alike up to the lattice's small factor; light bending twice the clock-only value; patterns that source a field evolving on the lattice attract as Newton says, only after the field arrives; with the shift alone, a radial fall through a black hole's horizon on the path of a body dropped from infinity. Frame dragging, a 2D black hole and a collapsing ball forming one (shells crossing their horizons within 0.6% of GR's times) were shown on the earlier palindromic walk, not rerun. These agree with GR; no new number.
- *Quantum mechanics and matter.* Matter's step, one tick of the lattice Dirac flow with a Wilson term (candidate; r = ½ posited), has the Dirac equation as its long-wavelength limit, with spin ½, by identification with lattice Dirac fermions; it is symmetric under the cube's rotations and reflections, so every two-index response (light's permittivity and permeability, the speed tensors) is the same in every direction. Maxwell's equations come from the which-way phase on relations. The Born rule is an identification, not a derivation. Helium, positronium, the 21 cm line, and pair creation (candidate) come by identification with standard constructions. Colour is a split into three parts (C9, the author's ruling, candidate: odd is forced, three is the least choice), its field's cost and coupling posited; the hadron spectrum and the nucleon's mass come by identification with lattice QCD (candidate; conditional on C9, the tunings and the quark masses as inputs). The weak interaction is absent.
- *Cosmology.* A beginning in which every place is over capacity and commits at once, ending as an inflation-like epoch (shape). The ratio of matter to light measures how far over capacity the first commitments were (shape; its value open). Friedmann's eras. The first ripples: a silence.

**What goes in by hand.** Two primitives and their qualities (P1–P8, C1–C9, each posited or candidate). Four constants of CO's own (δ, P_max, Λ and the stopping number N) and a birth share. Measured inputs: G, c, ħ, 1/137, the masses. From quantum mechanics: Lüders conditioning, the joint law of an entangled pair, an interaction's coupling and pointer basis. For gravity beyond linear order: locality, two derivatives, Lorentz-invariant interactions. Tunings, accepted for now: counter-terms that keep every species' top speed equal (one per species and handedness, the same in every direction); a counter-term per fermion mass, unless matter is bound to a wall in a short inner direction (candidate, shown in one space dimension without interactions); and an improved lattice form for every field between places (light, gluons, gravity), so that none slows at short wavelength (the window needed by cosmic rays, the zero posited).

**How it could fail.**
- *Photons that split.* In standard physics a photon never decays. In CO, light runs straight while electrons bend slightly at short wavelength, so photons above a threshold split into electron pairs: E_th = C√(mₑc²·ħc/ℓ), C = 2.6–5.1 by direction and by what the speed counter-terms do, about 5–9 PeV at the candidate synchrotron bound on the hop (higher if the hop is smaller). A confirmed photon above about 2 × 10¹⁷ eV excludes CO's form of light at every allowed hop, whatever the counter-terms do. LHAASO's highest so far is 2.5 PeV. A candidate test, conditional on a composite's speed excess following its parts (an imported result); light's straight form is itself a tuning.
- *Gravity that entangles.* Gravity's source in CO follows a body's engagements, not its unresolved state (the author's ruling, candidate), so two masses cannot become entangled through gravity alone, as in every classical-gravity model. Observing it (Bose et al. 2017; Marletto and Vedral 2017) would falsify CO's gravity.
- *A universal stopping number.* Measurement completes once a contact's chain reaches N quanta, N a constant of CO's own (posited, set only by experiment; C8, the author's ruling, candidate). As a many-body echo is reversed more exactly, the number of spins the disturbance has spread over when the echo is lost should stop growing at the same N in every system; quantum mechanics has it keep growing to the system's size. A system-dependent ceiling would falsify it. Present data give N ≳ 50.

**Bounds the data already set.** Hop length ℓ ≲ 4 × 10⁻³¹ m (LHAASO's 2.5 PeV photon has not split), so δ ≳ 2 × 10⁻⁸; candidate ℓ ≲ 3 × 10⁻³² m, δ ≳ 3 × 10⁻⁶ (the Crab synchrotron cutoff, at the standard nebular field); no sequential hop order; no scalar gravitational wave; no one-way hop; no empty newborn places.

**What it does not do.** No masses, no 1/137, no weak interaction, no reason for three generations; three colours only as a choice. No tested prediction that differs from established physics, and no number matched where the standard theories are silent. Its results come through standard theorems: where a quality is a choice, the result is a reconstruction, not a derivation. The gravity runs on matter's step are small, in one and two dimensions (the horizon crossing radial; the collapse shown only on the earlier walk); three dimensions and strong fields beyond a horizon crossing are open. The tunings are its weakest point: a network that picks a frame needs them, no mechanism in CO yet removes them, and CO stands at tree level.
