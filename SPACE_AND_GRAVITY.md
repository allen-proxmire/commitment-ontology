# Space and Gravity

Results only. The working record, with every route tried and every withdrawal, is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## Primitives

| primitive | statement |
|---|---|
| **Possibility** | a quantity held at places, moving between related places, conserved in all passing and committing; each new place is born with its share (candidate); counted in quanta of ħ (units of action) |
| **Commitment** | one irreversible update to possibility, at a place |

Added principles: the substrate is scaleless; sliding or relabelling places costs nothing (there is no backdrop); second-order field equations (no higher-derivative costs).

## Consequences

| term | statement |
|---|---|
| **Renewal** | a commitment that engages adjacent free possibility into its next commitment, releases most of what it held, and adds one to its count. Any commitment does this while free possibility is adjacent; it halts when none is |
| **Matter** | a commitment that keeps being fed. Mass is renewal frequency, ω = mc²/ħ; every renewal engages one quantum ħ |
| **Time** | matter's count. Read only by ratio, N_A/N_B |
| **Record** | what every draw withdraws from the free stock and keeps: δħ per renewal (candidate in this fixed form), and a continuous trace by the mean for travel, light and field energy (candidate). The record only grows: the arrow of time |
| **Pattern** | possibility in transit between commitments; transit keeps its mean direction until its own commitment redirects it (inertia; other patterns bend its route only through the fields they set). Possibility in transit is not free: a closed whole draws its turning once and its travel twice, and light, all travel, draws 2ħω (candidate) |
| **Vacuum** | possibility with no commitment: no count, so no time; no renewal, so no scale |
| **Relation** | who passes possibility to whom. A relation carries an occupancy (how much is in transit across it) and a which-way phase (candidate ruling) |

## Rules

| rule | statement |
|---|---|
| ratio hopping | a hop compares neighbours by ratio, the choice the scaleless substrate favours; with the lapse weighting of a conserved flux it is consistent with GR's exterior (given the cost's spatial equation), and Mercury excludes the other pairings: the field is linear in ln P. P is an amount (|a|² where possibility has an amplitude); the rule acts on amounts, not on phase; each hop moves one quantum, ħ (candidate) |
| capacity | a place holds at most P_max free possibility; over capacity it commits. The vacuum today is under capacity |
| hold-and-release | a renewal engages, completes, releases, less the share kept as record; nothing is destroyed. P_free = P(1 − h), h the fraction engaged |
| clock | renewal rate = (mc²/ħ)(1 − h) |
| gravity | on each relation, net transit runs toward the end that engages more; a place's h is the engaged share its relations have delivered. A link rule, needing no simultaneity. Patterns fall by refraction on the clock-rate field |
| mismatch | relations can be out of step with their ends; the cost depends only on how they fit together (sliding costs nothing) |
| now | what has committed, for a commitment, is its past light cone; spacelike events are not ordered with respect to it. Relativity's own point-relative becoming, with the time orientation supplied by record only growing; nothing added. This holds of the primitives; on the walk the cone is the lattice's, and its finite-degree causal structure picks a frame (`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`). |

## Results

**Clocks.** Two clocks compare as (1 − h_A)/(1 − h_B), independent of their mass: gravitational redshift, universal.

**The field.** Matter is a sink: each renewal retains δħ at the rate mc²/ħ, and each hop moves one quantum, ħ (both candidate), so the sink against the hop's conductance has strength σ = δmcℓ²/ħ. Ratio hopping gives

$$\nabla^2\ln P_{\text{free}}=\sigma\,\delta(\mathbf r)\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{P_{\text{free}}}{P_\infty}=e^{-U},\qquad U=\frac{GM}{c^2 r},$$

with, in hop units,

$$G=\frac{\delta\,c^{3}\,\ell^{2}}{4\pi\hbar}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \ell=\ell_P\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{\delta}} .$$

This is the weak-field form. On the network the occupancies make (g_ij = ψ⁴δ_ij), possibility conserved as a current carries the lapse weighting N, and ratio hopping gives ∂_i(ψ²∂_i N) = 0, GR's static vacuum equation for the lapse; with the cost's ψ and the sink's flux equal to the mass, the exterior is Schwarzschild (candidate; consistency with the cost, not a derivation of it). Its lapse agrees with the line above through U², its spatial factor through U.

**Free fall.** A matter wave of frequency mc²/ħ refracting on the clock-rate field has g = c²∇h (toward higher h, where ticks are slower): Newton's acceleration, mass-independent.

**The weak field.** The same h slows ticks (factor 1 − U + U²/2 …) and lengthens routes (factor e^{U}), giving the isotropic weak-field metric with γ = 1 and β = 1: light bending 4GM/(c²b), the Shapiro delay, and Mercury's precession at the measured value (Mercury selected the hop pairing, so its precession is a fit, not a test). These are standard weak-field gravity, reached from the primitives.

**Gravitational waves and the exterior.** A single scalar field radiates one sixth of GR's quadrupole power and as a breathing mode; both are excluded (Hulse–Taylor, LIGO–Virgo polarization). So relations must carry an occupancy (a length) that can be out of step with its ends. With sliding costing nothing and no higher derivatives (candidate at the linearised level; for the full exterior assumed), Lovelock's theorem fixes the cost of mismatch to

$$S=\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int (R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x,$$

Einstein–Hilbert, in Regge's form on the network: tensor waves with two polarizations and Einstein's quadrupole coefficient. Under this cost the exterior of a body is Schwarzschild, horizon included. **Outside matter, gravity has GR's form (given Lovelock's hypotheses);** the mass that sets it is GR's for dust, and Tolman's, ∫(ρ + S)N√γ d³x, for pressure-supported bodies short of nuclear stress (candidate: travel draws twice; field energy as an amount). G is the relations' stiffness against mismatch, already fixed above; Λ is the one constant the symmetry permits and does not fix, taken from data.

**Hypotheses.** That the occupancies are the only dynamical field. Met by the rules: h is the engaged share a place's relations have delivered (the gravity rule), a functional of matter and of transit on the relations, with no rule of its own; a free-stock wave was set aside by identifying gravitational waves with route shear (R1 excluded it only as the driver of expansion), and a scalar radiating mode is excluded by the wave test above. The clock field is slaved to the occupancies.

**Classical source.** The free stock and h are classical (ruled in [`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md): free possibility has no amplitude), so a superposed mass sources h by its mean, not per branch: Møller–Rosenfeld semiclassical gravity, with Schrödinger–Newton as the Newtonian limit of a pattern's own field. Page–Geilker 1981 excludes the mean source for a body never resolved; CO escapes it only if the engagements a macroscopic body undergoes are commitments (the engagement criterion, open), since renewals resolve nothing and a pattern left alone sources h by its mean. Page and Geilker's second objection (a mean source must be conserved) stands as a cost: a mean source that jumps when a body is resolved is not conserved at the jump, and the Einstein–Hilbert cost above requires a conserved source. CO therefore expects no entanglement through gravity, while the which-way field, set by transit, does entangle: that asymmetry is the ruling's content, and Bose et al. 2017 and Marletto–Vedral 2017 would test it (if two masses become entangled through gravity alone, CO is wrong, not downgraded). Schrödinger–Newton self-gravity is then CO's at G as measured: negligible for atoms (it competes with dispersion only above ~10¹⁰ u) and untested where it would show. The sink's shot noise, with G fixed, comes from the Poisson count of renewals and does not depend on δ: φ ≈ ωU√(τ/Γ) (ω the clock's frequency, U the source's potential at the clock, τ the averaging time, Γ the source's renewal rate), about 10⁻³⁵ rad over one second per nucleon on an electron clock at atomic distance (N nucleons add as √N). Costs: the mean-field term is a deterministic nonlinearity in the amplitude's evolution, which signals faster than light if a far commitment conditions the local source (Gisin 1990; Bahrami et al. 2014) and does not if the local source stays the unconditioned marginal until the far event is in the local past cone; and a classical field coupled to quantum matter by linear dynamics must carry noise visible in Cavendish experiments (Oppenheim et al. 2023), which CO evades only by being nonlinear. Candidate; whether the tensor waves are quantised is not settled by it.

**Three dimensions.** In one or two dimensions relations carry no transverse modes, n_TT = (d+1)(d−2)/2. In even numbers of space dimensions a passing signal leaves a tail, so a commitment has no "where." In four or more there are no stable orbits or atoms, and the uniqueness above admits a free constant. Three is the only dimension in which every rule holds and nothing is added: a consistency condition. The pieces are Ehrenfest's, Huygens' and Lovelock's, as assembled by Tegmark (1997); their standing as consequences of these rules is what is CO's.

**The beginning and an inflation-like epoch (no expansion count or ripple spectrum yet).** All the first possibility free in few places puts every place over capacity, so every place commits, and with no distance yet these first commitments are unordered, none before another; every first commitment is fed, so matter forms immediately; the first renewals pool record and spread possibility over new places until all are under capacity, and commitment stops. Rapid making of places with its own beginning and end, both set by P_max. Candidate: the first places all mutually related; thinning to what each can feed (count), pairing by first passage (lines), and a slide-invariant curvature cost (flatness) shape the adjacency; flatness is dimension-blind (the cubic and triangular lattices both have zero Ollivier curvature), so three is the consistency condition's to pick. Runs support each part at small size; convergence to a clean lattice is not reached. Prior art: Trugenberger's combinatorial quantum gravity.

**Expansion.** The first epoch's outward flow set matter receding; inertia keeps it so; gathering brakes it. Newton's law on a shell gives Friedmann's equation and the matter era,

$$H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{k}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}.$$

The radiation era (candidate): radiation draws twice its energy and falls as a⁻⁴, so the shell's acceleration takes ρ + 3p, Friedmann's coefficient; with ρ ∝ a⁻⁴ its first integral is Friedmann's equation, curvature the constant of integration. Λ from data.

## Constants

δ (retained share), P_max (capacity), Λ: the theory's own, values not given. G, c, ħ: measured. The hop length ℓ is fixed by δ through G.

## Open

The values of δ, P_max and Λ; the walk on the curved network, which carries matter's stress tensor; the mechanism behind the first relating (three dimensions by consistency, not by mechanism); the hole's interior.

## Status

Reconstruction: the sector reproduces weak-field gravity, GR's exterior and tensor waves, and the matter era, from the primitives and the rules above, with Lovelock's theorem doing the work for waves. Where it reaches (outside matter, dust), it makes no prediction that differs from GR; inside pressure-supported matter short of nuclear stress and in the radiation era its source is GR's by two candidate rulings (travel draws twice; field energy as an amount), conditional on the walk's construction on the curved network (matter's stress tensor is the walk's and the field's, candidate) and on a place's whole possibility sitting at the floor (P = P∞, the identification the exterior uses). References: Yilmaz (exponential metric; not a solution of the forced dynamics), Tegmark (1997), Trugenberger (2017–19), Lovelock (1971), Regge (1961).
