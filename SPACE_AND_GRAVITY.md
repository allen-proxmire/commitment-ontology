# Space and Gravity

Results only. Grades are in [`TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md`](TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md); every route tried is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## Terms

| term | statement |
|---|---|
| **Possibility** | a quantity held at places, moving between related places, counted in quanta of ħ. Never made or lost in passing; what a draw takes becomes record; new places are born with their share |
| **Commitment** | one irreversible update to possibility, at a place |
| **Renewal** | a commitment that engages adjacent free possibility into its next commitment and releases what it held, less a share kept as record. It halts when none is adjacent |
| **Matter** | a commitment that keeps being fed. Mass is renewal frequency, ω = mc²/ħ |
| **Time** | matter's count, read only by ratio |
| **Record** | what every draw keeps: δħ per renewal, and a share of travel, light and field energy. Not possibility. It only grows: the arrow of time |
| **Pattern** | possibility in transit between commitments. It keeps its mean direction (inertia). A closed whole draws its turning once and its travel twice; light draws 2ħω |
| **Relation** | who passes possibility to whom. It carries an occupancy (a length, possibly different each way) and a which-way phase |

The substrate is scaleless; sliding or relabelling places costs nothing; field equations are second order.

## Rules

| rule | statement |
|---|---|
| ratio hopping | a hop moves one quantum, ħ, and compares neighbours by ratio: the field is linear in ln P |
| capacity | a place holds at most P_max free possibility; over capacity it commits |
| hold-and-release | P_free = P(1 − h), h the fraction engaged |
| clock | renewal rate (mc²/ħ)(1 − h), following from the commitment cycle under the free-share rule |
| gravity | net transit runs toward the end that engages more; a place's h is the share of its possibility its relations hold. Patterns fall by refraction on the clock-rate field |
| draws set relations | a draw sets the occupancies of the relations around it, against a stiffness of one quantum per relation |
| now | a commitment's now is its past light cone; spacelike events are not ordered with respect to it |

## Results

**Clocks.** Two clocks compare as (1 − h_A)/(1 − h_B), independent of their mass: gravitational redshift, universal.

**The static field.** Matter is a sink: each renewal keeps δħ at the rate mc²/ħ. The relations impose the profile, and ratio hopping across it carries the body's draw. In the static case,

$$\nabla^2\ln P_{\text{free}}=\sigma\,\delta(\mathbf r),\quad \sigma=\frac{\delta Mc\ell^2}{\hbar}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{P_{\text{free}}}{P_\infty}=e^{-U},\qquad U=\frac{GM}{c^2 r},$$

$$G=\frac{\delta\,c^{3}\,\ell^{2}}{4\pi\hbar}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \ell=\ell_P\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{\delta}} .$$

This is a static form: read in time, the inflow is diffusion and far too slow to build or carry a field, so the field is carried by the relations ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md)). On the network the lapse obeys ∂_i(ψ²∂_i N) = 0, GR's static vacuum equation, and the exterior is Schwarzschild: consistency with the cost, not a derivation of it.

**Free fall.** A matter wave refracting on the clock-rate field has g = c²∇h: Newton's acceleration, independent of mass.

**The weak field.** The same h slows ticks (1 − U + U²/2 …) and lengthens routes (e^{U}): the isotropic weak-field metric with γ = 1 and β = 1. Light bending 4GM/(c²b), the Shapiro delay, and Mercury's precession (a fit: Mercury selected the hop pairing).

**Waves and the exterior.** A scalar field alone would radiate a sixth of GR's quadrupole power as a breathing mode; both are excluded (Hulse–Taylor, LIGO–Virgo). So relations carry a length that can be out of step with its ends. With sliding free and no higher derivatives (shown at the linearised level, assumed for the full exterior), Lovelock's theorem fixes the cost of mismatch:

$$S=\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int (R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x .$$

Tensor waves with two polarizations and Einstein's quadrupole coefficient; Schwarzschild outside a body. The mass that sets the exterior is GR's for dust and Tolman's, ∫(ρ + S)N√γ d³x, for pressure-supported bodies short of nuclear stress, given the walk on the curved network and P = P∞ at a place (an identification).

**What carries each part.** Lapse: the share each place's relations hold. Stretch: the relations' lengths. Frame dragging: the one-way part of the occupancy, which behaves like a U(1) link (only its loops are physical). Shear: a frame at each place. Each is set by the matching part of the source: ρ + S, the diagonal stress, the momentum density, the off-diagonal stress.

**Classical source.** The free stock and h are classical, so a superposed mass sources h by its mean: Møller–Rosenfeld semiclassical gravity, with Schrödinger–Newton as its Newtonian limit. Page–Geilker (1981) excludes a mean source for a body never resolved; CO escapes only if the engagements a large body undergoes resolve it (the engagement criterion, open). So CO expects no entanglement through gravity; Bose et al. 2017 and Marletto–Vedral 2017 would test it, and entanglement through gravity alone would make CO wrong. The sink's shot noise is φ ≈ ωU√(τ/Γ): about 8 × 10⁻³⁶ rad in one second per nucleon on an electron clock at 10⁻¹⁰ m. Costs, open: a mean source that jumps when a body is resolved is not conserved at the jump; the mean-field term must not signal; a linear classical–quantum coupling would carry noise (Oppenheim et al. 2023), which CO avoids only by being nonlinear.

**Three dimensions.** In one or two dimensions relations carry no transverse modes; in even dimensions a signal leaves a tail; in four or more there are no stable orbits and Lovelock admits a free constant. Three is the only dimension in which every rule holds: a consistency condition (Ehrenfest, Huygens, Lovelock, as assembled by Tegmark 1997).

**The beginning.** All the first possibility, free in few places, puts every place over capacity, so every place commits, with no order among the first commitments. Their renewals spread possibility over new places, each born with a share below capacity, until all are under capacity and commitment stops: an inflation-like epoch with its own end. No expansion count or ripple spectrum yet.

**Expansion.** Newton's law on a shell gives Friedmann's equation,

$$H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{k}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3},$$

for the matter era, and for the radiation era with radiation drawing twice its energy. Space grows by births: in the voids a new place appears with its share, leaving no record and no tick. Λ reads as a constant birth chance per place, about 2 × 10⁻⁵⁷ per tick; its value is an input.

## Constants

δ (retained share), P_max (capacity), Λ: the theory's own, values not given. G, c, ħ: measured. The hop length ℓ is fixed by δ through G.

## Open

The values of δ, P_max, Λ and the birth share; the nonlinear rule for the relations (strong fields); the walk on the curved network; the mechanism of the first relating; the hole's interior.
