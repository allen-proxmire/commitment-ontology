## Terms

| term | statement |
|---|---|
| **Possibility** | a quantity held at places, moving between related places, counted in quanta of ħ. Never made or lost in passing; what a draw takes becomes record; new places are born with their share |
| **Commitment** | one irreversible update to possibility, at a place |
| **Renewal** | a commitment that engages adjacent free possibility into its next commitment and releases what it held, less a share kept as record. It halts when none is adjacent |
| **Matter** | a commitment that keeps being fed. Mass is renewal frequency, ω = mc²/ħ |
| **Time** | matter's count, read only by ratio |
| **Record** | what every draw keeps: δħ per renewal, and a share of travel, light and field energy. Not possibility. It only grows: the arrow of time |
| **Pattern** | possibility in transit between commitments. It keeps its mean direction (inertia). A closed whole draws its turning once and its travel twice; light draws 2ħω |
| **Relation** | who passes possibility to whom. It carries direction-reversing quantities: a one-way part and a which-way phase. Places carry size (the held share, the stretch); the squares between relations carry shear |

The substrate is scaleless; moving or relabelling places, and resetting a place's clock, cost nothing (there is no backdrop, in space or in time); field equations are second order.

## Rules

| rule | statement |
|---|---|
| ratio hopping | a hop moves one quantum, ħ, and compares neighbours by ratio: the field is linear in ln P |
| capacity | a place holds at most P_max free possibility; over capacity it commits |
| hold-and-release | P_free = P(1 − h), h the fraction engaged |
| clock | renewal rate (mc²/ħ)(1 − h), following from the commitment cycle under the free-share rule |
| gravity | net transit runs toward the end that engages more; a place's h is the share of its possibility it holds. Patterns fall by refraction on the clock-rate field |
| draws set the network | a draw sets the held share and stretch of the places around it, against a stiffness of one quantum per relation |
| now | a commitment's now is its past light cone; spacelike events are not ordered with respect to it |

## Results

**Clocks.** Two clocks compare as (1 − h_A)/(1 − h_B), independent of their mass: gravitational redshift, universal.

**The static field.** Matter is a sink: each renewal keeps δħ at the rate mc²/ħ. The relations impose the profile, and ratio hopping across it carries the body's draw. In the static case,

$$\nabla^2\ln P_{\text{free}}=\sigma\,\delta(\mathbf r),\quad \sigma=\frac{\delta Mc\ell^2}{\hbar}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{P_{\text{free}}}{P_\infty}=e^{-U},\qquad U=\frac{GM}{c^2 r},$$

$$G=\frac{\delta\,c^{3}\,\ell^{2}}{4\pi\hbar}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \ell=\ell_P\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{\delta}} .$$

This is a static form: read in time, the inflow is diffusion and far too slow to build or carry a field, so the field is carried by the network's places, relations and squares ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md)). On the network the lapse obeys ∂_i(ψ²∂_i N) = 0, GR's static vacuum equation, and with the cost's ψ (from the cost being Einstein–Hilbert) the lapse is Schwarzschild's at every order: consistency with the cost, not a derivation of it. The quick form e^{−U} agrees with it through U², where β is read; at U³ the cost's lapse (−¼U³ in g₀₀) supersedes it.

**Free fall.** A matter wave refracting on the clock-rate field has g = c²∇h: Newton's acceleration, independent of mass.

**The weak field.** The same h slows ticks (1 − U + U²/2 …) and lengthens routes (e^{U}): the isotropic weak-field metric with γ = 1 and β = 1. Light bending 4GM/(c²b), the Shapiro delay, and Mercury's precession (a fit: Mercury selected the hop pairing).

**On CO's own walk.** The field reaches the walk at each place through two flips (the author's ruling, candidate): the mass flip scales as θ → θN (the clock rule), and the Wilson flip sets light's local speed, 2 cos w = c₀N/ψ², with ψ² the hop stretch. On the uniform walk light's long-wavelength speed is exactly 2 cos w and the rest energy exactly θ for every w. In a uniform weak field, in one and two dimensions (runs G5, G6): a slow packet falls at g c²(1 + s)², with s the Wilson term's speed excess ([`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md)), so every mass falls alike up to 2s; the walk's light follows the local speed the flip sets, so with γ = 1 it bends by twice the clock-only amount; the stretch puts no push on matter at rest. A scalar flip cannot tilt the light cone; the shift enters instead as a spin-blind directed hop on each relation, odd under reversing the relation and coupling to momentum, as GR's shift does (the author's ruling, candidate). It adds v_s = 2 sin b · sin k to the quasi-energy, b the relation's nudge: every pattern near the zone centre drifts at 2 sin b, light's cone tilts by the same amount at long wavelength with its mean speed unchanged, and a moving pattern feels the gravitomagnetic push dk_x/dt = −(∂_x v_s) k_y, a pattern carried with the drift none (run G7). On both axes, a rotating shift gives the Coriolis push of a rotating frame, and a curl-free shift (the Painlevé–Gullstrand form of a static field) carries zero-momentum patterns as free-fallers, every mass alike (run G12, two dimensions). The form holds up to about 40% of light's speed; the walk's heavy extra species are dragged the opposite way and do not scale with the clock (run G9); they are not particles (needed; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md)).

**Waves and the exterior.** A scalar field alone would radiate a sixth of GR's quadrupole power as a breathing mode; both are excluded (Hulse–Taylor, LIGO–Virgo). So places carry a stretch that can be out of step with their neighbours'. At linear order the cost is forced on the cubic graph: with moving places and resetting clocks free and two derivatives, the only costs are linearised Einstein–Hilbert, up to a strength and a speed, and they are isotropic though the cube is not (run G1). With moving places alone, one direction-dependent cost would survive. Beyond linear order: with no backdrop at every order (P7e), locality and two derivatives at every order, and Lorentz-invariant interactions (assumptions), the only consistent continuation of the linear cost is Einstein–Hilbert plus Λ (Wald 1986; Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri, Henneaux 2001), matter's coupling excluding the free branch (Feynman–Deser; general matter a further import) (continuum, long wavelength; the nonlinear cost on the lattice carriers is open; reading the metric as clock rate and stretch is an identification):

$$S=\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int (R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x .$$

Tensor waves with two polarizations and Einstein's quadrupole coefficient; Schwarzschild–de Sitter outside a body (Schwarzschild for Λ → 0). The mass that sets the exterior is GR's for dust and Tolman's, ∫(ρ + S)N√γ d³x, for pressure-supported bodies short of nuclear stress, given the walk on the curved network and P = P∞ at a place (an identification).

**What carries each part.** Lapse and stretch: each place's held share and its stretch. Frame dragging: each relation's one-way part, which behaves like a U(1) link (only its loops are physical). Shear: the squares between relations. Of the three placements tested, this staggered one is the only one on which no backdrop holds exactly, at nearest-neighbour range (run G3). Each is set by the matching part of the source: ρ + S, the diagonal stress, the momentum density, the off-diagonal stress.

**Gyroscopes.** The carriers are symmetric, so they couple to the symmetric part of the walk's stress tensor, which carries half the curl of the spin density. Spin and orbital angular momentum then precess alike: frame dragging Ω = −(c/2)∇ × h_0i, and geodetic Ω = (γ + ½)(GM/c²r³)(r × v), GR's 3/2 at γ = 1. Gravity Probe B: −6601.8 ± 18.3 and −37.2 ± 7.2 mas/yr, against GR's −6606.1 and −39.2; its rotors test the orbital part, not the spin's own ratio, which rests on P8b's frame identification. Matter's response is fixed, given no backdrop applied to the walk (P7e): it feels the field through the coupling that sources it. Candidate, given that ruling, the single coupling and γ = 1, at linear order; exact on the lattice for a free, continuous-time stand-in for the walk (run G4).

**Classical source.** Gravity's source is set by engagements (the author's ruling, candidate): a body's classical field is sourced by its state given the record of its engagements, evolved forward between them; gravity adds no engagements of its own. A mean source of an unresolved state is excluded, since with the Lüders update it signals faster than light (Bahrami et al. 2014); gravity that itself engages (Tilloy–Diósi) would need a smearing length near 10⁻¹⁰ m (Gran Sasso, Donadi et al. 2021), which CO does not have. So: no entanglement through gravity (Bose et al. 2017 and Marletto–Vedral 2017 would test it; entanglement would make CO wrong); no self-gravity of an unresolved state (a continuously monitored body's self-pull aims at its own conditional centre, so it changes only how sharply the record pins the body down: no new line at √(ω_c² + ω_SN²), as in the causal-conditional prescription of Liu et al. 2022, and a change degenerate with temperature calibration; a line there would count against this source); Page–Geilker passed, since macroscopic bodies are engaged constantly; no added collapse, with field noise and a small heating set by how often a body is engaged. The sink's shot noise is φ ≈ ωU√(τ/Γ): about 8 × 10⁻³⁶ rad in one second per nucleon on an electron clock at 10⁻¹⁰ m. Costs, open: the source jumps at engagements, so energy and momentum are conserved on average; the decoherence–diffusion trade-off (Oppenheim et al. 2023) bounds how faithfully a weakly engaged body sources gravity; which contacts are engagements is set by C8's criterion (a broad final state and a chain reaching N quanta, the author's ruling, candidate), so N, not derived, now also sets when gravity learns where a body is.

**Three dimensions.** In one or two dimensions relations carry no transverse modes; in even dimensions a signal leaves a tail; in four or more there are no stable orbits and Lovelock admits a free constant. Three is the only dimension in which every rule holds: a consistency condition (Ehrenfest, Huygens, Lovelock, as assembled by Tegmark 1997), graded shape, since its conditions (stable orbits, clean signals) are observed ones, and the cubic graph is three-dimensional by construction.

**The beginning.** All the first possibility, free in few places, puts every place over capacity, so every place commits, with no order among the first commitments. Their renewals spread possibility over new places, each born with a share below capacity, until all are under capacity and commitment stops: an inflation-like epoch with its own end. No expansion count or ripple spectrum yet.

**Expansion.** Newton's law on a shell gives Friedmann's equation,

$$H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{k}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3},$$

for the matter era, and for the radiation era with radiation drawing twice its energy. Space grows by births: in the voids a new place appears with its share, leaving no record and no tick. A newborn place carries a passing pattern smoothly, its amplitude the mean of its neighbours' (needed: born empty, it would scramble light into short wavelengths, against the clean redshift of distant light; run G11). Λ reads as a constant birth chance per place, about 2 × 10⁻⁵⁷ per tick; its value is an input.
