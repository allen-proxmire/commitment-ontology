# Space and Gravity

Results only. Grades are in [`TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md`](TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md); every route tried is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/).

## Terms

| term | statement |
|---|---|
| **Possibility** | a quantity held at places, moving between related places, counted in quanta of ħ. Never made or lost in passing; what a draw takes becomes record; new places are born with their share |
| **Commitment** | one irreversible update to possibility, at a place |
| **Renewal** | a commitment that engages adjacent free possibility into its next commitment and releases what it held, less a share kept as record. It halts when none is adjacent |
| **Matter** | a commitment that keeps being fed. Mass is renewal frequency, ω = mc²/ħ |
| **Time** | matter's count, read only by ratio |
| **Record** | what every draw keeps: δħ per renewal, and a share of travel, light and field energy. Not possibility. It only grows: the arrow of time |
| **Pattern** | possibility in transit between commitments. It keeps its mean direction (inertia). A closed whole draws its turning once and its travel twice; light draws 2ħω |
| **Relation** | who passes possibility to whom. It carries direction-reversing quantities: a one-way part and a which-way phase. Places carry size (the held share, the stretch); the squares between relations carry shear |

The substrate is scaleless; moving or relabelling places, and resetting a place's clock, cost nothing (there is no backdrop, in space or in time); field equations are second order.

## Rules

| rule | statement |
|---|---|
| ratio hopping | a hop moves one quantum, ħ, and compares neighbours by ratio: the field is linear in ln P |
| capacity | a place holds at most P_max free possibility; over capacity it commits |
| hold-and-release | P_free = P(1 − h), h the fraction engaged |
| clock | renewal rate (mc²/ħ)(1 − h), following from the commitment cycle under the free-share rule |
| gravity | net transit runs toward the end that engages more; a place's h is the share of its possibility it holds. Patterns fall by refraction on the clock-rate field |
| draws set the network | a draw sets the held share and stretch of the places around it, against a stiffness of one quantum per relation |
| now | a commitment's now is its past light cone; spacelike events are not ordered with respect to it |

## Results

**Clocks.** Two clocks compare as (1 − h_A)/(1 − h_B), independent of their mass: gravitational redshift, universal.

**The static field.** Matter is a sink: each renewal keeps δħ at the rate mc²/ħ. The relations impose the profile, and ratio hopping across it carries the body's draw. In the static case,

$$\nabla^2\ln P_{\text{free}}=\sigma\,\delta(\mathbf r),\quad \sigma=\frac{\delta Mc\ell^2}{\hbar}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{P_{\text{free}}}{P_\infty}=e^{-U},\qquad U=\frac{GM}{c^2 r},$$

$$G=\frac{\delta\,c^{3}\,\ell^{2}}{4\pi\hbar}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \ell=\ell_P\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{\delta}} .$$

This is a static form: read in time, the inflow is diffusion and far too slow to build or carry a field, so the field is carried by the network's places, relations and squares ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md)). On the network the lapse obeys ∂_i(ψ²∂_i N) = 0, GR's static vacuum equation, and with the cost's ψ (from the cost being Einstein–Hilbert) the lapse is Schwarzschild's at every order: consistency with the cost, not a derivation of it. The quick form e^{−U} agrees with it through U², where β is read; at U³ the cost's lapse (−¼U³ in g₀₀) supersedes it.

**Free fall.** A matter wave refracting on the clock-rate field has g = c²∇h: Newton's acceleration, independent of mass.

**The weak field.** The same h slows ticks (1 − U + U²/2 …) and lengthens routes (e^{U}): the isotropic weak-field metric with γ = 1 and β = 1. Light bending 4GM/(c²b), the Shapiro delay, and Mercury's precession (a fit: Mercury selected the hop pairing).

**On CO's own step.** Matter's step is one tick of the lattice Dirac flow with a Wilson term (candidate; [`MATTER.md`](MATTER.md)). The field enters it as one Hamiltonian (P7f restated; chosen, candidate):

$$H=\tfrac12\{N/\psi^2,\,K\}+\beta mN+\tfrac12\sum_j\{b_j,\sin k_j\}+\tfrac14(\nabla\times\mathbf b)\cdot\boldsymbol\Sigma,\qquad K=\sum_j\alpha_j\sin k_j+\beta r\sum_j(1-\cos k_j),$$

with N the lapse (the clock rule), ψ² the hop stretch and b the shift: local light speed N/ψ², rest energy mN, and the shift a drift that tilts the light cone as GR's shift does, its spin term turning a spinor with the dragged frame (Σ the Dirac spin matrices; Obukhov, Silenko and Teryaev's form). There is no window for the field to leave; the tick's only limit is τ_f(3·N/ψ² + mN + |b_x| + |b_y| + |b_z| + ¼|∇×b|) < π (a sufficient bound); exactly, for a uniform drift at τ_f = 0.4 and m = 0, a drift above about 5 times light's speed along an axis and 3.2 along a body diagonal, deep inside a black hole. On the step, in one and two dimensions: a slow packet falls at g c²(1 + rm), the Wilson term's speed-excess factor (1 + s)² (run F1a); light bends by twice the clock-only amount (F1b); two patterns that each source a field evolving at light's speed attract as Newton says, only after the field has crossed between them (F1c, 1D, the lapse alone); the extra species fall at about 0.6 of matter's rate, the same way (F1d); and with the shift alone in the Painlevé–Gullstrand form, a pattern at rest in the falling frame crosses the horizon on the path of a body dropped from infinity (F1e, 1D radial; within 0.4%) and keeps to it inside, on the falling path to 0.07% down to 0.3r_s (recount; drift within the tick limit, run F6). On the step the shift's drift and gravitomagnetic push, a rotating frame's Coriolis push and the curl-free free-fallers also match (to within 0.25%), light past a point mass bends twice the clock-only value (2.01), and in the closed loop in two dimensions with the stretch each pattern falls toward the other at 0.97–0.99 of Newton's 2D rate, after the field arrives (runs F2a–d). The extra species are not particles (needed; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md)).

**The web behind the lattice.** The cubic lattice stands in for a web of relations (candidate). With no backdrop a place has no position apart from the web, so the web's geometry is its hop strengths: the relations carry lengths, a place's size along a line is the mean of the two relations meeting at it, a diagonal per square gives the angle between lines, and whether the web is flat or curved is read from these alone (Gauss's curvature; Lamé's equations in three dimensions). On the flat webs tried the step carries a pattern straight however the web is labelled (three webs in two dimensions, one in three; one launch each); a flat web whose lines are relabelled unevenly from hop to hop is little felt at the two longer wavelengths run (two dimensions, one seed); and a mass's weak field bends a pattern as it bends light, the lengths and the clock each giving about half, with a grid excess of about 0.9(kℓ)² (two dimensions, one wavelength). Unevenness independent from place to place is curvature at the hop's scale and is felt: a far-travelled neutrino (association at about 3σ) would hold it below 4 × 10⁻⁴ to 2 × 10⁻⁹ per hop, by ℓ, and a PeV photon likewise if light's carriers feel it (reading). Open: longer wavelengths in three dimensions, a curved web with shear, strong fields, and the rule for which relations a place has (runs C15; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_web.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_web.md)).

**Waves and the exterior.** A scalar field alone would radiate a sixth of GR's quadrupole power as a breathing mode; both are excluded (Hulse–Taylor, LIGO–Virgo). So places carry a stretch that can be out of step with their neighbours'. At linear order the cost is forced on the cubic graph: with moving places and resetting clocks free and two derivatives, the only costs are linearised Einstein–Hilbert, up to a strength and a speed, and they are isotropic though the cube is not (run G1). With moving places alone, one direction-dependent cost would survive. Beyond linear order: with no backdrop at every order (P7e), locality and two derivatives at every order, and Lorentz-invariant interactions (assumptions), the only consistent continuation of the linear cost is Einstein–Hilbert plus Λ (Wald 1986; Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri, Henneaux 2001), matter's coupling excluding the free branch (Feynman–Deser; general matter a further import) (continuum, long wavelength; the nonlinear cost on the lattice carriers is open; reading the metric as clock rate and stretch is an identification):

$$S=\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int (R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x .$$

Tensor waves with two polarizations and Einstein's quadrupole coefficient; Schwarzschild–de Sitter outside a body (Schwarzschild for Λ → 0). The mass that sets the exterior is GR's for dust and Tolman's, ∫(ρ + S)N√γ d³x, for pressure-supported bodies short of nuclear stress, given the walk on the curved network and P = P∞ at a place (an identification).

**What carries each part.** Lapse and stretch: each place's held share and its stretch. Frame dragging: each relation's one-way part, which behaves like a U(1) link (only its loops are physical). Shear: the squares between relations. Of the three placements tested, this staggered one is the only one on which no backdrop holds exactly, at nearest-neighbour range (run G3). Each is set by the matching part of the source: ρ + S, the diagonal stress, the momentum density, the off-diagonal stress.

**Gyroscopes.** The carriers are symmetric, so they couple to the symmetric part of the walk's stress tensor, which carries half the curl of the spin density. Spin and orbital angular momentum then precess alike: frame dragging Ω = −(c/2)∇ × h_0i, and geodetic Ω = (γ + ½)(GM/c²r³)(r × v), GR's 3/2 at γ = 1. Gravity Probe B: −6601.8 ± 18.3 and −37.2 ± 7.2 mas/yr, against GR's −6606.1 and −39.2; its rotors test the orbital part, not the spin's own ratio, which rests on P8b's frame identification. Matter's response is fixed, given no backdrop applied to the walk (P7e): it feels the field through the coupling that sources it. Candidate, given that ruling, the single coupling and γ = 1, at linear order; exact on the lattice for a free, continuous-time stand-in for the walk (run G4).

**Classical source.** Gravity's source is set by engagements (chosen, candidate): a body's classical field is sourced by its state given the record of its engagements, evolved forward between them; gravity adds no engagements of its own. A mean source of an unresolved state is excluded, since with the Lüders update it signals faster than light (Bahrami et al. 2014); gravity that itself engages (Tilloy–Diósi) would need a smearing length near 10⁻¹⁰ m (Gran Sasso, Donadi et al. 2021), which CO does not have. So: no entanglement through gravity (Bose et al. 2017 and Marletto–Vedral 2017 would test it; entanglement would make CO wrong); no self-gravity of an unresolved state (a continuously monitored body's self-pull aims at its own conditional centre, so it changes only how sharply the record pins the body down: no new line at √(ω_c² + ω_SN²), as in the causal-conditional prescription of Liu et al. 2022, and a change degenerate with temperature calibration; a line there would count against this source); Page–Geilker passed, since macroscopic bodies are engaged constantly; no added collapse, with field noise and a small heating set by how often a body is engaged. The sink's shot noise is φ ≈ ωU√(τ/Γ): about 8 × 10⁻³⁶ rad in one second per nucleon on an electron clock at 10⁻¹⁰ m. Costs, open: the source jumps at engagements, so energy and momentum are conserved on average; the decoherence–diffusion trade-off (Oppenheim et al. 2023) bounds how faithfully a weakly engaged body sources gravity; which contacts are engagements is set by C8's criterion (a broad final state and a chain reaching N quanta, chosen, candidate), so N, not derived, now also sets when gravity learns where a body is.

**Three dimensions.** In one or two dimensions relations carry no transverse modes; in even dimensions a signal leaves a tail; in four or more there are no stable orbits and Lovelock admits a free constant. Three is the only dimension in which every rule holds: a consistency condition (Ehrenfest, Huygens, Lovelock, as assembled by Tegmark 1997), graded shape, since its conditions (stable orbits, clean signals) are observed ones, and the cubic graph is three-dimensional by construction.

**The beginning.** All the first possibility, free in few places, puts every place over capacity, so every place commits, with no order among the first commitments. Their renewals spread possibility over new places (at the river's rate, Expansion below), each born with a share below capacity, until all are under capacity and commitment stops: an inflation-like epoch with its own end. No expansion count or ripple spectrum yet. A size, for the part of the universe visible today (shape): counting one first place per matter pattern that commits (3 × 10⁸⁰, a whole of three quarks counting once: its commitment is at one place (chosen, candidate); assuming as many dark patterns as ordinary ones, and a net lepton number no larger than the baryon number (the neutrinos' share is unmeasured; the data allow up to 10⁸ times more, which would remove the growth)) and about one place per photon at the epoch's end (10⁸⁸–10⁸⁹), the first ball's radius is 25 nm to 4 μm and the epoch's end 9 μm to 3 mm, for ℓ between δ = 1 and the candidate neutrino bound; a growth of 350–760 in length. The standard history run back to light at the hop's energy gives 27 μm to 5 mm, an agreement by construction ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)).

**Expansion.** Newton's law on a shell gives Friedmann's equation,

$$H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{k}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3},$$

for the matter era, and for the radiation era with radiation drawing twice its energy. Expansion can be written as a shift: an outward river b = H(x − x₀), the same about any centre at long wavelength, where a uniform shift is free (P7e). With the stretch uniform the constraint reads 6H² = 16πGρ + 2Λc², Friedmann's equation at k = 0. Over a finite web the static field equation has no solution for the mean density; the mean goes to this constraint (reading; [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)). For the universe's expansion the births are what happens and the river is the bookkeeping (chosen, candidate): places are born at τ∇·b = 3Hτ per place per tick, so the birth rate is the constraint, not a second rule, and Λ's constant birth chance is its constant part. The two descriptions agree only at long wavelength: a river through fixed places would shift a distant pattern's lattice terms by its speed (c₂ → c₂ − b/2 along the river), and that shift grows with distance from x₀, giving far-off matter other small-scale physics and the observer a special centre the universe does not have. Near a mass at rest on the network the river flows through fixed places, with no births or removals; there the shift of the lattice terms is real and the mass is a real centre. In general the places ride with the stuff (chosen, candidate): with u the velocity of the stuff at a place, births are τ∇·u per place per tick and the river flows through the places at w = b − u; where there is no stuff, births run at the constraint's rate, τ√(3Λ)c, Λ's constant chance. That gives 3Hτ for the expansion; near a mass at rest, no births and w = b without Λ, and with Λ its rate in the empty space around the mass, whatever the mass. Births at the river's divergence alone are excluded as the general rule: the river converges in empty space near a mass, where nothing is removed. Inside a body at rest no places are born; Λ's constant chance is then a chance per empty place, and inside the body Λ only adds to the river in squares (b² = 2Gm(r)/r + Λc²r²/3), far too little to matter. In empty space the places move, beyond the uniform expansion, with the least motion that keeps their count (chosen, candidate): u = ∇φ with ∇²φ the birth rate, joined to the stuff's motion normal to its edge and at rest far away; a moving body then drags the places near it, the drag falling as (R/r)³, and slips past them at its sides (run F9). Open: how places change neighbours where they slip or shear, and which relations a newborn place has (the photon ceiling's pattern across the sky rests on the axes holding their direction); which velocity counts where streams cross (the frame of zero momentum is the natural reading, and light alone has none); stuff pushed by pressure, whose spreading the constraint does not set; the step from thin stuff at rest (no births) to none (Λ's rate); the river's direction around a mass with Λ, which cannot turn round in empty space; a test, since the rules differ only in hop-scale terms (run F8). On the step a ball sourcing its own outward river expands as flat dust: the shell holding a fraction q of the mass keeps to r^{3/2} = r₀^{3/2} + (3/2)√(r_s q) t within 2.3%, and the shells grow by one factor (v ∝ r) with a spread of 1.1% (run F7, radial reduction; the river and the uniform ball are entered, so the run shows the step carrying the flat expansion, not choosing it). Flatness is then the stretch being uniform across the ball, which rests on the beginning ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md)). In the voids a new place appears with its share, leaving no record and no tick. A newborn place carries a passing pattern smoothly, its amplitude the mean of its neighbours' (needed: born empty, it would scramble light into short wavelengths, against the clean redshift of distant light; run G11). Λ reads as a constant birth chance per place, about 2 × 10⁻⁵⁷ per tick; its value is an input.
