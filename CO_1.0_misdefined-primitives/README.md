# Commitment Ontology

The world is made of **commitments**: irreversible updates at places. Time at a place is the count of commitments there, so time runs one way at the bottom. Between commitments, places hold **possibility**, a conserved quantity that hops between related places. The vacuum never commits. **Matter** is commitment that keeps re-making itself. A **record** is what has been committed, and it is kept.

## The aim, and where it lands

CO starts from one supposition: **time is a count of irreversible events**, blinks that happen at places and cannot be undone. Everything else was added only when that starting point was silent. The hope was that a fresh starting point might give a leaner account of physics, or advance some small part of it.

It does neither. As it stands, no test excludes CO. Followed as far as it goes, it converges on interpretations that others reached by other routes:

| CO is forced to | which is |
|---|---|
| a preferred slicing in its dynamics that no observable can see | the khronometric / Hořava class of gravity, here at its GR limit |
| gravity laid down only by collapse events, which cannot entangle masses | collapse-sourced gravity (Tilloy, Diósi) |
| a causal order grown from irreversible events, whose geometry must be supplied | causal-set theory, and its open problem of getting a spacetime out |
| Born weights from phase lost at the moment of record | decoherence-based accounts of the Born rule (Zurek) |
| GR's form for how space curves, as the price of matching experiment | general relativity itself |
| particles as braids whose crossings only go forward | Bilson-Thompson's preon braids, with the mirror images removed |
| a renewal of three strands made of fair binary choices | Koide's lepton relation in Brannen's circulant form |
| antiparticles as negated twists, since mirrors are forbidden | a Majorana neutrino |

**What CO contributes** is an independent route to these, from a starting point not used before. It also contributes a few sharp negative results along the way:
- growth laws built on conserved units cannot produce a spacetime (a Dilworth bound);
- a hold rule that depends on the possibility present picks a slicing;
- nothing inside or outside the record fixes its free scale;
- among forward-only braids, three strands carry the largest share of self-sustaining patterns at every budget computed, and such patterns need an odd budget.

It also keeps an audited record of what was assumed, borrowed, tried and dropped.

## What goes in, what comes out

**In:**
- the primitives (commitment, place and relation, possibility, matter, pattern, record) and three added principles: the vacuum never commits, clocks are matter, no infinities;
- three dimensions of space, and a lattice for the engine (geometry is an input);
- rules added where the primitives were silent: the medium's outflow, the presence clock, the quantum rules, and for particles the twist rule and the selection principle S;
- borrowed from established physics:
  - GR's form for how space curves;
  - Lorentz invariance;
  - inertia = total energy;
  - GR's constraint algebra;
  - complex amplitudes;
  - Bilson-Thompson's braid model of particles (charge as twist, colour as the odd strand);
  - particle masses and couplings;
- measured: G, c, ħ, Λ, and bounds on CO's own scales.

**Out:**
- **From the rules alone:**
  - Newton's 1/r² law, linear and unscreened;
  - gravitational redshift;
  - universal free fall of patterns;
  - the Tolman pull;
  - no preferred frame from the flow;
  - one-way time.
- **From rules chosen to give them:** light bending (γ = 1), Mercury (β = 1), binary-pulsar radiation (0.98 of GR).
- **From borrowed inputs:**
  - gravity outside matter is exactly GR's, black holes included;
  - stars, spinning holes and mergers as GR's.
- **Quantum:**
  - gravity is laid down only by records, so it cannot entangle masses;
  - a window for the collapse length, 0.09 μm ≲ r_C ≲ 51 μm. This is gravity sourced by collapse events, of the Tilloy type;
  - the Born weights, from an added principle (Q6). A route that makes them follow from phase-destroying commitment holds only given a stated premise.
- **Particles (readings, in [`NOTE_braids_and_Koide.md`](NOTE_braids_and_Koide.md)):**
  - three strands, from S;
  - one family of 15 states, with no right-handed neutrino;
  - Koide's Q = 2/3 as p_stay = ½;
  - the neutrino as its own antiparticle (an inference; m_ββ ≈ 1.3–4 meV, below planned reach).
  
  Chirality and spin are open.
- **Structural results:**
  - CO's dynamics needs a preferred slicing at the discrete level, which no observable can see;
  - growth laws built on CO's units cannot produce a spacetime.

**No number comes out that was not put in.** CO reproduces known physics; the ways it could fail are in [`TESTS.md`](TESTS.md).

## What it is not

- **Not a theory of everything, and not a replacement for general relativity or quantum mechanics.** It reproduces both. Where it matches them beyond the weak field, it does so by borrowing their structure.
- **Not a derivation of any constant, particle, coupling or dimension.** G, c, ħ, Λ, the masses and three dimensions all go in. The braid picture of particles is borrowed, and its Koide reading fits known masses rather than predicting them. Its own scales (p_c, r_C, κ_b) are bounded by data, not fixed.
- **Not a claim that established physics is wrong.** Its gravity is GR's, and its quantum mechanics is standard quantum mechanics plus a collapse mechanism.
- **Not new in its collapse prediction.** Route (b) is gravity sourced by collapse events, a known class (Tilloy 2018). CO gives it a reading in terms of commitments.
- **Not evidence from simulation.** The runs are calculations on a toy engine, a cubic lattice with chosen rules. They test whether the rules give known results; agreement was built into the rules chosen.
- **Not confirmed by any experiment, and not peer-reviewed.** It is an independent, speculative ontology with an audited record of what was tried, what failed and what was borrowed.
