**1. Why.**
- **The gravity sector is near a natural stopping point.**
  - Runs 85–90 showed that Note H's carrier can hold GR's constraint structure through order a² in the kinetic sector at field order 2. Within the tested family this forces relativistic waves (Run 90; not certified globally).
  - Run 91 (27 October) then closed the whole field-order-2 algebra through a², kinetic and potential sectors together, once the third-order energy's lapse coupling may change. Field order 3, a global certificate and the khronometric route proper remain untested (ONTOLOGY §7).
  - These runs test a structure that was put in, GR's constraint algebra. By the record's own reading they do not derive it from the primitives.
- **The quantum sector is where CO may say something GR does not.** QM is what happens before ticking, and a pattern stays a pattern until it commits. That is the form of an objective commitment rule; it does not yet say when a pattern commits.
- **The field it belongs to has sharp, current experiments:**
  - matter-wave interference of 170 kDa clusters;
  - underground and dark-matter-detector searches for spontaneous radiation;
  - LISA Pathfinder's noise floor.

  So a criterion with a number in it is testable now.
- **The target, stated before any work:** derive the commitment threshold from the primitives as a number, and say which scale it shares with gravity (p_s, P∞, the hop, ε). One parameter fixed in two domains would be the strongest form of consilience the ontology can offer.

**2. The starting point: CO's primitives and rulings.**
- **Quantum mechanics is the study of what happens before ticking** (stated 23 October; ONTOLOGY §1).
  - Patterns of possibility have not committed.
  - A pattern keeps no clock of its own. The phase it accumulates counts time that belongs to the committed world around it: mc²τ/ħ, which in the engine is m∫N dt, turned by the local count (Run 16).
  - A measurement is a pattern meeting the committed world and being forced to commit to one channel.
- **The commitment trigger** (stated 25 October; ONTOLOGY §1). A pattern stays a pattern until it commits, either
  - **(a)** by interacting with the committed world, or
  - **(b)** by reaching an internal complexity at which it has to commit.

- **Gravity-collapse models.** Models that rest on spacetime fluctuations (Diósi–Penrose, Károlyházy) assume a medium whose nature they do not account for (an objection stated 25 October). So they are not taken as CO's route; CO has to derive what spacetime is first.
- **A commitment is stamped with the local count** (ruled 26 October; ONTOLOGY §1): its record is the count at the place where it happens, not a global tick.
- **Patterns** hop on their place's ticks and need free capacity there (R6, provisional). Massive patterns obey
$$
-N^4\nabla^2\psi+m^2N^2\psi=\omega^2\psi ,
$$
  and **a pattern's energy draws** (R5, ruled 19 October), with
$$
d=\kappa\big(4\omega^2N^{-4}-2m^2N^{-2}\big)\psi^2 .
$$
- **Dissolved relations.** A dissolved relation goes back to possibility as a two-part pattern, "as in entanglement" (stated 22 October; Note G). Two things are not stated in the record:
  - whether this is CO's account of entanglement;
  - how interactions produce two-part patterns.
- **The vacuum never commits, and clocks are matter** (rulings of 15 October). Matter is commitment that keeps re-making itself.
- **Commitment already appears in the record's rules and runs:**
  - **R2's hold rule is a commitment rule, and it already brings ħ in.** A commitment holds until the possibility present at its two places, summed over ticks, reaches
$$
\sum_{\rm ticks}(P_a+P_b)\ge H=2\hbar_tP_\infty ,
$$
    so ħ is a hold's length.
  - **R11.** Infalling matter commits where the possibility per place falls *to* 2p_s.
  - **Run 74.** δu, how long commitment takes as seen from far away, is not fixed by the rules.
  - **Run 16 (exploratory).** A pattern's phase, turned by a discrete count, picks up noise. That costs it coherence at a rate that rises steeply with its mass and falls as 1/(possibility per place). So matter-wave interferometry bounds P∞ *from below*, the same direction as gravity.
  - **The write-up's next-steps item 4.** It proposes a commitment rate ∝ |amplitude|² × local bandwidth, an objective-collapse model close to Diósi–Penrose.
  - **Run 14's withdrawn event layer** committed by Born choice.
- **The scales:**
  - p_s ≳ 4×10⁵ units per place (Run 24, from data; an extrapolation of three points over five decades);
  - P∞/p_s ≳ 1.6–1.9×10⁵ (Run 66, from data, resting on Run 66's choice that φ curves γ̃ and on the borrowed inertia = total energy). So P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰ units per place and ε = p_s/P∞ ≲ 6.3×10⁻⁶;
  - the hop, about 10^2.5–10^3 ℓ_P ≈ 5×10⁻³³–1.6×10⁻³² m (from the engine, not data; an open tension with ℓ_P).

**How a superposition draws (Reading I0; rulings of 27 October).**
- The record left the superposed case open. Reading I0 sets out four readings:
  - **(S)** by its average, which gives a Schrödinger–Newton self-interaction, and signals once commitment is added;
  - **(B)** each branch its own field (Penrose's E_G);
  - **(C)** only through its records;
  - **(H)**, which is the same as (S).
- **Ruled (C).** Records come from both routes:
  - route (a), at the surroundings' resolution;
  - route (b), a random "commit and continue" of an isolated pattern at a rate λ, localising with a coarseness length r_C.
- **The medium holds no branches.**
- **Consequences:**
  - an uncommitted pattern has no self-interaction;
  - route (b) is a rate, not a threshold;
  - gravity cannot entangle masses. If a test of gravitationally mediated entanglement finds it, these rulings fail (§8);

**5. Commitment, operationally.**
- **Route (a), meeting the committed world.**
  - When a pattern interacts with matter (which is commitment) in a way that depends on its path, the interaction can leave a record. Standard decoherence theory describes the same event: the environment acquires which-path information.
  - Interactions that leave no which-path record must leave the pattern coherent. Crystal Bragg reflection in neutron interferometers, material gratings and mirrors are all interactions with committed matter that keep coherence.
  - So route (a) must reproduce Q5's rates and Q5's coherences. If it does, it adds an objective endpoint (a record is kept) but **no new number**. That is a consistency requirement and a relabelling risk, not a prediction.
  - Run 16 has every pattern's phase already turned by the committed world's count. So "an isolated pattern, with nothing committed around it" (route (b)) needs defining.
- **Route (b), internal complexity.** This is the only place a new number can come from. An isolated pattern commits on its own.
- **Which channels: the local stamp (ruled 26 October).** A commitment's record is stamped with the local count at the place where it happens, not with a global tick.
  - **Why this choice.**
    - It is the record's own account of time: time at a place is the count of commitments there (ONTOLOGY §1), and R4 says the engine's tick is not physical time.
    - It is the rule that already turns a pattern's phase (Run 16): each branch's record is its own
$$
\phi_{\rm branch}=m\!\int\! N\,dt,\qquad N=P/P_\infty .
$$
  - **What it rules out.** Energy-projector channels and their automatic immunity from the heating bounds. A global stamp could only sort branches by energy; it would make the engine's tick physical; and it would add nothing a laboratory could test beyond route (a).
  - **What it implies.** Branches at different places carry different records, so a commitment can tell them apart by where they are: the channels are position-like. Two requirements follow, and they are now the sector's targets.
    - **No trigger read from the local count.** Branches at different heights in an external field also differ in ∫N dt, and interferometers keep them coherent (Appendix A, point 1; Q6), so commitment cannot be triggered by the local count itself.
      - Under the 27 October rulings an uncommitted pattern has no self-interaction either, so there is no self-sourced quantity to read.
      - Route (b) is therefore a rate: an isolated pattern commits at random, at a rate λ, with coarseness r_C (§3).
    - **A coarseness length.** Position-like channels heat matter (Appendix A, point 3, under the borrowed A8). Staying under Q2 (XENONnT 2026; LISA Pathfinder) needs a coarseness length well above the hop: §6(iii) excludes the hop by more than twenty-two orders. CO's rules do not yet supply one. Under the rulings r_C is route (b)'s coarseness, and Reading I0's bounds suggest it may have to be very large (photon coherence on long baselines points to 10⁶–10⁸ m or more). *(Run 92, 27 October: that holds only if radiation's gravity is read per pattern. Read per population, as ruled, the window opens at r_C = 2.3×10⁻¹⁰ m; §8, I0.)* If one scale sets both λ and r_C, and also appears in gravity, that is the consilience target (§1).
  - **What it leaves open.**
    - Route (a) still needs Q5's rates and coherences.
    - Q7 stays as a requirement: a position-like rule must not decohere energy superpositions where coherence is seen.
    - The energy-projector conjecture of the checks' revision is withdrawn by this ruling.

**6. Candidate readings of "internal complexity", each with its scale.**

*Under the 27 October rulings route (b) is a rate, and an uncommitted pattern has no self-interaction. So the threshold readings (i)–(iv) below have no CO-native quantity to read; they are kept for the record and for the constraints their exclusions give. Reading (viii), a stochastic rate, is the form the rulings select.*

*All estimates are order of magnitude.*
- **(i) Possibility load per place: commitment when a pattern's own draw lowers the possibility at its places by about p_s.**
  - With R2's presence clock, δP/P∞ = |Φ|/c² at weak field, so the condition is
$$
\frac{Gm}{c^2R}\gtrsim\varepsilon,\qquad \varepsilon\lesssim6\times10^{-6}\ \text{(an upper bound only)}.
$$
  - For the 170 kDa clusters (R = 4 nm), Gm/(c²R) ≈ 5×10⁻⁴¹. Nothing commits in a laboratory only if ε ≳ 5×10⁻⁴¹. The gravity bounds give no lower bound on ε, so under this reading Q1 is a lower bound: 5×10⁻⁴¹ ≲ ε ≲ 6.3×10⁻⁶, a range of 35 decades. (For comparison, the Sun's surface value is 2×10⁻⁶.)
  - This reading shares R11's scale, p_s, but not R11's rule. R11 commits where P has fallen *to* 2p_s (N = 2ε); this reading commits where P has fallen *by* p_s (1 − N ≈ ε). R11 applied literally to patterns would need near-black-hole compactness.
  - It is a condition on one branch. It does not say which superposition commits, or into which basis.
  - With R at the Compton length it gives m ≳ m_P√ε (≲ 3.3×10¹⁶ Da).
  - In the laboratory it predicts standard QM for any ε above about 5×10⁻⁴¹: consistent, not distinctive.
- **(ii) A fixed count of displaced possibility.**
  - Commitment would come once the two branches differ by n units of possibility in total. This treats the branches as separate sources (A7), so it is a borrowed form.
  - For the 170 kDa clusters, displaced by 100 nm, with the hop at 10³ ℓ_P and P∞ at its bound, one branch's draw summed over a ball of radius Δx around it is
$$
P_\infty\frac{Gm}{c^2}\,\frac{2\pi\,\Delta x^2}{\ell^3}\gtrsim2\times10^{44}\ \text{units}.
$$
  - The difference between the branches is a dipole field. Summed out to radius L it grows as 2πΔx·L, so the count depends on the cutoff and is larger still.
  - Any natural n (1, or a few) commits every superposition at once, and Q1 excludes that.
  - A large n would be put in by hand. This reading is excluded unless the count is fixed by something else.
- **(iii) A Diósi–Penrose rule, regularised at the hop (a borrowed form, A7).**
  - If each branch drew separately, CO's draw, which is Newtonian at weak field, would give Penrose's E_G as the branches' difference self-energy. It belongs to the branch-wise reading (B), which the rulings exclude, so it is not CO-native. It is kept because its exclusion constrains any position-coupled rule.
  - With the hop, about 10⁻³² m, as regulator, R₀ is more than twenty-two orders of magnitude below the current bound R₀ > 4.5×10⁻¹⁰ m (XENONnT 2026). **Excluded for position-coupled channels.** The earlier papers reached the same result at their own short scale.
  - A coarse regulator would need a length CO's rules do not yet supply. Combinations of the hop with P∞, built as (iv) builds masses (ℓP∞² ≳ 0.2–0.7 Å, for example), are not excluded by this argument. They are dimensional analysis too.
  - With the local stamp (§5) the channels are position-like, so this exclusion applies directly: the coarseness length has to come from elsewhere in the rules.
- **(iv) A mass threshold from CO's scales.** CO's dimensionless scales combine with the Planck mass m_P = 1.31×10¹⁹ Da into masses:

| combination | bound (from the gravity bounds; the hop from the engine) |
|---|---|
| m_P / P∞ | ≲ 2.0×10⁸ Da |
| m_P / p_s | ≲ 3.3×10¹³ Da |
| m_P ε | ≲ 8.2×10¹³ Da |
| m_P / (10^2.5–10³) (the hop) | ≈ 1.3–4.1×10¹⁶ Da |

  - The first three are upper bounds and may lie lower, even below today's interference record (1.7×10⁵ Da).
  - Only m_P/P∞ reaches the range of levitated nanoparticles and proposed space tests (about 10⁸–10¹¹ Da), where superposition experiments are being prepared. The other three lie above it, at 3×10¹³–4×10¹⁶ Da.
  - With free powers of independent scales, a combination can be found near any mass: m_P/P∞^p leaves a window today for every p < 1.28.
  - **This is dimensional analysis, not a derivation.** It becomes a prediction only if a rule derived from the primitives produces one of these combinations.
- **Readings not yet assessed, from the record and the checks:**
  - **(v) Commitment when a pattern ticks, i.e. becomes a clock or matter.** This is the most direct reading of "before ticking". Interference of atoms whose internal states are clocks (Margalit et al. 2015) rules it out in its plain form. So CO has to say what, in a ticking body, stays uncommitted: the 170 kDa clusters are matter, yet their centre of mass interferes.
  - **(vi) R2's hold rule applied to patterns.** I4 plans to use it.
  - **(vii) Run 16's discrete-count dephasing.** Its interferometry bound on P∞ points the same way as the gravity bound, so it gives a bound, not a window.
  - **(viii) A stochastic rate.** For example the write-up's rate ∝ |amplitude|² × local bandwidth. All of (i)–(iv) are thresholds.
  - **(ix) R11 applied literally,** which needs near-black-hole compactness.

**7. A consistency window, and what would test it.** *(Under the 27 October rulings route (b) is a rate, so this threshold window applies only if a rate rule turns out to carry a mass scale.)* Suppose a derived rule gave m_c = m_P/P∞, with patterns heavier than m_c committing by route (b).
- **Quantum side.** Q1 needs m_c > 1.7×10⁵ Da, so P∞ < 7.7×10¹³ units per place.
- **Gravity side.** The pulsar triple and clock agreement give P∞ ≳ 6.4×10¹⁰ (Runs 24, 66). That bound rests on p_s's extrapolation, on Run 66's φ/U choice and on the borrowed inertia. With Mercury alone, P∞ ≳ 6.6×10⁷.
- **The window.** 6.4×10¹⁰ ≲ P∞ ≲ 7.7×10¹³ is three decades. One parameter is bounded below by gravity and, under this hypothetical rule, above by interferometry. That is a consistency window, not consilience: two one-sided bounds are not two determinations.
  - *Note, 27 October (Reading I5, ruled): this window cannot share P∞ with route (b). Under κ_b = 1/(aP∞), Run 93's window needs a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴, 10–12 decades above it (7.9 or more across the lunar-laser-ranging variants). The same window bounds a·P∞ above at about 4×10⁹² (the strict radiation-gravity edge). If a threshold reading were revived, it would need its own scale.*
- **Proposed experiments could test it.**
  - Interference at 10⁸ Da would push the upper bound to 1.3×10¹¹, a factor of 2 above the lower bound.
  - Interference at about 2×10⁸ Da (m_P/6.4×10¹⁰) would close the window and exclude this reading. That needs separations and times like Q1's, standard decoherence accounted for, and the gravity-side bound standing.
- **ε is then bounded, not fixed.** With P∞ fixed, ε = p_s/P∞ is bounded below through p_s ≳ 4×10⁵ and above by the pulsar triple. For m_c = 10⁸ Da that gives 3.1×10⁻⁶ ≲ ε ≲ 6.3×10⁻⁶, and Δ = εC/(1 + ε) (ONTOLOGY §5, item 3) would be bounded on both sides. It becomes a number only if p_s is fixed too.

If a rule derived and registered beforehand gave this combination, the window would test it. No such rule exists yet, so this is a target, not an output.

**Appendix A. Cautions from a reading of the earlier papers against established physics** (reading of 25 October, corrected 26–27 October). Each point is a constraint any commitment rule must meet, whoever proposes it. None of the earlier papers' constructions is a starting point here.
- **Context.** The earlier work placed its ontology beside Diósi and Penrose's gravity-related collapse. It reproduced τ ∝ ħ/E_G as a scaling, with E_G identified rather than computed, and argued that a coarse, environment-set channel basis escapes the 2020 radiation bound.
- **Five points.** Points 3 and 4 are constraints on any rule. Points 1, 2 and 5 are cautions on what a rule must not be or do.
  1. **A mechanism keyed to externally imposed clock-rate differences conflicts with interferometry.**
     - If two branches running at different rates set by an external potential must commit once their relative action reaches ħ, interferometers in a gravitational field should decohere. They don't:
       - neutrons keep the gravitationally induced phase m g Δz T/ħ, about 60 rad at full tilt (Colella, Overhauser and Werner 1975);
       - atoms keep the phase from a source mass's potential difference (Overstreet et al. 2022), and light-pulse interferometers keep 10⁶–10⁹ rad of phase;
       - optical-clock superpositions of internal energies keep ΔE t/ħ ≈ 3×10¹⁵ rad per second of coherence (Sr, 429 THz), about 10¹⁷ rad over 37 s.
     - These test a rule keyed to the potential. In a light-pulse interferometer in uniform g the two arms' proper times are equal at leading order, so they do not test a rule keyed to proper time.
     - The clock superpositions test energy differences between branches.
     - None of these examples touches differences a pattern sources itself, as the earlier papers' symmetric lump-in-two-places case shows: its two branches have equal energy, so a clock-rate mechanism gives it no relative phase at all.

     **CO's rule must not let externally imposed clock-rate differences, or ordinary energy differences between branches, force commitment.** Whether self-sourced differences may is open.

     The mainstream effect of this kind needs no new physics. Through time dilation, a body's internal degrees of freedom decohere its centre of mass, with
     $$
     \tau_{\rm dec}=\sqrt{2/N}\,\hbar c^2/(k_BTg\,\Delta x)
     $$
     (Pikovski et al. 2015; disputed by Bonder, Okon and Sudarsky).
  2. **A well-defined preferred time removes the reason for collapse in Penrose's 1996 argument.** That argument needs the absence of a common time translation across the branches. His later equivalence-principle version (Penrose 2014) works with absolute time, as in Newton–Cartan theory, and has to be checked against CO separately. CO derives universal free fall (Run 16), so that version could apply. A global tick alone does not identify places across branches either, since places can be relabelled freely.
  3. **Exact immunity from the radiation bounds needs channels that commute with the Hamiltonian** (stated for a Lindblad channel, the borrowed A8).
     - For a Lindblad channel with Hermitian jump operators Ĵ_α = g_αΠ̂_α (a dephasing-type channel),
$$
\frac{d\langle \hat H\rangle}{dt}=-\tfrac12\sum_\alpha g_\alpha^2\,\big\langle[\hat\Pi_\alpha,[\hat\Pi_\alpha,\hat H]]\big\rangle .
$$
     - This vanishes for every state if [Π̂_α, Ĥ] = 0 for all α. In finite dimensions it vanishes only then, since Tr Ĥ[Π̂,[Π̂,Ĥ]] = ‖[Π̂,Ĥ]‖²_HS. For unbounded operators the converse can fail: Π̂ = p̂ and Ĥ = p̂²/2m + mg x̂ give [p̂,[p̂,Ĥ]] = 0 with [p̂,Ĥ] ≠ 0.
     - Staying under the bounds needs only a small enough rate, which coarse channels can also give. That is the equivalent of a large Diósi–Penrose R₀.
     - If the channels are coarse rather than energy projectors, the scale that sets their coarseness also lowers E_G, so one regulator would serve both. Whether CO has one is open (§10).
     - Projectors that commute with the full interacting Hamiltonian are nonlocal. The local subsystem projectors a detector needs do not commute with it.
  4. **Bell's theorem.**
     - A commitment rule that is local in Bell's sense gives CHSH ≤ 2, whatever the pre-commitment pattern is: Bell's λ can be any complete state, a nonlocal pattern included.
     - Quantum mechanics allows up to 2√2, and loophole-free experiments give S > 2 (2.42 ± 0.20, Hensen et al. 2015; 2.0747 ± 0.0033, Storz et al. 2023).
     - **So the commitment rule itself cannot be local in Bell's sense.** Committing the two parts of one pattern must give joint outcomes that do not factorize given the pattern, while each side's marginals stay independent of the other side's setting.
     - No-signalling alone is not an advantage, since GRW and CSL have it too.
     - CO has a preferred foliation (one global hop rate) in which such a commitment can be defined. That brings in the bounds on the speed of influence in a preferred frame (Salart et al. 2008; Bancal et al. 2012).
     - The earlier papers' "locality without superdeterminism" fails on this point. The reading's own conclusion, that "the nonlocality has to live in the pre-commitment pattern", does not follow, and is corrected here.
  5. **Renaming is not computing.** Writing E_Δ = E₁₁ + E₂₂ − 2E₁₂ as "Str − Coh" names the difference self-energy; it does not compute it.
