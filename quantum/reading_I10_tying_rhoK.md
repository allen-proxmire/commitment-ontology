## Reading I10: what could tie ρ_K to r_C; what a surface saturation record deposits

*29 October 2026. After Run 99 (C4-β with Γ_K = c/r_C, ρ_K free: a definite map at laboratory densities). Graded under Reading I7's criterion. Revised after an independent check (`checkI10/REPORT.md`), whose corrections are adopted. Ruled in §7. Numbers are arithmetic on Run 99's hashed code, not a test.*

### 1. Why
Run 99 left ρ_K free, so its map describes a family of candidates. A relation ρ_K(r_C) would turn the map into a curve.

### 2. What the record offers
The threshold is a stock K per place, read as a mass density (Reading I8, C4-β §2): ρ_K = Kaħ/(2cħ_tℓ⁴).

**(i) A count per cell, under C1a** (Reading I9 §2; the K = 1, a = 1 lines are Run 99's registered named lines). With ħ_t = V_c/2ℓ³:
$$
\rho_K=\frac{K\,a\,m_\ell}{V_c},\qquad m_\ell=\frac{\hbar}{c\ell},\qquad N_{\rm cell}=\frac{K\,a\,m_\ell}{m_N},\qquad \rho_K\propto r_C^{-3}.
$$
- **K.** The record has no per-place capacity and no bandwidth quantum (Note D). K = 1 is a choice of unit, not a minimum. K = p_s or P∞ lands above any laboratory density.
- **a and the hop.**
  - a ∈ {0.1, 1, 2π} (Readings I4, I5, I7).
  - The hop is either ℓ_P (P08) or the engine's
$$
\ell=\ell_P\sqrt{0.99\pi\,a\,p_s/3},
$$
    which grows with p_s, and p_s is bounded only below (≳ 4×10⁵, conditional since Reading I6).
  - So on the engine's hop
$$
N_{\rm cell}\propto K\sqrt{a/p_s}.
$$
- **C1a itself** is a renaming of r_C onto ħ_t (Run 97). It adds no independent number.

**(ii) Route (b) reaching the cell's ceiling.** A cell saturates where its route-(b) rate alone would reach one commitment per light-crossing:
$$
\lambda N_{\rm cell}=\frac{\kappa_bc^2\rho_KV_c}{\hbar}=\frac{c}{r_C}\iff\rho_K=\frac{\hbar}{\kappa_b\,c\,r_C\,V_c}=aP_\infty\frac{\hbar/(cr_C)}{V_c}.
$$
- This is Reading I9's Compton-mass row divided by κ_b. It ties ρ_K to r_C **and** κ_b, which stays free over 7–8 decades: a surface, not a curve.
- Γ_K/λN_cell = 1 by construction, so there is no 10× jump. The cell-as-unit channel (Reading I9, ruling 3) gives a step of (ρ_K/ρ_b)(V_sat/V) ≲ 1.05.
- Read literally as a ceiling, cells denser than ρ_K would commit at c/r_C, below route (b)'s own rate. That contradicts "a threshold, not a cap" (Reading I8, C4-β §1). The additive reading is a choice.

**(iii) C1b's count:**
$$
\rho_K=\frac{K}{P_\infty}\,\frac{m_N}{V_c(r_N)}\quad\text{for every species}.
$$
K = P∞ is excluded by Run 99, and a laboratory onset needs K ≳ 10¹⁰P∞, which is fitted.

**Not relations, or already excluded:**
- **Atomic density, m_u/a₀³** (borrowed): a constant.
- **Λ's density:** the per-particle regime, excluded at c/r_C.
- **A self-gravity condition, GρV_c/(r_Cc²) = ε:** Note I §6(i)'s reading, excluded because a pattern has no self-interaction and because it reads the potential (Q6). ε is in any case bounded only above.
- **An independent condition on ħ_t:** under C1a it would fix r_C, not ρ_K.

**What the lines say:**
- **On P08's hop the count per cell is fixed,** and the minimal line has no free number beyond r_C. It lands at laboratory densities only at 45–51 μm, with margins of about 10¹¹.
- **On the engine's hop, the count per cell moves the free number from ρ_K to p_s,** the gravity sector's crossover. For p_s at its lower bound the lines sit at r_C ≳ 4–7 μm with margins of 10⁵–10⁷ in rate. That is about 3 decades in acceleration noise, since Γ_max ∝ ASD². Raising p_s moves the lines toward Run 99's near zone.
- **Read the other way, a near-zone onset would read p_s.**
  - The near zone's N_cell is 8×10¹⁰–1.2×10¹³ (369 of its 375 laboratory points); 6 in-band germanium points reach 2.3×10¹⁴.
  - With K = a = 1 this corresponds to a hop
$$
\ell=\frac{\hbar}{c\,m_NN_{\rm cell}}
$$
    of 9×10⁻³¹–2.6×10⁻²⁷ m (6×10⁴–1.6×10⁸ ℓ_P). Under the engine's G that is p_s ≈ 3×10⁹–3×10¹⁶ (1×10¹²–3×10¹⁶ outside germanium).
  - The record allows that range: p_s ≳ 4×10⁵, and p_s ≲ P∞/1.6×10⁵ ≲ 10³⁰.
  - So under this line, an onset of saturation in the laboratory would measure the gravity sector's crossover. This is a link between the two sectors, not a prediction, since p_s is free.
- **The ceiling relation (ii)** needs κ_b within 0.3–4 decades of κ_top to land at laboratory densities, at r_C ≥ 12 μm, and it has no jump.

**The finding.**
- **The record can tie ρ_K to r_C, but only by moving the free number elsewhere:** to the hop (through p_s), to K, or to κ_b.
- **The one fixed tie, P08's hop with K = 1, lies far from present sensitivity** (45–51 μm; margins of about 10¹¹).
- **On the engine's hop the tie runs through p_s.** A laboratory onset in Run 99's near zone would correspond to p_s ≈ 10¹²–10¹⁶ (a = 1, K = 1). This is the first link between the quantum sector's saturation and a gravity-sector constant. It is a candidate, fitted through p_s.

### 5. What a surface saturation record deposits (open since Reading I8 ruling 2)
- **Forced by the record:**
  - A saturation record deposits (Reading I8 ruling 2). Q2 says deposits land *only* where a record fixes position, a necessary condition.
  - It deposits where its Π̂_x jumped (Reading I6 ruling 5).
  - It carries the struck units' draw, not the whole body's (Reading I6 ruling 6).
  - λ_tot is local, counting depositing records (Reading I6 ruling 9).
- **Not forced; open sub-choices:**
  1. **Which units are struck.** The band where 0 < S < 1, or every unit in the layer about 2–4 r_C deep that ∂Π̂_x reaches (∂Π̂_x/∂x_j ∝ m_j∇G_{r_C}; Reading I8, C4-β §4). The layer holds 10–100 times the band's mass.
  2. **The width:** √1.5 r_C (written for a single unit) or the Π̂ kernel's r_C. Immaterial for the floor.
  3. **The energy:** rest mass or E_TK.
  4. **The unravelling.** D[Π − c] = D[Π] for any real c, so the record count depends on c. Reading I9 ruling 4 implies c = 1 in the bulk and 0 outside. Since deposits make the unravelling physical, that choice is part of the rule.
- **Effect on Run 96's floor.**
  - By Campbell's theorem, deposit noise is Σ_r E_r²⟨g_x²⟩_r/λ_tot,r, region by region.
  - Surface records exceed the shell's own route-(b) records by 10⁴ or more, so the shell's term is essentially removed.
  - The floor therefore falls by the shell's g_x²-weighted share of J, housing included: 10⁻⁶–10⁻² for the band, up to about 4×10⁻² for the struck layer. Surface record rates are 6×10¹⁶ to about 10²⁶ s⁻¹, against route (b)'s 9×10¹⁵ s⁻¹ for LISA Pathfinder's test mass.
  - The floor can only fall (Reading I9 §5), so Runs 98 and 99's comparison at the registered floor stays conservative for survival. Gravity's graininess changes by a few per cent at most.

### 6. Choices for a ruling
1. **Record §4's finding:** the record ties ρ_K to r_C only by moving the free number (to p_s, K or κ_b). The one fixed tie lies at 45–51 μm, far from present sensitivity.
2. **Whether to name the engine-hop minimal line (C1a, K = 1, a = 1) as a candidate** linking saturation to p_s, for later runs to carry. Its free number is p_s.
3. **The surface-record deposit:**
   - (a) rule §5's forced part, with the struck units taken as the ∂Π̂ layer, the width r_C and the energy E_TK;
   - (b) or leave the sub-choices open, since none moves the floor by more than a few per cent.

### 7. Rulings (29 October; Allen: "do as you recommend")
1. **§4's finding is recorded:** the record ties ρ_K to r_C only by moving the free number (to p_s, K or κ_b); the one fixed tie lies at 45–51 μm, far from present sensitivity.
2. **The engine-hop minimal line** (C1a, K = 1, a = 1; ℓ = ℓ_P√(0.99πa p_s/3), N_cell = ħ/(cℓm_N)) **is a named candidate,** linking saturation to p_s, carried by later runs and mapped against p_s.
3. **The surface-record deposit (option a):** a threshold-surface saturation record deposits the draw E_TK of the units its ∂Π̂ layer reaches, at the recorded surface position, smeared at r_C; the unravelling has c = 1 in a saturated bulk and 0 outside. Run 96's floor can only fall, by a few per cent at most.
