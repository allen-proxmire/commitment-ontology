**Why.** Reading I7 asked what could fix route (b)'s coarseness r_C from the primitives. It ruled a grading:
- **derived:** follows from the record as it stands;
- **lead:** needs new statements, which are then tested as proposed rulings;
- **fitted:** chosen to land in the window.

It found that no single statement in the record fixes r_C without a further free quantity. Candidate C1 is the first lead put to a run.

**The candidate.** A route-(b) commitment has no partner to resolve it, so it is placed by the medium: as sharply as the volume of places whose vacuum possibility amounts to one hold, H = 2ħ_tP∞. The pattern's own possibility still fills the hold. The new statements needed are:
- C1 itself;
- a hold counted over a volume of places;
- one place per ℓ³;
- a value of ħ_t, the hold's length in ticks, which the record leaves free;
- for C1b, a coarseness that differs by species.

**C1a: one hold at one moment.**
$$
(2\pi)^{3/2}r_C^3=2\hbar_t\,\ell^3 .
$$
P∞ cancels, so C1a maps r_C one to one onto ħ_t: a renaming, with no test. It needs ħ_t ≈ 10⁸⁴–10⁹² with P08's hop (10⁷⁴–10⁸⁵ with the engine's). That puts one hold at 10²³–10³¹ times the age of the universe, which contradicts nothing, since ordinary matter's renewal rate E/(aħ) does not depend on ħ_t. Its one cost is that the engine's ħ = 5 ticks (Run 15) must be an engine setting only.

**C1b: one hold over its own duration.**
$$
(2\pi)^{3/2}r_C^3=\frac{2\hbar_t\,\ell^4\kappa_b}{\lambda_C},\qquad r_{C,e}=r_{C,N}\Big(\frac{m_e}{m_N}\Big)^{1/3}=0.082\,r_{C,N} .
$$
Each species gets its own coarseness, so Run 96's window is rebuilt species by species (W_s):
- electrons at r_e for XENONnT and for their share of LISA's kicks, Earth's heat and the interferometers;
- nucleons at r_N for SNO, the floor and the cap;
- intergalactic-gas heating with every free particle at its own r_s, and bound atoms heated through their centre of mass.

C1b passes if and only if W_s is non-empty.

**Relic neutrinos (B16).** Ruled route-(b) units; the window's bounds stay within model92's non-relativistic-kick validity, with the relativistic extension a labelled variant.
- **Primary:** every neutrino's coarseness under C1b lies below its validity length, so no valid bound applies.
- **Variant (K97-rel), relativistic kicks:**
$$
\kappa_b\le\frac{\sqrt2\,(\Sigma_{\max}-\Sigma_im_ic^2)}{c\sqrt{8/\pi}\,I_1\sum_im_ic^2/r_i}=4.33\times10^{-30}\,r_N\quad(\Sigma_{\max}=0.12\ \text{eV},\ \text{normal ordering},\ m_1=0).
$$

**What it means.**
- **C1, the first worked lead for r_C, does not close and does not derive.**
  - Its one-moment reading is a renaming.
  - Its over-the-hold reading survives every bound in the record. Under one further extension it makes a definite, falsifiable statement: the lightest neutrino is nearly massless.
- **That statement rests on three new statements and one unregistered extension:**
  - C1 itself;
  - a value of ħ_t of 10¹²⁶–10¹⁴⁷;
  - species-dependent coarseness;
  - relativistic GRW kicks below the neutrinos' Compton length.

  It is a lead, not a result.
