## Reading I8: candidate C4, commitment set by internal complexity

*28 October 2026. Allen: "do C4". Checked independently (`checkI8/REPORT.md`); its corrections are adopted. Ruled the same day (§8). Nothing here is registered and no candidate's value has been computed. Graded under Reading I7's criterion (derived, lead, fitted).*

### 1. Why
Reading I7 listed C4 as the only candidate for route (b) that might have a consequence the GRW-type models lack. It had to be defined first: "internal complexity" is not yet a statement in CO's terms (Note I §2–§3 and §10; Reading I6 §7).

### 2. What Allen has said
- A pattern commits on its own "by reaching an internal complexity at which it has to commit" (25 October; Note I §2, A2).
- Allen's further idea, recorded in Reading I6's draft of 27 October (§8, paraphrased there):
  1. the same atoms in different configurations would decohere differently;
  2. a pattern can hold only so much before it saturates;
  3. a complex pattern holds a tension, each part ready to commit if the others do.

### 3. What the record already has

**What "internal complexity" means in the ruled rate.**
$$
\lambda=\frac{\kappa_bmc^2}{\gamma\hbar}=\frac{E/(a\hbar)}{P_\infty},
$$
so route (b) records once per P∞ of a body's own renewals. Internal complexity, in the record, is the renewal count (rest energy) plus number density smoothed at r_C. A new C4 must read something else, per unit and linearly.

**Point 1, configuration.**
- **Above r_C, yes.** A superposition of two configurations decoheres at
$$
\Gamma=\sum_i\lambda_i\big(1-e^{-d_i^2/4r_C^2}\big)
$$
  (Reading I3 §4). Symmetrisation is subadditive for decoherence, and equal to this for a rigid body within r_C.
- **The suppression R = 1/[2n(2πr_C²)^{3/2}]** acts on force-noise effects (heating, emission, excitation, break-up). It is not a density dependence of decoherence. For decoherence the ruled GRW-type units are essentially configuration-blind, as Reading I6's drafts said.
- **At the chemical scale, no.** Isomers and conformers differ at about 1 Å, which a localisation at r_C ≥ 0.09 μm cannot see: d²/4r_C² ≲ 3×10⁻⁷. The CSL-type reading, which would see more, closes the window (Reading I6 §6).
- **Route (a) already depends on configuration** (polarisability, thermal emission; Reading I1). Note I §9 calls that "not remarkable".

**Point 3, tension.** It splits in two:
- **(3a)** One part's commitment *localises* the others. That is Reading I3's two-place rule plus amplification, and it is in the record. The rate is Nλ(1 − e^{−D²/4r_C²}), which equals Σλ_i only for D ≫ r_C.
- **(3b)** One part's commitment *commits* the others, a cascade. That is excluded:
  - T1 "commits only that part" (Reading I3 rulings 1 and 3);
  - T2 is excluded (Q4);
  - whole-body deposits are excluded (Reading I6 ruling 6).
- **The route-(a) cascade,** where a route-(b) record makes its unit committed matter and route (a) follows for its neighbours, adds nothing beyond T1 and route (a). A solid's interior is recorded only internally and deposits nothing (Reading I6 §4).

**Point 2, saturation, has counterparts the record has already graded.**
- **R6's "record when blocked"** (Reading I4 §3): λ ∝ (1 − N)c/ℓ. It is zero in the vacuum and tied to the potential, so it is excluded by Q6.
- **R11 read literally**, commitment where P falls to 2p_s (Note I §6 (ix)): never reached in the vacuum.
- **Note I §6 (i):** commitment when the pattern's own draw lowers P by p_s. It is excluded because there is no self-interaction.
- **No capacity exists in the record.** Note D §2: "no per-place capacity"; "P04 gives non-negativity, not an upper bound"; "the bandwidth quantum is ABSENT and UNFORCED". R1's outflow is unbounded. p_s is a crossover and P∞ the vacuum's level; neither is a capacity.
- **A precedent in the earlier work** compared a threshold with amplification, adopted amplification, and kept the threshold as the alternative. The two differ in predicting a **sudden versus gradual loss of interference with size**.

### 4. Constraints any C4 must meet
- **No-signalling:** per unit, linear in the state, branch by branch, and not a functional of the unit's conditional wavefunction (Q4; Reading I3 §4 and ruling 5). Run 95 detected three such rules failing: a rate set by the conditional state (NL1), a nonlinear outcome rule (NL3), and the hold filled by the whole pattern's stock ("per pattern").
- **Route (b) is a rate, not a threshold** (Note I §3 and §5, a ruled consequence). A threshold beside route (b) reopens that ruling, and must say so.
- **No trigger read from the local count, and no branches in the medium** (Note I §5; Q1). A condition on possibility at the places reads the local count.
- **Q6:** no dependence on the external potential, which interferometry excludes.
- **Q7:** energy superpositions must keep their coherence where it is seen.
- **Q2 and Reading I6 §3:** a condition that stays met fires again and again, which bears on the heating bounds and on what a renewal cannot be.
- **Interference data:** 172 kDa clusters coherent over about 80 nm for 12 ms; Rb atoms over 54 cm for 2 s; clock states in superposition.
- **Run 96's window,** with B16.

### 5. Candidates (statements only; no values computed)

**C4-β′. Saturation as the hold's ceiling** (the most CO-native reading of point 2).
- *The statement:* a unit's hold cannot fill faster than once per tick. That is CO's own ceiling (the event layer's "a place committing at most once per ħ"; no infinities). The condition is that the unit's stock reach one hold:
$$
q\ \ge\ H=2\hbar_tP_\infty\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad E\ \gtrsim\ \frac{aP_\infty\hbar c}{\ell},
$$
  with ħ_t cancelling (q/H = E·t_tick/(aP∞ħ)).
- *Status:* it needs no new primitive and no C1b input.
  - Per unit, it is inert: the threshold energy is decades above any unit's.
  - Per body, it is Run 95's "per pattern" control, which signals.
  - It is registered only to put on record that the one capacity-type quantity already in the primitives does nothing.

**C4-β. Saturation with a new capacity primitive.**
- *The new statements:*
  - a capacity per place, a new primitive with a value, and a choice of whether it is local (if local, Q6 must be met);
  - a definition of a unit's "spread" that is linear and branch-wise, not a functional of its wavefunction (the plain "places its pattern spans" is state-dependent and signals);
  - what happens when the condition is met: once, at a rate, and over what localisation width.
- *Its direction:* stock per place grows as a unit's spread shrinks, so compact, bound units saturate first and delocalised superpositions least. Internal complexity becomes compactness.
- *Its distinctive observable,* against GRW: a sudden rather than gradual onset of lost interference with size.
- *Not used:* a joint test with C1b's ħ_t. Run 97 calls that ħ_t fitted, and with it a nucleon carries 10¹¹⁰–10¹²⁹ pair-units, so the comparison would be decided by inspection.
- *Grading:* a lead needing three new statements, one of which (a capacity) Note D says the record lacks.

**C4-γ. Internal relations commit.**
- *Not in CO's terms without a new primitive.* A CO relation is adjacency between places, which is what space is; a chemical bond is not one. Letting bonds commit changes a primitive, and a bond has no worldline for ruling 11's rate.
- *Its effect is negligible in any case:*
  - counted by energy, it is ≲10⁻⁹ of the units' rate;
  - counted per relation, isomers often have equal bond counts (every CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ alkane isomer), and an energy-independent rate would reopen M2;
  - bond geometry differs at the Å scale, invisible at r_C;
  - route (a) already tells isomers apart through polarisability.

**C4-δ. A rate gain from internal correlations.** Excluded. A per-unit, linear rate depends on correlations only through T1 (that is, (3a)) and symmetrisation. The whole-pattern form failed in Run 95.

### 6. What this means
- **Points 1 and 3 are in the record** only at the trivial level (above r_C, plus route (a); localisation, not cascade).
- **Point 2 is the only open direction.** In the record's primitives it is inert (C4-β′).
- **To make it act, CO needs a primitive it does not have:** a capacity per place, plus a linear definition of spread and a rule for what saturation does (C4-β). That is the clearest finding so far of a **missing primitive**, in the sense of Allen's "if CO is missing a primitive or ruling". Its signature would be a sudden onset of lost interference with size.

### 7. Choices for a ruling
1. Whether §3's mapping is right.
2. Whether to register C4-β′ as the baseline (cheap, expected inert).
3. **Whether CO should have a capacity per place** (a new primitive). If so, its value or bound, and whether it is local. Then C4-β written out in full: the linear spread and the rule after saturation.
4. Whether C4-γ and C4-δ are recorded as excluded.

3. **C4-β′ is registered as the baseline,** and **C4-β is written out in full** ("go ahead as recommended").
4. **C4-γ and C4-δ are recorded as excluded.**
