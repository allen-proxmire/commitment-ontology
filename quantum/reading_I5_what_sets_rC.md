## Reading I5: what in the record could set route (b)'s coarseness r_C (a reading, with post hoc arithmetic on Run 93's window)

*27 October 2026. Checked independently (`checkI5/REPORT.md`); its corrections are adopted below; ruled the same day (§8). Nothing here is registered. The numbers in §4 are arithmetic on Run 93's registered window W (`model93.py`, unchanged), not a test.*

*Note, 28 October (after Run 96): Run 96's window supersedes the numbers in §4–§8. The family r_C = ℓP∞ⁿ now needs n ≈ 0.70–1.18 at p_s's bound (0.35–1.16 with p_s free), and 5.25×10²⁶ ≲ aP∞ ≲ 1.78×10³⁵ (Reading I7, draft).*

**The question.** Readings I0, I1 and I4 left one thing open in route (b): its coarseness r_C. Reading I4 ruled κ_b = 1/(aP∞), so the rate is set by the vacuum's possibility per place, P∞. r_C is not fixed by any rule (I4 ruling 5), and I4 is not to be registered until a rule fixes it (I4 ruling 6). Note I (I2) asks for r_C's origin to be registered before its value is computed. This reading collects the lengths the record supplies, checks each against W, and sets out what a ruling would have to choose.

### 2. Lengths the record supplies directly

- **The hop, or the pair, r_C ≈ ℓ.** At p_s's lower bound, ℓ ≈ 10⁻³⁵–10⁻³² m: below the model's non-relativistic floor (5.15×10⁻¹⁶ m) and below W's lower end by 22 or more decades. Note I §6 (iii) already excludes it for position-like channels. If p_s is larger, the engine's hop grows as √p_s, but it stays excluded by the weak-field edge: with r_C = ℓ,
$$
\frac{\kappa_b r_C^2}{\ell_P^2}=\frac{0.99\pi}{3}\,\frac{p_s}{P_\infty}\le6.5\times10^{-6}\ll1,
$$
  since P∞/p_s ≳ 1.6×10⁵. The engine's ħ = 5 ticks (Run 15) gives r_C = 5ℓ, excluded the same way. **Excluded.**
- **One hold's light-crossing, r_C = ħ_tℓ = aħc/E_pair.** A length, but ħ_t is free, so this renames r_C rather than setting it. It becomes a rule only if one renewal's energy E_pair is identified with something. Every identification below is borrowed (ONTOLOGY lists particle masses as borrowed); none is CO's own. With a = 0.1, 1, 2π:

| E_pair identified with | r_C = aħc/E_pair | against W |
|---|---|---|
| the Planck energy | 1.6×10⁻³⁶–1.0×10⁻³⁴ m | excluded |
| a nucleon's rest energy | 2.1×10⁻¹⁷–1.3×10⁻¹⁵ m | excluded (below the nucleon floor for a ≤ 1; above it at a = 2π, but below W's end by more than 5 decades) |
| an electron's rest energy | 3.9×10⁻¹⁴–2.4×10⁻¹² m | excluded (below the electron floor, 9.46×10⁻¹³ m, or below W's end by 2 decades) |
| an atomic or photon energy (13.6 eV, 1 eV, a CMB photon) | 1.5×10⁻⁹ – 2×10⁻³ m | inside W, but the energy depends on the pattern, which one κ_b (M2) and one r_C do not allow |

  Reading I4 §3 found the same cap from the other side: r_C ≥ 2.32×10⁻¹⁰ m needs E_pair ≤ a×850 eV, so one renewal cannot carry a unit's rest energy.
- **Run 74's δu** (Note I §10 asks whether it is the same scale). It is unfixed, like ħ_t, so it renames r_C. Run 74's δu ≳ τ_h/ε gives c·δu ~ ℓP∞/p_s, which is outside W at the data limits.
- **The pattern's own extent.** Not fixed by any rule, and it would make r_C depend on the state. Route (b) as ruled has one r_C. A bound electron's extent, the Bohr radius 5.3×10⁻¹¹ m, is below W's lower end in any case. **Not a rule as the rulings stand.**
- **Other lengths in the record.**
  - R11's core radius 3Gm/c² depends on mass (about 10⁻⁵⁴ m for a nucleon) and is never reached in the vacuum.
  - The medium's diffusion (removed for the well by R4) and Run 81's lattice-gas sound give no physical length without ħ_t.
  - The 100 pc dark-matter smoothing and photon coherence's r_C ≳ √N·D/2π are bounds, not mechanisms.
- **Cosmic lengths.** c/H₀ = 1.37×10²⁶ m and Λ^(−1/2) = 9.5×10²⁵ m are about eight decades above the ceiling. **Excluded.**

So no length the record supplies directly lies in W.

### 3. Lengths built from the record's scales

What remains is a combination of the hop with CO's dimensionless scales, P∞ and p_s. Under κ_b = 1/(aP∞), a combination with P∞ alone would set the rate and the coarseness with the same number, which is Note I's consilience target, and for a fixed power one P∞ must then put both inside W at once.

That is a constraint on the exponent of a dimensional family, not on a mechanism. The exponent is a free continuous power, and the hop (through p_s) and a are free too. It is the r_C counterpart of Note I §6 (iv): "m_P/P∞^p leaves a window today for every p < 1.28". Note I §9 lists a value "picked from free combinations of the scales" as not remarkable.

### 4. The family r_C = ℓP∞ⁿ against W (post hoc arithmetic)

`scan_rC_family.py` scans log₁₀P∞ from 10.8 to 130 for each n, and asks whether (1/(aP∞), ℓP∞ⁿ) lies inside Run 93's primary W ('indep', per population, primary B13). The check (`checkI5/check_scan.py`) recomputed the edges by bisection and in closed form:
- **lower edge,** at W's pinch r_lo = 2.32×10⁻¹⁰ m where the weak-field edge meets SNO:
$$
n_{\min}=\frac{\log(r_{\rm lo}/\ell)}{2\log(r_{\rm lo}/\ell_P)-\log a};
$$
- **upper edge,** where the r_C ceiling meets lunar laser ranging's aP∞ ≥ 3.66×10²⁴:
$$
n_{\max}=\frac{\log(1.5\times10^{18}\,\text{m}/\ell)}{\log(3.66\times10^{24}/a)}.
$$

| hop | a = 0.1 | a = 1 | a = 2π |
|---|---|---|---|
| P08 (ℓ_P) | 0.490–2.072 | 0.500–2.156 | 0.504–2.229 |
| the engine's, at p_s = 4×10⁵ (10^2.31, 10^2.81, 10^3.21 ℓ_P) | 0.445–1.982 | 0.444–2.042 | 0.443–2.094 |

- **Below n_min,** κ_b falls as 1/P∞ while the weak-field edge ℓ_P²/r_C² falls only as P∞^(−2n), so κ_b drops under the edge before r_C reaches W.
- **Above n_max,** the r_C ceiling is reached before a·P∞ reaches the 3.66×10²⁴ that lunar laser ranging requires.
- **With p_s free,** the engine's hop ℓ ∝ √p_s can be larger, and the lower edge disappears: ℓ(p_s)·P∞ⁿ reaches W for n as small as about 0.05 (for example P∞ ≈ 10⁵², p_s ≈ 10⁴⁷). The lower edge exists only if the hop is fixed.

Sample powers (a = 1 unless stated):

| n | P∞ allowed (log₁₀) | r_C (m) |
|---|---|---|
| ½, P08 hop | none at a = 1 or 2π (κ_b r_C²/ℓ_P² = 1/a exactly, on the weak-field edge). At a = 0.1: 50.8–93.6 | 4.1×10⁻¹⁰ to 1.0×10¹² |
| ½, the engine's hop | about 47–92.6 | about 10⁻⁸ to 10¹⁴ (inside the weak-field edge by 10^4.2–10^7 across a) |
| 1 | 32–34 to 50–53 | 0.03–7 m to the ceiling |
| 2 | about 24–26.5 | 5×10¹² m to the ceiling, across a |

`scan.log` has the first scan's rows. Its n = ½, P08, a = 1 row (from 2.36×10⁻¹⁰ m) is a rounding artifact, as above. It also pairs every hop with every a; only the pairings in the table above are the engine's.

**Against the gas self-gravity bound.** That bound (Run 93's addendum; checked in `checkGas/REPORT.md`) is not yet registered. With a rough cap (κ_b ≲ 10⁻²⁹ above 3×10¹⁵ m, 10⁻³² above 3×10¹⁷ m):
- n = 2 is excluded for every engine-consistent hop;
- it survives only for the P08 hop (a = 1 or 2π, r_C up to 3×10¹⁵ m), plus a sliver for 10^2.5 ℓ_P at a = 2π;
- n_max falls to 1.88–1.98 for the engine's hops.

**What this shows.**
- **For a fixed power n between about 0.44 and 2.1, one P∞ can set both the rate and the coarseness.** With p_s free, any n ≲ 2.2 can.
- **It is not a prediction.** n is free, and each n allows a decade or more of P∞. Nothing in the record picks n. A rule would have to say why the coarseness grows as that power of the vacuum's possibility.
- **What W does constrain:** n ≲ 2.2, and 3.7×10²⁴ ≲ aP∞ ≲ 4×10⁹². Across Run 93's lunar-laser-ranging variants, the lower end ranges from 3.6×10²² to 3.7×10²⁵, so P∞ ≳ 6×10²¹ at the weakest setting with a = 2π.
- **A test of the family can no longer be registered blind to n.** The allowed range is now known, so only a mechanism that fixes n beforehand would count (Note I §8 I2, §9).

### 5. A side note: the hypothetical mass threshold

Note I §7 records a consistency window 6.4×10¹⁰ ≲ P∞ ≲ 7.7×10¹³ under a hypothetical threshold rule (m_c = m_P/P∞, with Q1). Under κ_b = 1/(aP∞), W needs a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴. The gap is 9.9 decades at a = 2π, 10.7 at a = 1 and 11.7 at a = 0.1, and still 7.9 or more across the lunar-laser-ranging variants. The threshold readings are kept only for the record (Note I §6, after the 27 October rulings), so this is not a conflict with a ruling. If a threshold reading were revived, it could not share P∞ with route (b).

### 6. What this means

- **The record does not set r_C.** No length it supplies directly lies in W:
  - the hop and the pair are excluded at any p_s (by length at p_s's bound, by the weak-field edge above it);
  - one hold's length and δu are unfixed, and every available identification of E_pair is borrowed and either too short or pattern-dependent;
  - cosmic lengths are too long.
- **What could set it is a rule tying r_C to P∞,** the scale that already sets κ_b. In the family r_C = ℓP∞ⁿ, any fixed n from about 0.44 to 2.1 admits a P∞, always with aP∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴. Such a rule would make route (b) one-parameter (Note I's consilience target) and turn W into a range for P∞.
- **No such rule is in the record.** Picking n would be a new postulate, and dimensional analysis unless a mechanism is given: for example, why a commitment localises over the distance at which the possibility a pattern carries equals some multiple of the vacuum's.
- **I4 therefore stays unregistered** under its ruling 6, unless a rule is adopted.

### 7. Choices for a ruling

1. **Whether r_C is a new primitive scale,** kept as an input bounded by W (as p_s and P∞ are bounded by data), or must come from a rule.
2. **If from a rule: what it is tied to:**
   - P∞ (the family of §4, Note I's consilience target);
   - one renewal's energy E_pair (which needs E_pair identified; every available identification fails);
   - something not yet in the record.
3. **The hop:** whether it is P08's ℓ_P (which with the engine's G needs p_s ≈ 0.0015–0.55, excluded by Run 24), or the engine's ℓ(a, p_s). If the engine's, whether p_s is fixed. Without that, the family has two parameters and no lower edge.
4. **a,** which sets the engine's hop as well as κ_b.
5. **Whether r_C may differ by species** (relaxing the single r_C; Note I §10 leaves open which patterns share it).
6. **If a test of the family is ever registered:** which W it uses (the B13 variant, 'indep' or 'com', per population or per pattern, with or without the gas bound). Since the allowed n are now known, only a mechanism that fixes n beforehand would make it a test.
7. **Whether to record in Note I §7** that the threshold window and route (b)'s P∞ are incompatible (by 10–12 decades), and that W bounds aP∞ above at about 4×10⁹².

### 8. Rulings (27 October, "as recommended")

1. **r_C is a primitive scale for now,** an input bounded by W, as P∞ and p_s are bounded by data. It is not derived from a rule.
2. **No test of the family r_C = ℓP∞ⁿ is registered** unless a mechanism fixes n beforehand.
3. **The hop stays an open tension** (as decided 20 October): P08's ℓ_P against the engine's ℓ(a, p_s).
4. **a is not ruled.**
5. **One r_C for every species.**
6. **Recorded in Note I §7:** the hypothetical threshold window (P∞ ≲ 7.7×10¹³) cannot share P∞ with route (b), which needs a·P∞ ≳ 3.7×10²⁴ (10–12 decades apart); and W bounds a·P∞ above at about 4×10⁹².

So I4 stays unregistered (its ruling 6), and route (b) keeps two parameters, κ_b = 1/(aP∞) and r_C.
