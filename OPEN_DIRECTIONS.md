# Open directions

What is open, graded as elsewhere (candidate: before a ruling; shape: a form without a rule; silence: the primitives do not speak), with what it would take. Results-only documents carry what stands; this file carries what does not yet. Working detail is in `RECORD_OF_METHOD/`.

## Space and gravity

- **Second-order field equations.** Assumed as a Lovelock hypothesis. One attempt to derive it (mismatch as a count, cost linear in the count) was refuted on reading: the loop deficit is a signed non-abelian angle, not a count, and a scale-free cost favours R² as readily as R. What it would take: an integer-valued functional of commitments whose continuum limit is Regge's action with the sign handled (Benincasa–Dowker 2010 is the model).
- **The clock field slaved to the occupancies.** Open hypothesis; a dynamical scalar would make the theory scalar–tensor, excluded.
- **The radiation era.** Not derived; pressure drawing is not in the rules.
- **The now.** Candidate ruling: what has committed, for a commitment, is its past light cone; spacelike events are not ordered with respect to it. This is relativity's own point-relative becoming (Stein 1991 in flat spacetime, given a time orientation, which record-only-grows supplies; Rideout–Sorkin 2000 for a discrete network); CO adds nothing and owes nothing here. Retires the "universal edge" earlier entries carried. Exposed by it: the beginning ("every place commits at once") and the gravity rule's wording ("possibility moves toward where it is engaged") are written with a simultaneity; the first can be read as "the first commitments are unordered" since there is no distance yet, the second lives through the covariant form. Both to be reworded.

## Matter

- **Statistics.** Two patterns on one stock: merge or exclude. One-pattern Dirac only; no rule for two. Checked: the one-pattern walk, second-quantised in the standard way (site modes with canonical brackets, positive Fock metric, generator on the branch |ε| < π, vacuum the lowest state), admits only anticommutators (Pauli 1940); the matter sector is compatible with Fermi statistics. Not forced: three of those inputs are standard field theory's, not CO's (the fourth, the branch, is now fixed by a ruling: the generator is odd under C, which selects |ε| < π everywhere the band does not meet ±π, i.e. outside the doubler sector). Readings tried and refuted on cold reading: a 2π-turn exchange (relocates the choice); a sign criterion (the W boson); count positivity as the bounded-below premise (the count is constant under either statistics). What would move the remaining three is unknown; lattice field theory has no spin–statistics theorem, and the QCA Dirac constructions take fermionic statistics as input. Must still split He-4 and Rb-87 (merge), He-3 and electrons (exclude), cavity photons (merge).
- **Doublers.** Reduced to the standard Wilson form by two candidate rulings; extra species remain at the hop scale and are predicted charged particles; their absence bounds the hop, ℓ ≲ 6 × 10⁻¹⁹ m. Open as a bound. What it would take: a cancellation (staggering or a covering map) or an account of the copies.
- **Dark matter.** Shape: a pattern that commits but never measures. A renewing pattern carrying no sign, one tile, engaging only free stock: it has mass, pools possibility and bends h, and never engages another pattern's transit, so it is never resolved and leaves no readable record. Permitted by the rules as written; not predicted (nothing picks its mass, abundance or number of kinds). CO's gravity is GR's, so the invisible mass is needed for GR's reasons and no MOND is offered. One weak consequence: with no sign to carry a ratio it is unmeasurable by construction, so direct detection would be null by rule.
- **The masses.** Silence.
- **1/137.** Input.
- **g = 2.** Conditional on the coupling.

## Nucleus

- **Three-tile patterns.** Shape, after one cold reading: the proton as three tiles bound by kinship; colour as the bookkeeping (gauge redundancy, correctly); confinement as a held string with grain ≈ 0.5 fm, a new constant (Λ_QCD). Removed on reading: tension from the tile mass (falsified by flavour-universality of σ and by pure-glue string tension; a tile has no mass by the sector's own definition); "never commits alone" as stated (false of the plasma and of deep-inelastic scattering). Missing and required: gluon degrees of freedom (self-coupling, running coupling, half the momentum). Whether tiles and kinship are a third primitive by another name is open. One narrow question kept: whether "one pattern" can be made to coincide with the colour-singlet constraint (Gauss's law) rather than a correlation.
- **The count three.** Silence, now stated as a result in `NUCLEUS.md` ("The wall, stated"): the inventory is abelian; the nucleus needs a self-coupling three-way matching; four routes closed; a third primitive would be a labelled kinship at the standard theory's price, cascading only to the hadron spectrum, so not earned. Given the label, self-coupling follows from carriers being patterns (candidate reading). Excluded on cold reading (R11): three copies of the walk sharing one flip are three decoupled free walks with a circulant mass texture (three flavours, not colour); the cyclic shift is not the centre of SU(3) (which is a scalar phase), and n copies give Z_n for any n, so the three was an input. Carried forward: no shared internal flip can give colour or confinement, since it commutes with flavour-blind hops; any route to the three needs a position-dependent, non-commuting internal coupling, a gauge field, which the inventory lacks. The author's moiré idea survives only as a question: can two things turning against each other make a comparison that varies from place to place. Shape (channels): if neighbouring places share k parallel relations, a hop is an exit-and-entry choice, a k × k matrix on the link, position-dependent and non-commuting, from the lattice-formation ruling without a posited label; k itself unset (capacity, δ territory). Read: sliding relabels channels freely (the gauge redundancy) and leaves their count; the formation rule's pairing of in- to out-relations is the matching, a k × k unitary on the link with amplitudes, non-commuting for k ≥ 2. Three clauses from existing rulings; the count k was then computed (R12): starting with four channels per pair, the formation model drives the multiplicity to one on more than 99% of pairs at every quanta-per-place tried, because the pairing rule locks each quantum to one channel and thinning removes the rest. Channels excluded as the source of the matching. The wall, final form: the inventory's one non-abelian matching is the frame (space's); colour needs a complex three-component spatial scalar with a unitary matching, which nothing in two primitives supplies. The earliest "one" where the nucleus needs "three" is the amplitude rule, which fixes one amplitude's algebra and is silent on how many a transit carries; the internal-state ruling admits factors beyond the four. The slot for a third primitive is located (a factor of what some transit carries), but cold reading shows a candidate at the electromagnetic sector's standing needs at least four posits (which transit; a U(3) matching on relations with its cost; unit determinant, else a fifth force on baryon number excluded at 10⁻⁴⁷; the scale), and even then the proton is unbuilt (flavour, statistics, the singlet rule, second-quantised gluons, mass). Shape; the wall is the sector's last word.
- **Entanglement.** Candidate: a commitment belongs to the pattern (one pattern sampled twice); a commitment resolves exactly what it engages; a pair is one pattern with two places and one undivided stock, made by a conserving commitment, and the measurement is the cut. Cost, open: one commitment at two spacelike places does not fit the now ruling as stated; the relativistic collapse literature pays it as two events with a nonlocal covariant law (Tumulka) or a wavefunction-fixed foliation (Dürr et al. 2014); CO has not chosen.
- **Pair and tile.** Dropped on reading, 2026-11-06. Entanglement is a property of a state (free to hold after preparation, destroyed by measurement); confinement is a property of the dynamics (an energy cost, switched off above T_c). "One object at two strengths" named no parameter. What the comparison did show: along a kinship the dial is the bookkeeping the kinship carries, none (an entangled pair), a single phase (abelian, 1/r, cuttable: Cooper pairs are weakly bound this way), or a three-way matching (non-abelian, a tube); that abelian forces do not confine in three dimensions and non-abelian ones do is standard, and the entangled pair is not a point on that dial but a different kind of thing.
