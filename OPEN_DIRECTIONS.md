## Space and gravity

- **What carries the field.** Candidate. The free stock cannot: read in time, ratio hopping is diffusion, D ≤ 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ m²/s, so a body at rest builds its field only out to millimetres–metres in the age of the universe, and a moving body leaves a wake. Free streaming gives 1/r² (excluded by torsion balances to 50 μm); a stock obeying a wave equation would radiate a scalar (excluded). So it is carried by the network's own parts, staggered as exact no-backdrop selects among the placements tested (nearest-neighbour range, run G3): lapse and stretch at places, frame dragging in the relations' one-way part, shear on the squares between relations. G's value follows by static consistency at the body. *Takes:* the nonlinear rule for the relations (a lattice tetrad form), for strong fields. Open: whether a relation's stiffness equals the hop's conductance.
- **The update rule.** Linear, written: each part of the field updated each tick by one stable stencil (three axis steps, with a fourth-order Wilson-type term lifting the field's doubler), signals never faster than c, gauge kept since the walk's total energy and momentum are exactly conserved. Preferred-frame effects of order (ℓ/r)². *Takes:* the nonlinear form.
- **The nonlinear rule.** Candidate. With no backdrop at every order (P7e, chosen), the only consistent continuation of the linear cost is Einstein–Hilbert plus Λ (Wald 1986; Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri, Henneaux 2001), given locality and two derivatives at every order and Lorentz-invariant interactions; matter's coupling excludes the free branch. It supplies the self-coupling P6 names (in the static case); that the draw becomes record does not follow and stays a choice; the static lapse is GR's at every order. *Takes:* the two assumptions (the second is the frame problem below); the nonlinear cost on the lattice carriers. Its natural form is Regge's (area × deficit at hinges): on CO's lattice, with each cell cut into flat simplices and lengths from the carriers, it reduces at weak field to the linear cost, isotropic and relabelling-free, with corrections of order (kℓ)² (run E1, Euclidean). Open: the cut (hinges as the cut's triangles or as the squares) and the longer diagonals' lengths. Strong fields reach matter's step as the shift alone, with lapse and stretch at 1, wherever spacetime admits unit lapse and flat slices (chosen, candidate). On the flow step a pattern at rest in the falling frame crosses a Painlevé–Gullstrand horizon on the falling path (run F1e, 1D radial; within 0.4%) and keeps to it inside, on the falling path to 0.07% down to 0.3r_s (recount; drift within the tick limit, run F6); the tick's only limit is τ_f(3·N/ψ² + mN + |b_x| + |b_y| + |b_z| + ¼|∇×b|) < π (a sufficient bound); exactly, for a uniform drift at τ_f = 0.4 and m = 0, a drift above about 5 times light's speed along an axis and 3.2 along a body diagonal. On the step a 2D black hole holds, light escaping from outside and swept in from inside (F2e), and a cloud sourcing its own drift collapses toward GR's dust path, closer as the cloud grows, crossing its horizons on time (F2f, radial reduction).
- **Inside matter and the radiation era.** Candidate: travel draws twice, light twice its energy, field energy as an amount, giving Tolman's (ρ + S)N. Matter's step on the curved network: candidate. The field reaches matter's step as one Hamiltonian (P7f restated, chosen, candidate). On the step, in one and two dimensions: free fall at g c²(1 + rm), light bending twice the clock-only value, the closed loop (runs F1a–c); in three dimensions, free fall the same along an axis and a body diagonal and light bending twice the clock-only value, in a weak uniform field (F4), and past a point mass (F5). The shift's pushes, the curl-free free fall, light past a point mass and the closed loop in two dimensions also hold on the step (F2a–d). Beyond weak fields, by analogy with a known construction (Arrighi and Facchini's curved-spacetime quantum walk, the massive Dirac equation for any bounded metric), with its frame read from CO's carriers (lapse 1 − h at places; shift and edge lengths on relations; angles from shear on squares). - **The now.** Posited for the primitives: a commitment's now is its past light cone. The walk's lattice picks a frame (see Lorentz invariance with loops).

The epoch is about 6 e-folds in length. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md).
- **The primordial ripples.** Silence: an initial condition of the beginning. Working picture: part of the first event itself, whose unevenness must be 4.6 × 10⁻⁵ in the places' stretch at every size from 10²⁶ to 10³⁰ hops, not averaging away ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/the_beginning.md)). ## Matter

- **Lorentz invariance with loops.** Conditional on tuning (chosen: the frame kept). The walk's finite-degree causal structure picks a frame; with loops, species-dependent speeds of order α/π follow (Collins et al. 2004), against bounds near 10⁻²⁰. Every sector carries a speed that symmetry leaves free, gravity's included (run G1); the common tick sets them equal at tree level, and loops would split them; GW170817 bounds gravity's against light's to ~10⁻¹⁵. Gravity's nonlinear rule rests on the same question: its uniqueness assumes Lorentz-invariant interactions. In the long-wavelength theory the ruling (no backdrop at every order) excludes frame-picking terms, so a frame can come only from the lattice. Held exactly on the lattice, no backdrop would conserve the total energy current and force equal speeds at dimension four; but for interacting matter on a lattice it cannot hold exactly (scattering kinematics: energy, crystal momentum and the energy current over-constrain 2 → 2 events), so it holds only up to order (kℓ)² and loops can split speeds as before. A cancellation between the walk and the links is unsupported: on lattices without a symmetry mixing time and space, gauge and fermion speeds renormalise differently and are tuned separately. The step's cube symmetry removes direction-dependent counter-terms (the earlier palindromic walk needed four for light, to 1 part in 10³⁰; record, I4). Every exit read is closed for CO's network as it stands: a common origin for walk and links (induced fields split at about 1/ln(1/mℓ)), a deformed Lorentz symmetry (exact only for free patterns; additive lattice momentum allows no continuous boosts), and boosts on pairs with backreaction (which would imply equal one-loop speed shifts, which Wilson-type lattices do not have; by analogy, CO's own one-loop run not done). **Tuning (chosen).** The frame is kept, since it carries the photon and neutrino tests; the one exit would remove the photon test, and in Snyder's form the neutrino test too. the species' speeds are held equal by counter-terms set by hand. The one door left is a network whose coordinates do not commute (κ-Poincaré-type, relative locality), with the soccer-ball cost. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md).
- **Statistics.** Two patterns of one kind add a direct and an exchanged route with a relative factor. Candidate (P8b): ±1 in three dimensions given a flat exchange factor (Leinaas–Myrheim); the sign is the spinor's full turn carried by the places' frames, (−1)^{2s}, splitting helium-4, rubidium-87 and photons (merge) from helium-3 and electrons (exclude). *Takes:* excluding parastatistics for three or more; the two-pattern dynamics on one stock. Excluding parastatistics is the same question as an internal label: parastatistics is ordinary statistics plus an internal compact symmetry (Doplicher–Roberts), and Greenberg's order-3 paraquarks were colour's precursor (colour itself is gauged SU(3)). With no internal label, walk patterns are ordinary fermions (the filled sea of C6 rules out bosons).
- **Two patterns.** Candidate: carried quantities are branched with what passes; the bound/emitted split. Gives helium, positronium and the 21 cm line by identification. *Takes:* the covariant coupled walk between commitments. With the walk a field (C6) and speeds tuned, two patterns are lattice QED's two-body sector, by identification (helium and the 21 cm line given the nucleus and the proton's moment as inputs); the candidate is that CO's branching and bound/emitted rules coincide with it.
- **Engagement versus route.** Candidate (C8, chosen): a contact through a channel is an engagement when its final state is broad against the coupling (|V| < ħΓ/4, no oscillating back) and the chain it opens reaches N quanta; engagements trigger both the Lüders update and gravity's source. N is a constant of CO's own (posited): every route tried is closed (four: ties to CO's other constants, the web's capacity, a cap on the joint amplitude, the final state's continuum), so only experiment sets it (N ≳ ~50 under the readings in the N note); exchanges between patterns inside one body are passages that count toward the chain, so a body of many patterns commits internally once a chain inside it reaches N (its place an independent draw, recording nothing about where the body is). Bounded: a released-energy threshold lies above the eV scale. How fast breadth grows depends on the Hamiltonian, so the signature is size, not time. A chain is the quanta reached from the opening contact by the system's own hand-ons; a step whose quantum a drive supplies extends no chain (chosen, candidate), so a quantum computer's driven gates add nothing to a chain, and its quantum-passing gates do where the circuit scrambles (reading in [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/quantum_computing.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/quantum_computing.md)).
- **No spontaneous collapse.** Candidate: a pattern left alone is never resolved; quantum mechanics' own prediction.
- **Doublers.** Needed: the copies the Wilson term leaves above 10¹⁴ GeV are not particles. At a zone corner with n components at π the step is e^{−iτ_fβ(m + 2rn)}: their rest energy is mostly the Wilson turn, which is part of the hop and not a commitment (chosen, candidate), so they are not matter. As particles they would violate the equivalence principle: under P7f restated their Wilson masses scale with light's local speed N/ψ², so in a field they fall at about 0.6 of matter's rate (run F1d), and the shift drags them the opposite way (G7, earlier walk). The other exit, a field rule that scales every band with the clock (N multiplying all of H), is open on the flow step, where it is a unitary flow; it was closed on the earlier walk (G9); no ruling yet. As stable particles they are excluded a second way: the beginning ends at about ħc/ℓ, near their masses (1 to 3 times, independent of ℓ), so it would make them in bulk, and no annihilation brings a relic of 10¹⁵ GeV within 10¹⁹ of the observed density. So "not particles" is needed, given that ending. On the staggered walk (a shape, quark sector; Nucleus: baryons as twists), the copies are instead its two kinds, which share one charge. *Takes:* whether their energy passes into the light species before the epoch ends; a single hop-scale event passes about 0.4α² of it (run G10, earlier walk), and no conserved number keeps it. Whether it empties them before the balance with radiation breaks is borderline: it hangs on how fast hop-scale events fade as the universe cools, as (T/T_ℓ)^p, with p ≲ 3.4 leaving no relic and p ≈ 3 by a rough count. With smooth births (needed by light), their energy is carried to longer wavelengths as places are born, like the rest of the radiation, so none stays at the corner (run G11, 1D, earlier walk).
- **Number change.** Candidate, an identification (C6, C7: the walk read as a field with filled negative states; patterns after the beginning start only in pairs of opposite sign). Needed by laboratory pair creation and the hot early universe's electron–positron bath. Charge and net count are conserved. *Costs, open:* the filled states' vacuum energy (the cosmological-constant problem); loops, which bring the frame problem's α/π speed split.
- **The weak interaction.** Silence. Not in CO's inventory. Needed for neutrinos (the relic background, made in the hot era), for kinds to change (n → p e ν̄), and for the nuclear processes the Sun runs on. *Takes:* a further coupling of the walk, which would have to be chiral (acting on one handedness only). Shape: CO's inner ring with two walls (tuning 2) is Kaplan's periodic domain wall, the start of Grabowska and Kaplan's proposal (gauge fields on one wall, carried across by a flow), at the cost of re-reading what the walls do ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/weak_interaction.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/weak_interaction.md)).
- **Loop effects.** QED parts by identification, conditional on tuning: with species' speeds tuned, the walk and its U(1) links are identified with lattice QED, and the QED contributions follow from its continuum limit (speeds, mass and charge tuned): the electron's anomalous moment to QED's order, the running of α, the Casimir force. Hadronic and weak parts (the muon's moment, a_e at its measured precision, the proton radius in the Lamb shift) wait on an internal label and the weak interaction. CO adds nothing of its own to them.
- **Dark matter.** Shape: a pattern that commits but never measures. Permitted, not predicted. Given the shape, the rules now say: it gravitates with the same single coupling (linear order), new places make none of it, it is free of the frame problem's loop split, and, given CO's present inventory, it is a fermion and so not ultralight (m ≥ 0.13 keV from dwarf galaxies; fuzzy dark matter excluded). Matter over antimatter: only the first commitments start renewing patterns (C7, chosen, candidate), so the photon ratio is a shape, η ≈ N₀(P_max − P_b)/E₀ × (0.1–1), the first places' room as a share of the beginning's excess, given that the excess ends as light in heat balance and a quantum per tick carries the hop's energy. So the matter-to-light ratio is really a measurement of how far over capacity the very first commitments were: with the gap above ħ, each began at least 10⁸–10⁹ quanta over capacity (shape, conditional; space and gravity record, 13 Nov); its value is open ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)).

## Nucleus

- **Colour.** Candidate (C9, chosen): a quark is a pattern of a second kind carrying a complex three-component colour state (three orientations in an internal space, not in space), the count three chosen; turning the colour space is an SU(3) redundancy on the relations; colour-neutral combinations (a quark and an antiquark, three quarks) are the physical states. The two primitives alone do not supply colour (six routes closed); C9 is the added quality. *Takes:* the colour field's cost and coupling (posited), the quarks' charges (a kind label, not ruled), the proton's mass from beneath.
- **Quarks and gluons.** Shape: quarks as patterns of a second kind in three colour states (C9), gluons as patterns of the colour field on relations. By identification with lattice QCD's continuum limit (candidate; conditional on C9, tuning, the colour field quantised with its posited cost and coupling, and the quark masses as inputs): confinement (numerical, not proved), the hadron spectrum, the nucleon's mass, short of electromagnetic and isospin splittings (the charges wait on the kind label). CO adds nothing of its own to them.
- **Baryons as twists.** Shape; the pions and chiral breaking by identification (candidate). With two light flavours and one handedness on each wall, the quarks' wall-to-wall coupling condenses with a direction U(x) in flavour space (two-flavour QCD's chiral symmetry breaking to isospin, by identification with lattice QCD; numerical, not proved); its ripples are pions and its windings over the three directions of space are baryons (Skyrme), the flavour direction locked to the direction in space, the nucleon and Δ as two rotation states of one twist (spin = isospin), a fermion because the number of colours is odd (Witten). Colour's three is internal; the twist's three is space's. *Takes:* everything the nucleus takes; the walls' handedness extended to interactions; quark mass read as a coupling between the walls (which conflicts with the weak interaction's one-wall reading; at most one survives); and a term that holds the twist's size (a model's, not derived). Replaces the earlier candidate from the staggered walk's label, gone with the flow step. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md).
- **Up and down charges.** Shape: the unit charge difference in every pair is the kind label itself (Q = T₃ + Y), so it comes with the weak interaction. With exact gauge invariance, anomaly cancellation for one generation then forces Q_u = ½ + 1/(2N_c), Q_d = −½ + 1/(2N_c) (+2/3, −1/3 for N_c = 3), a proton charge of exactly +1 for any odd N_c, and N_c odd (Witten's global anomaly). *Takes:* colour, and a chiral gauge field on CO's lattice (best-established route: Ginsparg–Wilson fermions, exponentially local, which the locality ruling allows). See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md).
- **Entanglement.** Candidate: a pair is one pattern with one undivided stock, and the measurement is the cut. Inherited, not supplied: the joint law and the marginal at one place.
