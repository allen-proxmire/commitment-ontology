# Open directions

What is open, each with its status and what it would take. Status words: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), posited (decided, not derived), held (priced, not added), shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak). Every route tried is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/); longer notes are in [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/): how patterns combine; dark matter, and matter over antimatter; the frame with loops.

## Space and gravity

- **What carries the field.** Candidate. The free stock cannot: read in time, ratio hopping is diffusion, D ≤ 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ m²/s, so a body at rest builds its field only out to millimetres–metres in the age of the universe, and a moving body leaves a wake. Free streaming gives 1/r² (excluded by torsion balances to 50 μm); a stock obeying a wave equation would radiate a scalar (excluded). So it is carried by the network's own parts, staggered as exact no-backdrop selects among the placements tested (nearest-neighbour range, run G3): lapse and stretch at places, frame dragging in the relations' one-way part, shear on the squares between relations. G's value follows by static consistency at the body. *Takes:* the nonlinear rule for the relations (a lattice tetrad form), for strong fields. Open: whether a relation's stiffness equals the hop's conductance; whether P∞ = (d/2)f, a place's possibility as its share of its relations' feed (its derivation lost with the new placement), squares with the formation runs' count.
- **The update rule.** Linear, written: each part of the field updated each tick by one stable stencil (three axis steps, with a fourth-order Wilson-type term lifting the field's doubler), signals never faster than c, gauge kept since the walk's total energy and momentum are exactly conserved. Preferred-frame effects of order (ℓ/r)². *Takes:* the nonlinear form.
- **The nonlinear rule.** At linear order the relations' cost is forced to linearised Einstein–Hilbert, isotropic on the cube, by moving places and resetting clocks being free (run G1); the static lapse is GR's at every order given the cost's ψ. *Takes:* the nonlinear cost, by Deser's bootstrap (contested in the literature) with the relations' field energy drawing, or Lovelock as a check.
- **Inside matter and the radiation era.** Candidate: travel draws twice, light twice its energy, field energy as an amount, giving Tolman's (ρ + S)N. *Takes:* the walk on the curved network, with a spin connection, and P = P∞ at a place (the clock inside matter then agrees with the lapse to at most ~10⁻⁴⁹).
- **The now.** Posited for the primitives: a commitment's now is its past light cone. The walk's lattice picks a frame (see Lorentz invariance with loops).

## Matter

- **Lorentz invariance with loops.** The open problem. The walk's finite-degree causal structure picks a frame; with loops, species-dependent speeds of order α/π follow (Collins et al. 2004), against bounds near 10⁻²⁰. Every sector carries a speed that symmetry leaves free, gravity's included (run G1); the common tick sets them equal at tree level, and loops would split them; GW170817 bounds gravity's against light's to ~10⁻¹⁵. *Takes:* a walk whose causal structure picks no frame, or a protecting symmetry. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md).
- **Statistics.** Two patterns of one kind add a direct and an exchanged route with a relative factor. Candidate (P8b): ±1 in three dimensions given a flat exchange factor (Leinaas–Myrheim); the sign is the spinor's full turn carried by the places' frames, (−1)^{2s}, splitting helium-4, rubidium-87 and photons (merge) from helium-3 and electrons (exclude). *Takes:* excluding parastatistics for three or more; the two-pattern dynamics on one stock.
- **Two patterns.** Candidate: carried quantities are branched with what passes; the bound/emitted split. Gives helium, positronium and the 21 cm line by identification. *Takes:* the covariant coupled walk between commitments.
- **Engagement versus route.** Silence: no rule says when a contact through a channel is an engagement rather than a passage. Bounded: a released-energy threshold lies above the eV scale. Candidate shape: a stopping number N counting a chain's breadth in quanta (N ≳ ~50). Since breadth grows by multiplication, the time to reach N grows only as ln N: the signature is size, not time.
- **No spontaneous collapse.** Candidate: a pattern left alone is never resolved; quantum mechanics' own prediction.
- **Doublers.** The remaining copies are predicted charged particles above 10¹⁴ GeV. *Takes:* a cancellation, or an account of them.
- **Number change.** Held: priced, not added. The smallest rule (a route passing through configurations with a pair added) carries the loop exposure above.
- **Loop effects.** Silence: the anomalous moment, the Lamb shift, the running of α, the Casimir force are measured; CO has no account of them yet.
- **Dark matter.** Shape: a pattern that commits but never measures. Permitted, not predicted ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)).
- **The masses.** Silence. **1/137.** Input.

## Nucleus

- **The wall.** Colour needs a complex three-component matching that two primitives do not supply; six routes closed. A third primitive would need at least four posits, and the proton would still be unbuilt.
- **Quarks and gluons.** Shape: quarks as a second kind of commitment, gluons as patterns of a colour field. Missing: the three, the non-commuting matching, confinement.
- **Entanglement.** Candidate: a pair is one pattern with one undivided stock, and the measurement is the cut. Inherited, not supplied: the joint law and the marginal at one place.
