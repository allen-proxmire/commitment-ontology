# Open directions

What is open, each with its status and what it would take. Status words: candidate (proposed, open to reversal), posited (decided, not derived), shape (a form without a rule), silence (the rules do not speak). Every route tried is in [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/); longer notes are in [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/): how patterns combine; dark matter, and matter over antimatter; the frame with loops.

## Space and gravity

- **What carries the field.** Candidate. The free stock cannot: read in time, ratio hopping is diffusion, D ≤ 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ m²/s, so a body at rest builds its field only out to millimetres–metres in the age of the universe, and a moving body leaves a wake. Free streaming gives 1/r² (excluded by torsion balances to 50 μm); a stock obeying a wave equation would radiate a scalar (excluded). So it is carried by the network's own parts, staggered as exact no-backdrop selects among the placements tested (nearest-neighbour range, run G3): lapse and stretch at places, frame dragging in the relations' one-way part, shear on the squares between relations. G's value follows by static consistency at the body. *Takes:* the nonlinear rule for the relations (a lattice tetrad form), for strong fields. Open: whether a relation's stiffness equals the hop's conductance.
- **The update rule.** Linear, written: each part of the field updated each tick by one stable stencil (three axis steps, with a fourth-order Wilson-type term lifting the field's doubler), signals never faster than c, gauge kept since the walk's total energy and momentum are exactly conserved. Preferred-frame effects of order (ℓ/r)². *Takes:* the nonlinear form.
- **The nonlinear rule.** Candidate. With no backdrop at every order (P7e, the author's ruling), the only consistent continuation of the linear cost is Einstein–Hilbert plus Λ (Wald 1986; Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri, Henneaux 2001), given locality and two derivatives at every order and Lorentz-invariant interactions; matter's coupling excludes the free branch. It supplies the self-coupling P6 names (in the static case); that the draw becomes record does not follow and stays the author's ruling; the static lapse is GR's at every order. *Takes:* the two assumptions (the second is the frame problem below); the nonlinear cost on the lattice carriers.
- **Inside matter and the radiation era.** Candidate: travel draws twice, light twice its energy, field energy as an amount, giving Tolman's (ρ + S)N. *Takes:* the walk on the curved network, with a spin connection, and P = P∞ at a place (the clock inside matter then agrees with the lapse to at most ~10⁻⁴⁹).
- **The now.** Posited for the primitives: a commitment's now is its past light cone. The walk's lattice picks a frame (see Lorentz invariance with loops).

## Matter

- **Lorentz invariance with loops.** Conditional on tuning (accepted for now). The walk's finite-degree causal structure picks a frame; with loops, species-dependent speeds of order α/π follow (Collins et al. 2004), against bounds near 10⁻²⁰. Every sector carries a speed that symmetry leaves free, gravity's included (run G1); the common tick sets them equal at tree level, and loops would split them; GW170817 bounds gravity's against light's to ~10⁻¹⁵. Gravity's nonlinear rule rests on the same question: its uniqueness assumes Lorentz-invariant interactions. In the long-wavelength theory the ruling (no backdrop at every order) excludes frame-picking terms, so a frame can come only from the lattice. Held exactly on the lattice, no backdrop would conserve the total energy current and force equal speeds at dimension four; but for interacting matter on a lattice it cannot hold exactly (scattering kinematics: energy, crystal momentum and the energy current over-constrain 2 → 2 events), so it holds only up to order (kℓ)² and loops can split speeds as before. A cancellation between the walk and the links is unsupported: on lattices without a symmetry mixing time and space, gauge and fermion speeds renormalise differently and are tuned separately. Every exit read is closed for CO's network as it stands: a common origin for walk and links (induced fields split at about 1/ln(1/mℓ)), a deformed Lorentz symmetry (exact only for free patterns; additive lattice momentum allows no continuous boosts), and boosts on pairs with backreaction (which would imply equal one-loop speed shifts, which Wilson-type lattices do not have; by analogy, CO's own one-loop run not done). **Tuning, accepted for now (the author's ruling):** the species' speeds are held equal by counter-terms set by hand. The one door left is a network whose coordinates do not commute (κ-Poincaré-type, relative locality), with the soccer-ball cost. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/frame_with_loops.md).
- **Statistics.** Two patterns of one kind add a direct and an exchanged route with a relative factor. Candidate (P8b): ±1 in three dimensions given a flat exchange factor (Leinaas–Myrheim); the sign is the spinor's full turn carried by the places' frames, (−1)^{2s}, splitting helium-4, rubidium-87 and photons (merge) from helium-3 and electrons (exclude). *Takes:* excluding parastatistics for three or more; the two-pattern dynamics on one stock. Excluding parastatistics is the same question as an internal label: parastatistics is ordinary statistics plus an internal compact symmetry (Doplicher–Roberts), and Greenberg's order-3 paraquarks were colour's precursor (colour itself is gauged SU(3)). With no internal label, walk patterns are ordinary fermions (the filled sea of C6 rules out bosons).
- **Two patterns.** Candidate: carried quantities are branched with what passes; the bound/emitted split. Gives helium, positronium and the 21 cm line by identification. *Takes:* the covariant coupled walk between commitments. With the walk a field (C6) and speeds tuned, two patterns are lattice QED's two-body sector, by identification (helium and the 21 cm line given the nucleus and the proton's moment as inputs); the candidate is that CO's branching and bound/emitted rules coincide with it.
- **Engagement versus route.** Candidate (C8, the author's ruling): a contact through a channel is an engagement when its final state is broad against the coupling (|V| < ħΓ/4, no oscillating back) and the chain it opens reaches N quanta; engagements trigger both the Lüders update and gravity's source. N is a constant of CO's own (posited): every route tried is closed (four: ties to CO's other constants, the web's capacity, a cap on the joint amplitude, the final state's continuum), so only experiment sets it (N ≳ ~50 under the readings in the N note); exchanges between patterns inside one body are passages that count toward the chain, so a body of many patterns commits internally once a chain inside it reaches N (its place an independent draw, recording nothing about where the body is). Bounded: a released-energy threshold lies above the eV scale. Since breadth grows by multiplication, the time to reach N grows only as ln N: the signature is size, not time.
- **No spontaneous collapse.** Candidate: a pattern left alone is never resolved; quantum mechanics' own prediction.
- **Doublers.** With the Wilson term, the remaining copies are predicted charged particles above 10¹⁴ GeV. On the staggered walk (a shape, quark sector; Nucleus: baryons as twists), the copies are instead its two kinds, which share one charge. *Takes:* a cancellation, or an account of them.
- **Number change.** Candidate, an identification (C6, C7: the walk read as a field with filled negative states; patterns after the beginning start only in pairs of opposite sign). Needed by laboratory pair creation and the hot early universe's electron–positron bath. Charge and net count are conserved. *Costs, open:* the filled states' vacuum energy (the cosmological-constant problem); loops, which bring the frame problem's α/π speed split.
- **The weak interaction.** Silence. Not in CO's inventory. Needed for neutrinos (the relic background, made in the hot era), for kinds to change (n → p e ν̄), and for the nuclear processes the Sun runs on. *Takes:* a further coupling of the walk, which would have to be chiral (acting on one handedness only).
- **Loop effects.** QED parts by identification, conditional on tuning: with species' speeds tuned, the walk and its U(1) links are identified with lattice QED, and the QED contributions follow from its continuum limit (speeds, mass and charge tuned): the electron's anomalous moment to QED's order, the running of α, the Casimir force. Hadronic and weak parts (the muon's moment, a_e at its measured precision, the proton radius in the Lamb shift) wait on an internal label and the weak interaction. CO adds nothing of its own to them.
- **Dark matter.** Shape: a pattern that commits but never measures. Permitted, not predicted. Given the shape, the rules now say: it gravitates with the same single coupling (linear order), new places make none of it, it is free of the frame problem's loop split, and, given CO's present inventory, it is a fermion and so not ultralight (m ≥ 0.13 keV from dwarf galaxies; fuzzy dark matter excluded). Matter over antimatter: only the first commitments start renewing patterns (C7, the author's ruling, candidate), so the photon ratio is a shape, η ≈ N₀(P_max − P_b)/E₀ × (0.1–1), the first places' room as a share of the beginning's excess, given that the excess ends as light in heat balance and a quantum per tick carries the hop's energy; its value is open ([`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md)).
- **The masses.** Silence. **1/137.** Input.

## Nucleus

- **Colour.** Candidate (C9, the author's ruling): matter in transit may be a split whole, three parts alike in colour (the least odd split: odd forced, least a choice), rotations of their colour labels an SU(3) redundancy on the relations. The two primitives alone do not supply colour (six routes closed); C9 is the added quality. *Takes:* the colour field's cost and coupling (posited), the quarks' charges (a kind label, not ruled), the proton's mass from beneath.
- **Quarks and gluons.** Shape: quarks as the parts of a split whole (C9), gluons as patterns of the colour field on relations. By identification with lattice QCD's continuum limit (candidate; conditional on C9, tuning, the colour field quantised with its posited cost and coupling, and the quark masses as inputs): confinement (numerical, not proved), the hadron spectrum, the nucleon's mass, short of electromagnetic and isospin splittings (the charges wait on the kind label). CO adds nothing of its own to them.
- **Baryons as twists.** Shape: a nucleon as a winding of the flip's direction over the three directions of space (Skyrme), with the Δ as its next rotation state and three as dimension twice over (space and label). Candidate, held: the renewal's flip has a direction in a two-valued label's space, set place by place; its ripples are pions. The two-valued label is the staggered walk's: three one-axis tiles are the 2³ corners of a cell, which hold two Dirac fields (checked term for term), so the doublers become the label rather than heavy particles. In CO's step order the staggered walk is strictly local, has no doublers beyond the two kinds (for θ < π/4), keeps an exact sublattice symmetry that protects the mass, and has no mass-linear speed shift (the bound on ℓ changes form, not size; it depends on light's own dispersion). Excluded, with charge exactly conserved (C6): a charge that differs between the two kinds, since no kind label found is both exact and local at the hop scale (the natural one is twisted over the momentum zone, the kinds swapping going around it along two axes; no other split is proved absent); so the two kinds share one charge, and reading the doublet as up and down, or electron and neutrino, needs a charge source CO does not yet have. *Takes:* a cost for twisting and a reason twists hold their size; a source of the up–down charge difference. See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md).
- **Up and down charges.** Shape: the unit charge difference in every pair is the kind label itself (Q = T₃ + Y), so it comes with the weak interaction. With exact gauge invariance, anomaly cancellation for one generation then forces Q_u = ½ + 1/(2N_c), Q_d = −½ + 1/(2N_c) (+2/3, −1/3 for N_c = 3), a proton charge of exactly +1 for any odd N_c, and N_c odd (Witten's global anomaly). *Takes:* colour, and a chiral gauge field on CO's lattice (best-established route: Ginsparg–Wilson fermions, exponentially local, which the locality ruling allows). See [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/how_patterns_combine.md).
- **Entanglement.** Candidate: a pair is one pattern with one undivided stock, and the measurement is the cut. Inherited, not supplied: the joint law and the marginal at one place.
