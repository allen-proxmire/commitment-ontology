## Space and gravity

- **What carries the field.** Candidate: the free stock cannot carry it (each transport excluded); the occupancies, shape. Read in time, ratio hopping is diffusion, D = δℓ³c/(4πℓ_P²)(ħ/P∞) ≤ 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ m²/s at every allowed δ: a body at rest builds its field only out to millimetres–metres in the age of the universe, and a moving body leaves a wake (Péclet number v r/D ≳ 10¹³ at 1 mm/s and 1 m). Free streaming fast enough to follow the Sun (mean free path ≳ 10⁷ m) gives 1/r² inside that path, excluded by 1/r tested to 50 μm; a wave-equation stock is a radiating scalar, excluded. So the weak-field derivation (`TECHNICAL_APPENDIX.md` §2) holds as the static form only, and G = δc³ℓ²/(4πħ), with δ ≳ 2 × 10⁻⁷ from it, loses its mechanism until the field is carried by the occupancies, whose dynamics at c are GR's (given Lovelock's hypotheses).
- **Second-order field equations.** Candidate at the linearised level: the static law ratio hopping gives (∇² ln P = σδ, no Yukawa terms, from conservation) bounds covariant quadratic-curvature terms to ranges below the hop, so the wave sector is second order, given a local polynomial action. The Schwarzschild exterior still assumes no cubic or non-local terms. Ratio hopping on the network the occupancies make, with the lapse weighting of a conserved flux, is GR's static lapse equation and is consistent with Schwarzschild given the cost's ψ and the sink's flux equal to the mass (candidate; consistency, not a derivation). Neither counting nor scalelessness supplies the second order, and a count of commitments cannot: the vacuum has none, so such a functional has no variation where the exterior and the waves are (it can be the source term, as the sink σ is). What it would take: a cost on relations, linear in the deficit angle and weighted by hinge area (Regge's form), with the reasons for linearity and for the weight 1/G not yet in the rules.
- **The radiation era.** Candidate: light draws twice its energy (travel rule). Owed with inside matter: the walk on the curved network.
- **Inside matter.** Candidate, by two rulings: a closed whole (rest mass measured as a whole) draws its turning once and its travel twice, light draws twice its energy, and the which-way field's energy draws as an amount. Travel runs at the local clock rate, so the source is Tolman's, (ρ + S)N: GR's for gas stars, white dwarfs and planets. Owed: P = P∞ at a place (the clock inside matter then agrees with the lapse to at most ~10⁻⁴⁹, record not counting in P; shared with the exterior), the walk on the curved network, with a spin connection (matter's stress tensor is ruled the walk's and the field's, candidate; it supplies R_ij, beams and the preferred-frame bounds), positivity of the fixed-amount flux. Closure of wholes is an input, which makes the composition tests of active against passive mass empty for nuclei. Nuclear stress is out of reach.
- **The now.** Ruled for the primitives: what has committed, for a commitment, is its past light cone (relativity's own point-relative becoming; time orientation from record-only-grows). On the walk, read as a circuit of local gates, no universal now is needed, but the cone is the lattice's and the walk's finite-degree causal structure picks a frame: "no preferred frame" holds of the primitives, not of the substrate as identified (see Lorentz invariance with loops).

## Matter

- **Statistics.** Compatible with Fermi statistics, not forced: three of Pauli's four inputs are field theory's; the fourth (the branch) is fixed by the ruling that the generator is odd under C. Lattice field theory has no spin–statistics theorem. Must still split He-4 and Rb-87 (merge), He-3 and electrons (exclude), photons (merge). The silence, located: two patterns of one kind live on unordered pairs of places, and P8 adds the direct and the exchanged route to one configuration with a relative sign it does not fix; what fixes that sign is what the rules do not say.
- **The two-pattern problem.** Candidate ruling: carried quantities are branched with the configuration of what passes; gives the two-body Coulomb coupling by identification (helium −79.0 eV; the one-number, mean-field reading is excluded by it). The bound/emitted split ruled (candidate): what is fixed at the same moment by positions, currents and spins is bound; what has left its sources is light. With it, positronium's levels and magnetic hyperfine part and hydrogen's 21 cm line at tree level, by identification. Open: covariance of the coupled walk between commitments.
- **Engagement versus route.** Silence. No rule says when a commitment engages another pattern's transit rather than free stock. A renewal engaging co-located transit at the renewal rate is excluded (coherent transit and Bragg scattering of neutrons in perfect silicon; Bloch electrons); a small share per renewal is bounded, not excluded. P2 gives no criterion: transit is not free (P6) and does not count toward capacity; a released-energy threshold is bounded above the eV scale (Chapman 1995; Hackermüller 2004). What remains is a rate law with amplification, a threshold law, or an emergent record; the third removes the primitive and with it the Born identification and the Bell account. The record is two things: the count (carried as phase) and the trace in the free stock, which is classical (ruled: free possibility has no amplitude); δ is gravity's only and a pattern left alone is not resolved (candidate). Entered as C8 (candidate ruling): a commitment may engage possibility in transit (C1 alone names free stock as the only food); only through a channel, a final state of the engager, in renewal rate or transit, matching the quantum's frequency and direction within its width (scattering channels definable now; absorption channels once bound states are in the table, at present by identification, candidate); at Born's chance; and which contacts through a channel are engagements (commitments) rather than routes (coherent) is the posit still owed, in one of the three classes above. "Committed pattern" is dropped: every massive pattern is committed at each renewal. The posit's shape is a stopping number N: an engagement is a commitment when the chain of passages it sets off reaches breadth N, counted in quanta. N is the one place CO could make a prediction of its own: reversal would fail beyond it, at a breadth that does not move when the noise or the coupling is changed, where quantum mechanics allows reversal at every size. Adopted, it would be CO's first departure from quantum mechanics (the threshold family of reduction models); kept as a shape until a test exists. Double-quantum NMR echoes do not bound it (the drive supplies the quanta); a steering-only echo on flip-flop dynamics (Sánchez et al. 2020) gives N ≳ ~50 under named readings (mixtures as definite configurations, breadth as support, flip-flops between nuclei as passages). No echo shows a fixed breadth wall. A one-page statement of the signature for experimenters: [`RECORD_OF_METHOD/MATTER_notes/N_signature_note.md`](RECORD_OF_METHOD/MATTER_notes/N_signature_note.md).
- **No spontaneous collapse (candidate).** A pattern left alone is never resolved, so an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time. This is quantum mechanics' prediction; CO shares it against the spontaneous-collapse models and does not differ from quantum mechanics. Rests on κ = 0 where there is no final state (C8; bounded at κ ≲ 10⁻²¹, not excluded) and on the free stock being classical (ruled: no amplitude, by the amplitude rule's scope). Current experiment agrees with quantum mechanics and with the collapse models inside their surviving window; large-mass interferometry narrows the window and does not test CO against quantum mechanics. The trace is a dent of δħ at the Born-chosen place of each renewal, an independent draw from |a|² (posit), so it carries no which-way information, and h is fed by a classical stochastic sink whose mean is the mean-field (Schrödinger–Newton) source. A quantum free stock is excluded on both sides (set aside by the ruling, no amplitude, and excluded: by the dent and capacity alone, and with clock coherence by about 10¹⁵). Open: no-signalling of the mean-field back-action (Gisin 1990; Bahrami et al. 2014), tied to the entanglement reading; conservation of the mean source when a body is resolved (Page–Geilker 1981, second objection); the decoherence–diffusion trade-off for classical fields coupled to quantum matter (Oppenheim et al. 2023), which CO escapes only by nonlinearity; transit's draw on the sink (P6) ruled classical: light sources h by its mean, amounts (occupancy included) classical and the ratio branched (candidate); the stress tensor is the walk's and the field's (candidate), its construction on the curved network owed; light's coefficient ruled (the author's, candidate): twice its energy, by the travel rule, one G, and what it draws becomes record as a continuous trace (candidate); the mean source jumps at each absorption (Page–Geilker II). "Left alone" means no engagement of transit; which real contacts (thermal emission, gas collisions) are engagements is the silence above, so the visibility loss in warm-molecule interferometry (Hackermüller 2004) is borrowed from decoherence, not given by the rules.
- **Doublers.** Reduced to the Wilson form; the remaining copies are predicted charged particles at the hop scale, above 10¹⁴ GeV. The binding bound on the hop is the electron's speed, ℓ ≲ 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ m. What it would take: a cancellation, or an account of the copies.
- **Lorentz invariance with loops.** The walk's finite-degree causal structure picks a frame (the global tick is only one slicing of a circuit of local gates; the primitives pick none); with loops, species-dependent speeds of order α/π follow (Collins et al. 2004), against bounds near 10⁻²⁰. What it would take: a walk whose causal structure picks no frame (removing the global tick removes the universal now, not the frame), or a protecting symmetry. Light moves at the walk's c (candidate ruling); whether light carries a time step is open. The dilemma, stated: the network's degree is bounded by quanta per place (R5), and any uniform degree bound picks a frame (Bombelli–Henson–Sorkin 2009). Relations to all past-cone places (causal-set style) would avoid it but contradicts CO's births, degree, distance and locality rulings; discreteness of values rather than places (loop quantum gravity) would avoid it but needs occupancy quantum, against the classical-amounts ruling and CO's falsifier. Nearest relative: energetic causal sets (Cortês–Smolin 2014), which sit on the frame side. Priced: flow to Lorentz invariance at low energy is too slow for QED; keeping the frame out of matter's sector is not available, since matter walks on the substrate; a fermion–boson protecting symmetry (supersymmetry) would work but needs partners CO does not have; tuning the bare speeds to about 1 part in 10¹⁷ is permitted. CO stands as any real-time lattice field theory does: permitted with a tuning. A real-time discrete structure with a symmetry that ties species' speeds together would be the CO-shaped exit; none is known.
- **Routes through added patterns (the sea).** Silence: no rule adds a pattern on a route (the pion's force range is read through one). The posit that would fill it (a route may pass through configurations with a pair added, totals conserved) is a shape: it gives the configuration space, not the vertex, the photon sector or the exchange sign. On the Wilson walk it would not tame the lattice self-energy (chiral symmetry broken), and it carries the O(α/π) loop exposure. Priced with the capped light field, it is the smallest rule for number change: the vertex then comes with it below the cap, and on its adopted inputs and outside the doubler sector, Pauli's argument selects the exchange sign −1; it would give positronium's annihilation part and lifetimes. Held, because it makes loops live and so brings the frame's O(α/π) exposure forward.
- **A story for loop effects.** Silence. The effects loops predict are measured (the electron's magnetic moment to about 1 part in 10¹³ (its anomalous part to about 10⁻¹⁰), the Lamb shift, the running of α, the Casimir force, which can also be computed without vacuum fluctuations, as a van der Waals force between the plates' charges, Jaffe 2005); the picture of virtual particles is one reading of them, not itself observed. CO has no account of these effects yet; the frame problem was priced assuming they arise as standard loops on the lattice, so a different account would have to be priced anew.
- **Dark matter.** Shape: a pattern that commits but never measures (no sign, no colour, and unlike a neutrino not even weakly interacting; cold; engaging only free stock). Permitted, not predicted; CO's gravity is GR's, so the invisible mass is needed for GR's reasons. Note: [`OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md`](OPEN_DIRECTIONS_notes/dark_matter.md).
- **The masses.** Silence. **1/137.** Input. **g = 2.** Conditional on the coupling.

## Nucleus

- **The wall.** Stated as a result in `NUCLEUS.md`: the inventory's one non-abelian matching is space's frame; colour needs a complex three-component spatial scalar with a unitary matching, which two primitives do not supply. Six routes closed. The slot for a third primitive is located (a factor of what some transit carries); filling it at the electromagnetic sector's standing needs at least four posits (which transit; a U(3) matching on relations with its cost; unit determinant; the scale), and the proton is still unbuilt after them. The count three is measured (e⁺e⁻ ratio; π⁰ → γγ) and is the falsifier of any future route.
- **Quarks and gluons.** Shape: quarks commit (a second kind of commitment); gluons as patterns of the colour field on relations; colour as bookkeeping. Missing: the three, the non-commuting matching and gluon self-coupling, confinement. Open: whether exchanges inside one bound pattern are passages or not (measurement sector).
- **Entanglement.** Candidate: a commitment resolves exactly what it engages; a pair is one pattern with one undivided stock, and the measurement is the cut. Cost: two point commitments with a joint law inherited from standard quantum mechanics; selected over a foliation by the exhaustive reading of the now ruling (CO's posit; the substrate as identified has a frame, so a frame-fixed foliation is available and setting it aside is a choice, not a consequence). Inherited, not supplied: the joint law, the commutation of spacelike engagements, the marginal at one place; covariance in the continuum limit only. Open: the coupled two-pattern walk (Matter: the two-pattern problem); the interacting chain is that gap, not a further one: at the cut the engagement is the commitment and nothing is shared afterwards.
