- **The two-pattern problem.** Candidate ruling: carried quantities are branched with the configuration of what passes; gives the two-body Coulomb coupling by identification (helium −79.0 eV; the one-number, mean-field reading is excluded by it). Open: light as the transverse which-way field; covariance of the coupled walk between commitments.
- **Engagement versus route.** Silence. No rule says when a commitment engages another pattern's transit rather than free stock. - **No spontaneous collapse (candidate).** A pattern left alone is never resolved, so an isolated superposition does not decay with mass or time.
